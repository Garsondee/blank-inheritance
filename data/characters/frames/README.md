None of the six kids' `framePilotedRef` fields point at anything now (nulled out the same session, since the Frames they named no longer exist).

**Before drafting any Frame again, read this: the model changed on 2026-09-20.** Frames are **lockstep with their pilot** — one shared level, starting at level 1, not a separate level-12 character. A Frame is a **PC-grade character sheet that amplifies the pilot's own sheet** rather than replacing it, contributing hardness and high AC (GitHub #196). SF2E's vehicle rules were rejected; vehicles work the same way and sit alongside Frames as an equal track. `frame.schema.json` still carries level-12-era assumptions and has not been reworked. Full text: `open-questions.json#frame-scale-starting-level` and `#vehicle-and-frame-rules-model`.

None of the six real Frames exist yet. Class and reflavoring remain collaborative creative decisions between the GM and players, not something to pre-generate to fill out the roster. Read the `frame-class-assignment` and `frame-scale-starting-level` entries in [`../../campaign/open-questions.json`](../../campaign/open-questions.json) before drafting these again. The design bible's own Characters and Core Mechanic sections describe the retired model (level-12 Frames, Resonance and Bleed); don't draft from them.
