# Blank Inheritance
### 空白相続 — Kuuhaku Souzoku

A Starfinder Second Edition campaign for six players and one GM. Each player's character starts at level 1 and takes one of three equal tracks: student, Frame pilot (a combat mech) or vehicle driver. Frames and vehicles are PC-grade character sheets that share their pilot's level and amplify the pilot's own sheet rather than replacing it. The tone is *Giant Robo by way of Water Margin*: operatic, sincere and warm.

## Status

Episodes One and Two (Chapter 1) are authored under `data/`, and the Foundry module that runs them (`module/blank-inheritance/`) is built and under active development. Resonance, Bleed, the ground roles, Anchors and Suppression were cut on 2026-09-20; the Time Economy and Contact Pool are drafted and provisional. The six kid sheets in `data/characters/kids/` are test-only fixtures, to be removed before Session 0/1 (GitHub #202); the real six are made by the players at induction.
