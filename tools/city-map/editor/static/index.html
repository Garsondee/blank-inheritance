Scratchpad -- draw an asset by hand

Click to add points, double-click (or the Finish button, or Enter) to complete a shape, Escape to cancel. A last point placed near the start snaps closed automatically. Frontage: click once to set where/which way the access road connects (required before saving). Fill: pick a material below, then click any closed area shape (including through mirror/radial echoes) to colour it -- affects the real map, not just this preview.

    The background is a real render of the current map, not a live layer -- draw directly on it. Draw a polygon over anything to remove. Draw polygons for new construction sites (land or sea). Draw a line (double-click or Enter to finish) for a road sketch -- one continuous multi-point line works, or several shorter ones if that's easier; touching ends get auto-joined back into one road.
