## Article

Liam - So I kind of feel like Rupiah should have a nemesis. I'm imagining a sort of exaggerated, camp, chaotic neutral version of him. I imagine that the two of them were friends growing up, maybe they respected each other as performing artists, and maybe this other guy even developed feelings for Rupiah. But in the end, they had a falling out. I'm imagining this guy has very little restraint, so maybe he crossed a line and Rupiah decided he wanted nothing to do with him any more, and they've been catty rivals ever since.
I'm thinking that maybe now he has volunteered to go on a mission to find Rupiah. It could be an excuse for him to leave his home and become an adventurer; I think he would follow Rup's plan and come up with a fake identity of his own while he travels. Maybe we could start dropping clues that there is a search party out for Rupiah. (Possibly an opportunity to use Theater Lore?)
The search party themselves I'm imagining as a sort of parody of an adventure party, disguised as a group of travelling performers, who we will have to fight at some point. Something like the nemesis (Swashbuckler), a fire breather (Sorcerer), a mime (rogue), a musician (bard), and, uh, maybe a mitflit for good measure. These guys are just hired to help find Rupiah, so if things get a bit too close to lethal, they would probably run away or surrender, rather than be killed.
You could turn this into a recurring character if you want. Maybe the hunters capture him, believing that he is Rupiah in disguise. Perhaps he is forced to perform for the Lords on the hill, and we have the option to free him and he would agree to work with us against the greater evil.

Session 24:
- The Gauntlight Keep settles down somewhat after the takeover.
- Mitflits take charge with a spirited first meeting and once the rules of meetings have been explained the Mitflits take to it with alacrity! Once the Mitflits learn that they can 'vote' using ayes and nays the event becomes bedlam but the shape of the society starts to develop as a result.
- A man turns up at the keep saying he's got a delivery to make. When brought inside it is apparent that he has the magically tanned hide of a horse rolled up within his wagon. He makes the exchange with Hyuck. The coat of fur is of exceptional quality. Crafting roll for finishing. The end result is "The Barding of Vengeance" - a hide armour set that has no movement penalty, decent AC and bludging damage resistance +3.
- Has the group got any urgent business before we take a bit of a time jump?
- A message arrives for the group - a written letter in a very elaborate and delicate hand. It is from Inquisitor Sophia Reitz. It explains that they ended up being propelled into a different astral plane due to the malfunctioning device in conjunction with a magical storm front which has been sweeping across the inner sea region invisibly for the last few weeks. After a series of difficult encounters, they were finally able to return from that plane to Golarion, ending up near Absolom as intended but still missing Esther. She receives a magical note privately but is open in sharing it's content with the group. Essentially she can either make her own way to Absolom or she can wait until magical teleportation is reliable and safe again, whereupon she would get a note from the Inquisition and they would provide her with teleportation. She has decided for now to remain with the Mitflits as she has found them a fascinating culture with the potential for finding converts to the faith of Abadar. The note from the inquisitor Reitz also notes that they have performed all available healing options on the nuns the group rescued but none of them have been able to recover their memories fully. The inquisition has made the required legal steps concerning the illegal hunting camp and the mage Regina Key who was found with them. Regina is currently imprisoned and awaiting questioning. The inquisition would appreciate any further information the group can find regarding the murder, including the possibility of bringing in the person who Esther described.
- The council meets once again to address how to deal with Otari. After a week the tension between the keep and town has only slightly lowered, some of the people of Otari seem determined to stick to their semi-siege of the keep. More Mitflits arrive every day and the lack of space is starting to become an issue.
- The council decides that a new home must be found for the Mitflits before they either summon the wrath of whatever lies below Gauntlight or they end up in open conflict with Otari. When Gauntlight started to grow in population messages were sent out to all the major Mitflit settlements nearby but no response was received from Fort Ranhold, Fort Croglang and Stonehill Keep. All three have lied in ruin for decades at the very least, all three have defendable surface structures as well as underground areas. The group is to assess these forts, starting with Fort Ranhold, hoping to find somewhere suitable for the Mitflits to settle. The group is also charged with discovering what happened to the Mitflits who were supposed to be there if possible.
