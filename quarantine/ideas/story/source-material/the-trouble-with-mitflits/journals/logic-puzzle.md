## The Puzzle of the Enchanted Council:

In the mystical heart of an ancient forest, a massive, sentient tree known as the Archive of Ages presents a riddle to test the wit and wisdom of adventurers. The tree speaks of six unique creatures who have gathered in the enchanted council under its boughs. Each creature is distinct in species, carries a different magical artifact, and has a favorite mystical fruit.

Creatures: Dragon, Unicorn, Griffin, Phoenix, Centaur, Elf
Artifacts: Amulet of Shadows, Crown of Stars, Wand of Echoes, Cloak of Winds, Orb of Tides, Scepter of Mists
Fruits: Moonberry, Sunapple, Dreamgrape, Starplum, Eclipsepear, Glowfig

The tree provides these clues:
- The Dragon, who loves Moonberries, is seated next to the one who holds the Crown of Stars.
- The Unicorn, carrying the Wand of Echoes, is seated opposite the Griffin.
- The creature who savors Eclipsepears is seated to the left of the one who holds the Amulet of Shadows but is not the Phoenix.
- The Centaur, who enjoys Starplums, is seated next to the creature who holds the Orb of Tides.
- The one who carries the Cloak of Winds is seated opposite the creature who loves Dreamgrapes.
- The Elf, holding the Scepter of Mists, is seated directly opposite the Dragon

The tree asks: "Can you discern the seating arrangement, each creature's artifact, and their favorite fruit? They sit in a circle around a table upon which is placed their feast."

To solve the revised puzzle with six creatures, we'll follow a similar logical deduction process as before. We'll use a grid for organization, and systematically apply the clues to determine each creature's seating position, their artifact, and their favorite fruit.

Let's break down the clues and deduce:
- Dragon with Moonberries next to Crown of Stars: The Dragon loves Moonberries and is seated next to the creature with the Crown of Stars.
- Unicorn with Wand of Echoes opposite Griffin: The Unicorn, carrying the Wand of Echoes, is directly opposite the Griffin.
- Eclipsepear left of Amulet of Shadows; not Phoenix: The creature who likes Eclipsepears is seated to the left of the one with the Amulet of Shadows, and this creature is not the Phoenix.
- Centaur with Starplums next to Orb of Tides: The Centaur, who enjoys Starplums, is seated next to the creature holding the Orb of Tides.
- Cloak of Winds opposite Dreamgrapes: The creature with the Cloak of Winds is directly opposite the one who loves Dreamgrapes.
- Elf with Scepter of Mists opposite Dragon: The Elf, holding the Scepter of Mists, is seated directly opposite the Dragon.

- The Unicorn with the Wand of Echoes must be opposite the Griffin.
- The creature loving Eclipsepears (and not being the Phoenix) is to the left of the one with the Amulet of Shadows. This helps place the Griffin and the creature with the Amulet of Shadows.
- The Centaur, enjoying Starplums, is next to the creature with the Orb of Tides.
- The Phoenix, with the Cloak of Winds, is opposite the creature loving Dreamgrapes.
