## Nirvana (1d10)

- **2:** 2. **Title:** Trial of Purity      **Description:** A serene pool in Nirvana tests the purity of the adventurers' intentions. They must each share a truth about themselves.      **Skills:** Deception (to hide truths), Insight (to reveal profound truths)      **Reward for Success:** The pool blesses each truthful adventurer with a pool of 5 temporary hit points that last until depleted or for 24 hours.      **Bane for Failure:** Any deception causes the pool to drain their vitality, giving the liar a –2 penalty to Fortitude saves until the next long rest.
- **3:** 3. **Title:** Meditation Maze      **Description:** The adventurers come across a labyrinth that they must navigate, but moving through it physically proves impossible. Instead, they must meditate to find the spiritual path.      **Skills:** Occultism, Perception      **Reward for Success:** Gaining insight into Nirvana's nature, each adventurer gains a +2 circumstance bonus to saves against illusions for the next 24 hours.      **Bane for Failure:** Mental fatigue from the failed meditation grants a –1 penalty to Will saves until the next rest.
- **4:** 4. **Title:** The Libram of Lost Souls      **Description:** A tome containing knowledge of souls seeking redemption appears. Understanding its text requires great insight.      **Skills:** Arcana, Society      **Reward for Success:** Party members can each choose one skill in which they gain a +1 circumstance bonus for their next skill check.      **Bane for Failure:** The confusion from the text causes each adventurer to take a –1 penalty to the next Intelligence or Wisdom-based skill check.
- **5:** 5. **Title:** Ethereal Intruder      **Description:** A malicious spirit from the Ethereal Plane has slipped into Nirvana and begins sowing discord.      **Skills:** Stealth, Thievery, Survival      **Reward for Success:** Subduing the intruder yields a spiritual essence the group can use to get a +2 circumstance bonus on saving throws against ethereal or incorporeal beings for 24 hours.      **Bane for Failure:** The intruder steals a portion of each adventurer’s essence, giving them one level of drained condition until they can rest and recover.
- **6:** 6. **Title:** Choirs of Purity      **Description:** A group of angelic beings invites the adventurers to partake in a chorus that aligns mortal souls with Nirvana. Harmonizing requires skill and focus.       **Skills:** Performance, Concentration      **Reward for Success:** The harmonic resonance fortifies the party, allowing each member to re-roll one failed save within the next 24 hours.      **Bane for Failure:** Dissonance grates on the adventurers' nerves, giving each of them a –1 status penalty to Concentration checks until their next rest.
- **7:** 7. **Title:** Contest of the Enlightened      **Description:** A group of monks challenges the party to perform feats of agility and concentration, blending martial prowess with spiritual clarity.      **Skills:** Acrobatics, Athletics      **Reward for Success:** A monk's sash that grants the wearer a +2 circumstance bonus to Acrobatics or Athletics checks (chosen when the sash is first donned) for the next 24 hours.      **Bane for Failure:** A sprain or a pulled muscle from the attempt imposes a –5-foot status penalty to the adventurer's speed until the end of their next long rest.
- **8:** 8. **Title:** Puzzle of the Planar Glyphs      **Description:** The party finds a series of interlocking glyphs that radiate powerful energy. By solving the puzzle, they can harness this energy.      **Skills:** Crafting, Arcana      **Reward for Success:** Solving the puzzle strengthens the adventurers' bond with their gear, providing +1 potency rune to a weapon or armor of their choice for the next 24 hours.      **Bane for Failure:** Incorrectly aligned glyphs emit a disorienting flash, giving a –1 penalty to the party's AC for the next hour.
- **9:** 9. **Title:** Greeting of the Golden Serpent      **Description:** A wise golden serpent appears and offers to share ancient wisdom through riddles.      **Skills:** Nature, Society      **Reward for Success:** Each party member gains a ‘Serpent's Insight’ allowing them to ask the GM one yes-or-no question about the adventure, to be answered truthfully.      **Bane for Failure:** Failing to grasp the serpent's wisdom, each member takes a –1 penalty to Nature and Society checks for 24 hours due to uncertainty.
- **10:** 10. **Title:** Light of the Five Suns       **Description:** A rare alignment of five suns casts prismatic light that must be channeled using specialized knowledge.       **Skills:** Nature, Occultism       **Reward for Success:** The adventurers gain a radiant aura that provides 5 resistance against darkness and negative energy for the next 24 hours.       **Bane for Failure:** Misaligned prismatic light dazzles the adventurers, giving them a –2 penalty to Perception checks until they can rest their eyes for at least an hour.
