### appearance

Seriously? Don't you know all Gnolls look the same? Big fellow, black and tan fur, lots of teeth and has trouble standing up straight. Heh.

But, if you spend enough time with him, which I cannot recommend, you might notice his fur doesn't grow property around his right shoulder. You can guess it's an old wound, but it doesn't seem to stop him dragging around the biggest lump-of-iron-on-a-chain you ever saw, attached to a worn, dark thigh bone of some unfortunate creature. And around his neck is strung five tiny, white skulls that he likes to fidget with.

And he's always muttering under his breath, although you can't make out the words, and leaves a space beside him when he sits, as if for an imaginary companion. He pauses before entering rooms, rather unnervingly for such a big fellow, and cautiously sniffs the air as if, well, it sounds silly to say it, but as if he is afraid of something.

### backstory

Hyuck Bonebox is superstitious, even for a Gnoll, and claims to be guided by ancestral spirits, especially his grandmother. It is impossible to avoid overhearing his muttering to unseen presences. Whomever these 'spirits' might be, it is plain that their advice leaves much to be desired.

However, Hyuck is a sterling fellow to have around, fearless in battle, the first to charge in and causes dismay and mayhem with savage swings of his war flail. Allies find themselves unexpected free to act as the enemy tries to avoid an untimely meeting with spiked iron. But a fellow might need to overlook his Gnollish lack of table manners and tendency to steal body parts from the fallen. Ewwww.

Speaking of which, Hyuck's left boot conceals a long, serrated knife that is used for butchering meat. In a pinch it could serve as a deadly weapon - although he is more likely to rely on good old-fashioned teeth and his steel tipped claws, as they take no time to draw.

Hyuck is the life and soul of any party. His favourite party trick is to punch a hole clean through a pig! Living or dead! "There's a knack to it," he modestly says. That always goes down a treat at Gnolly Christmas.

In Otari, a large trunk is kept in his lodgings; a trunk with skull and crossbones scratched onto it. Within are his most important valuables - ancestral bones. And for important decisions he returns to consult the long, sightless skull of his elder brother, who always has "valuable" advice. Although this trunk includes many so-called relatives and trophies, it does not include any mementoes of his father.

But there are depths to Hyuck that aren't immediately obvious. Not great depths to be sure - but he is more than a one-punch wonder. His constant quest is to find his father, although what his intentions are are best described as unclear. Hyuck is convinced that he can follow his father's trail by scent alone. And it is a wonder to find how many strange places he finds his father has gone. How does this mysterious father figure always stay one step ahead? Yet, thanks to some brotherly advice, Hyuck never loses the trail...

### Straight Through the Middle!!! *(action)*

Once a day, you may declare that you are going to go right through the middle. THEN you attack at a cost of 3 actions, rolling to-hit twice and take the better score, with a damage bonus of 2 * Level. If you kill the target then you get a spectacular result and all hostile witnesses are intimidated. If you fail to kill the target you take 2 * Level damage yourself and are off-guard to the target. If you critically fail to hit the target you are additionally intimidated by the target and must roll 1d6 to determine if your weapon is damaged. On a 1-2 roll, your weapon is spectacularly shattered and you are disarmed. If you were unarmed, you temporarily lose the use of your dominant hand for 1 minute.
