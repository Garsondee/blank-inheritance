# The Trouble with Mitflits: campaign timeline

> **SOURCE MATERIAL — from the original campaign, not Blank Inheritance canon.** This is a reconstruction of how
> the original campaign actually unfolded at the table. Nothing here is canon on its own: where it disagrees with
> `ideas/story/bible/LEDGER.md` or the dossiers, **those win**. See `../README.md`.

*Compiled 2026-09-24. Every entry says what it rests on. Most of the timeline after April 2023 is
**inferred** from Foundry's own timestamps, because the GM's session log stops there.*

---

## 1. How this was pieced together

No single record covers the whole campaign. There are seven sources, and they overlap:

| Source | What it gives | How far to trust it |
|---|---|---|
| **Word of the Session** (`journals/word-of-the-session.md`) | One dated line per session, #1 (14 Jun 2022) to #31 (12 Apr 2023) | **Recorded.** The GM's own log. It stops after April 2023. |
| **Session Planning** journal | GM plans for three sessions the GM numbered 22–24 | **Recorded.** It shows intent; what actually happened can differ. |
| **Map-exploration records** (Foundry "fog") | The date each player **last** explored each map | Good for placing a map in play. It shows the *last* visit, not the first. |
| **Items on the character sheets** | When each feat, item or piece of loot was put on a sheet | Very good. Level-ups and loot land on the night they happened. |
| **Creation dates** of maps, NPCs, journals, tables | When the GM built something | Usually a day or two before it was played. Maps and monsters were often built early. |
| **Text written on the maps** (`scenes.md`) | GM notes, read-aloud text, puzzle answers | **Recorded**, but undated. |
| **Chat log** | 67 messages, 14 and 30 May 2024 only | **Recorded** for those two nights. |

Other things established along the way:
- **The table:** Garsondee (Ingram, GM), Pippa (Anabala), Thorn (Hyuck, plus the familiar Dave), Craig (Shucks),
  and Liam (Rupiah).
- **They were "the Tuesday group".** The GM's note on the Fogfen Marsh map says so. Of the 29 dated log lines, 14 fall on a Tuesday and most of the rest on the Monday or Wednesday beside it, so the log was probably sometimes written up a day off.
- **Steve is named in the log once:** "Steve didn't expect the Mitflit" (17/08/22).
- **The world** was created on 10 June 2022 and last opened on 24 July 2024. Foundry logged about **223 hours** with the world open.

**Legend:** **R** = recorded (written down at the time) · **I** = inferred from timestamps.

## 4. The unlogged year (April 2023 – May 2024), reconstructed

Nothing below was written down as a session log. The dates are when the players explored a map (**fog**), when
something landed on a sheet (**sheet**), or when the GM built something (**built**).

### Arc 4: Volkheed and Baldwin Manor (April – August 2023)

- **Written in advance: the plan.** On the bought "Fort" map (1 Mar 2023) the GM wrote: *"A castle which the group
  approaches - they are happy to hand over the Mitflit artefacts but require a little time and invite the group in.
  A siege begins. The group discovers that the people inside aren't innocent. Decision time! Either way the castle is
  occupied either by invaders or the people already there. The dark secret has to do with ritual sacrifice of
  prisoners. Both sides are fantatics."*
- **Late April:** the church fight concludes (fog 18 and 26 Apr). **Sister Syntax**, an android cleric, is
  built on 24 Apr and placed on the church map, so she was probably a guest character that night.
- **16 May:** built: the Baldwins (Agatha Laine, Reginald Cavil, Castria Anne), the named mitflits (Jizlirt, Riegs,
  Comveelk, Friz), and Volkheed's people (Bilda, Custert Donjam, Dola Ruwan, Ella). Also the **Laughing Hound** inn map.
  A note on the Volkheed map: *"Bilda Maid - Illness - Arrived safely at the Laughing Hound."*
- **24 May – 12 Jun:** the party at the Laughing Hound (fog 24 May and 12 Jun). A riding horse is built. There is shopping.
  A note on the inn map: *"Letter sent to Sofia Reitz explaining situation - **19th Sarenith, 18:00**."* This is
  the only in-world clock time in the whole record.
- **7 Jun:** built: **Alastair Baldwin**, Grot and Losa, and the **Croglang Mines** map.
- **19–20 Jun:** built: **Dread Wraith and Wraith** in a new folder, "Antagonists / Shadow". The same week the GM writes
  **Inicrium, Shadow Plane Intrusion, History of Baldwin Manor, Financial Document, Manifest and the Letter**. These are
  the documents later pinned to the manor map.
- **21 Jun:** everyone explores **Volkheed**. Hyuck gets the Fulminating Spear.
- **28 Jun:** built: **William Charles Baldwin**, the six Inquisitors, Fog Thralls, and a Glass Golem. The loot for the
  Croglang fight is placed.
- **16–18 Jul:** Shucks takes **Ghost Dedication** (16 Jul). On **18 Jul** all four players explore **Croglang Fort
  Exterior**, **"Hel"**, and **Abadar's Plane of Order** in a single night.
  - The "Hel" map is used as the **Shadow Plane**. Its read-aloud text begins *"As the beam of ethereal energy
    swathes the party, light soon succumbs to the oppressive darkness of an unthinkable abyss"*, and it calls the
    place *"The shadow plane"*.
  - Abadar himself is built on 17 Jul and placed on the Plane of Order.
  - Loot that night: Condensed Mana for Shucks, Rupiah and Hyuck; the Frightened condition on the mines map.
- **24 Jul:** Hyuck gains **Weight of Guilt** and **Glimpse of Redemption** (redemption-flavoured reactions). This
  follows the Abadar meeting, and he already bears Abadar's Minor Curse. **Ulthak**, a gnoll whose portrait file is
  named `dad.webp`, is built the same day.
- **Late July:** a card game (card stacks for every player, 27 Jul). **The Jewel of Abadar** map (1 Aug).
  Anabala and Hyuck both reach **level 7** (2 Aug).
- **21 Aug:** everyone explores **Croglang Mines**. The Temple Entrance map is built.
- **28 Aug:** everyone explores **Croglang Fort Interior**, the fight inside Baldwin Manor. Everyone gets *Mr Snuggly
  Wuffles's business card*. What's written on the map:
  - Pinned documents: History of Baldwin Manor, Letter, Financial Document, Manifest, Inicrium, Shadow Plane Intrusion.
  - *"Iniqruium - Solid Magic Potential"*
  - A Baldwin's accusation: *"These criminals broke into my ancestral home. They slaughtered my family or had some part
    in their demise. They have taken things from this house that don't belong to them. That's my fucking horse's
    leather on that Gnoll."*
  - GM notes: *"Hyuck got rid of the jacket."* and *"Shucks and Hyuck to be questioned."*
  - A Croglang Fort Exterior note: *"RISING FOG ON OTHER MAPS"*.

> **Ingram, 2026-09-24 (LEDGER #29):** "The Inicrium battery is an event which caused the Baldwin Manor to become

> That fits this arc: the shadow folder, the Inicrium documents, the "rising fog", the Shadow Plane crossing and

## 5. The in-world calendar

All of it happens in the Pathfinder year **4721 AR**, the published adventure's "current year".

- **Sarenith** is the sixth month (midsummer).
- The Baldwin arc's letter to Reitz is timed **19 Sarenith, 18:00**.
- The weather module's saved date is **18 Sarenith**.
- The clock at the May 2024 session and the world's final date both read **26 Sarenith 4721 AR**. A small-clock widget also stored
  "Moonday, Sarenith 7th", presumably an older position.
- The Baldwin manufactory's profit report is "as of Sarenith" 4721.

So, as far as the clock shows, everything from Volkheed to Nirvana took the characters only about **a week**. There
is no in-world date for anything earlier.
