## Still standing

- **§3, the incident, as the public saw it:** a violet-hued aperture with a black core opened and grew on the island
  where Absalom stands, and as it grew large enough to be noticed, adventurers and magic-users converged from across
  the island, and then the region, to contain it. (How it began, the throw and the failed catch, is GM-only.)
- **§4, the uncertain ending:** the aperture was forced back and crushed into a small space, and either destroyed or
  converted into something else. Nobody present ever confirmed which, and the dossier says the ambiguity is deliberate.
- **§10, first half:** the four founded the Directorate about five years after the incident, so that the response
  would be faster and more decisive next time.
- **§11, the dates** that later decisions haven't moved: about 200 years ago the incident; about 195 years ago the
  Directorate founded; about 150 years ago the move east; about 120 years ago the exotic matter catalogued and shelved.
