book: I · rank: Cadets, Clearance 1 · status: sketch · by: claude · source: D071 · from: cb:ch02.title, cb:ch02.rank, cb:ch02.line, cb:ch02.learn
learn: The official line: cultists summon these things. Nothing that's beaten dies.
The response roster starts calling. Every contact is somewhere the cult has been, the Directorate says the cult is summoning these things, and the evidence agrees.

Chapter 2 is the first chapter run as a board. Each scene's note says why the table comes to it (Arrive), what the players do (Do or Choose), what they learn (Learn), and where it leads (Next). The players choose the order of the leads; the Plot Room's call (the Fish Market) arrives whether they choose it or not.
