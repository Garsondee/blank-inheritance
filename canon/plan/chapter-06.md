# Chapter 6 · The Show
book: II · rank: Duty Section, Clearance 2 · status: sketch · by: claude · source: D071 · from: cb:ch06.title, cb:ch06.rank, cb:ch06.line
learn: The first look: eyes on stalks over the whole city. Perhaps a voice in a museum case.
At an invitation-only magic show the whole hall looks up through treated lenses and sees the sky full of eyes. The mitflits start arriving, and a girl walks away from a lorry.

## Beats

### beat--look-up
Cicada declares itself, in posters and pamphlets across the city: look up.

### beat--kiris-show
Kiri's show, and the card from Chapter 3 gets them in: invitation only, in a rented hall, full. At the door every guest is handed a small lens. At the climax the roof shutters open, Kiri tells them to look up through the lens, and the whole hall sees the sky over Kurose black with eyes on stalks and slow tentacles, always moving. Cicada calls it a god. Nobody explains it.

### beat--the-gathering-arrives
Mitflits from everywhere start arriving in Kurose, led by Anabala: a founder, and a name from their schoolbooks. This is the gathering, coming so that they're all in one place when they leave. They say Kurose feels like being watched over, and they like it. Some of them go missing.
