# Chapter 4 · Irregular
book: I · rank: Duty Section, Clearance 2 · status: sketch · by: claude · source: D071 · from: cb:ch04.title, cb:ch04.rank, cb:ch04.line
learn: It watches a place before it forms there, which isn't how summoning works.
The party learns to predict where the next one will form and gets there first, finds out what their blood screen meant, and is offered Gen 2.

Chapter 4 is the last of Book I. It opens as a board, because the party now has Clearance 2 and can send itself to a contact; it closes on one fixed day, the day the party is at the square before the creatures form.

### beat--the-warehouse-heist
A heist. A demonstration pulls the police and the response roster to one side of town while a crew empties a chemical warehouse on the other. It is where the party first meets both branches of its personnel-scale work at once, a criminal crew with Cadre fighters among them: the first sign that the crime thread and the cult thread meet at Manako's dealings with the crime lords. The police are across town at the demonstration, which is the point of the timing.
