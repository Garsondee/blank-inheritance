# Chapter 3 · Retrofit
book: I · rank: Cadets, Clearance 1 · status: sketch · by: claude · source: D071 · from: cb:ch03.title, cb:ch03.rank, cb:ch03.line
learn: Every Breaker goes for the same weak point. The Cadre knows their section's name.
The party turns their diggers into fighters, meets Cicada's armed Cadre for the first time, and watches Taka make a choice.

Chapter 3 is run as a board again. The Works Yard is the Plot Room's call and comes whatever the players do; the rest are leads they take in any order. This is the chapter where the cult starts answering the party's investigation.

### beat--meet-the-cadre
They meet an armed Cadre squad for the first time when it ambushes them to take evidence back. Its leader, the Soldier, is disciplined, non-lethal, withdraws by the book, and won't fire on anyone young. He calls them "Section Nine". His people won't finish off anyone who's down. The police are not in this street: the ambush is the cult's answer to the party's investigation.
