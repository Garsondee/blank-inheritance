# Ideas: moments Episode Two once held

Episode Two is a ghost for now, and these are what the old design planned for it. None is built.

## idea--speak-for-the-comms-officer
The Deputy Director has the radio log in front of him, which includes the Comms Officer refusing, twice, to order six trainees into a harbour road, and saying so on the net where it was recorded. He raises it himself and asks the party what they make of it. Whatever they say goes into her record and they are never told which way it went. Speaking up is free and unrolled, saying nothing is one of three real answers and leaves her refusals undefended, and agreeing with their own section officer works and costs the party nothing, and is the worst outcome for her. It is the one beat in the scene where something happens because of the party rather than to them. She mustn't become a debt they are owed or a protector they have bought, and the record comes back through her, in her own words, once the party has spent a while listening to her.

## idea--a-doctor-without-an-appointment
Waiting in the corridor, anyone trying to make sense of what Shucks did can work out that the Deputy Director stood up before the door was fully open and the man on the door didn't reach for the log. The access is real and unaccounted for. A critical failure hands out a tidy, plausible and wrong answer, an old friend from training, the Deputy Director's own physician, which the party carries until something contradicts it.
