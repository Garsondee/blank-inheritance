# Ideas: clues, papers and first sightings

## idea--the-reagent-stakeout
Dull reagents go missing in bulk over three months, and a lab stakeout ends at two in the morning in a fight through a window with four professionals who fight to flee, not to the death. It would pay off the stock that walks off the instrument makers' shelves.

## idea--first-gen-2-sighting
How the party first sees a Gen 2 move, against the clank of Gen 1: they see one move before Gen 2 is offered, with the grace an anomaly the later offer answers; the first sighting is itself the offer, a recruitment pitch; or they only see it move once one of them is flying it.

## idea--censored-papers
Every Directorate document the players see is censored to a clearance, and the same document exists at several levels. Obtaining a less-censored version is progress in itself, and where the censor cut tells the reader something. Places could carry the same levels (public, private, restricted).

## idea--glamoured-paper
Some documents give each player their own copy, plausible and complete on its own, with small differences between the copies. Laid side by side, the differences spell a hidden message, and the players find it only by noticing that they are not reading the same thing.

## idea--recruitment-manga
The Directorate publishes a short black-and-white recruitment manga, a cover and at most four pages, following a bubbly young recruit. It tells the approved history and what the job is meant to be, while a careful reader can see the fantasy is no longer true and that the publication itself serves the Directorate.
