# Ideas: hooks the map's places carry

Scene hooks and small unexplained things written into the map's place records while they were drafted. None is scheduled; each waits for a scene that wants it.

## idea--central-summoned-deep
The party is summoned to a level of Central they have never been cleared for, and nobody explains why the meeting is being held that far down. Or one of Central's armoured covers is open when it shouldn't be, with light coming from a part of the building on no plan the party has seen; or a vehicle goes in through the north-west roadway and never comes out.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D076 · from: loc:central-fortification · state: live

## idea--the-rock-visit
The party is brought to The Rock by someone with access and told plainly not to describe it to anyone who wasn't with them; one of the nine doors is open when they arrive, and nobody escorting them will say why; or a name they know turns up on a manifest, a duty roster or a cell door there.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D076 · from: loc:the-rock · state: live

## idea--the-rock-standing
Being trusted with The Rock's existence is a rarer kind of trust than being let deeper into a known building, so it could be tracked apart from Clearance, and losing it would be a heavy consequence, not a routine one.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D076 · from: loc:the-rock, b-loc:the-rock.open-questions · state: live

## idea--nine-and-nine
The Main Mech Hangar has nine bays, three of them empty for no stated reason, and The Rock has nine cells sized for a Frame. The two nines could be one plan, or a coincidence the players will notice anyway.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D076 · from: loc:main-mech-hangar, loc:the-rock · state: live

## idea--vermilion-posting
A character with any spellcasting background gets a posting order to the Vermilion Block: an order, not a request, and the director isn't taking questions. Or a silo delivery breaks down at the gate in full view, with a tighter escort than usual and a stock number that matches nothing on the manifest. Getting caught asking about the silos brings an escort back, a note made and a superior told; a missing or tampered shipment could see a whole unit's Clearance pulled while it's investigated.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D076 · from: loc:vermilion-block · state: live

## idea--the-director-knows-more
Whether the Vermilion Block's director genuinely knows what the reagents are for, or is as much in the dark about the deepest purpose as everyone else.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D076 · from: b-loc:vermilion-block.open-questions · state: live

## idea--deep-storage-is-the-vaults
Deep Storage under the Container Ground could be where the Directorate keeps the alien technology it digs up, with the deepest vault at its bottom floor, or the vaults could be somewhere else entirely and Deep Storage only the Directorate's attic.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D076 · from: loc:deep-storage · state: live

## idea--deep-storage-requisitions
A requisition slip for a part logged as scrapped comes back from Deep Storage stamped RECEIVED, from a floor the party can't reach; the custodian asks the party to quietly account for one floor before an inspection, without saying who is inspecting; or a name stencilled on an old crate matches a name in the party's own case file.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D076 · from: loc:deep-storage · state: live

## idea--deep-storage-custodian-cast
The Deep Storage custodian could become a named, recurring character, the person who decides what counts as in use and keeps a ledger of favours.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D076 · from: b-loc:deep-storage.open-questions · state: live

## idea--the-hospital-ward
A ward the General Hospital's own paperwork says is empty is not, and nobody will say why; or the Chief of Staff asks a small, deniable favour in exchange for bending a rule about who is seen and when. A scene caused there makes the next visit harder for everyone in the same uniform.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D076 · from: loc:general-hospital, b-loc:general-hospital.open-questions · state: live

## idea--the-impossible-result
A placement result comes back from the registrar not failed but impossible, matching no profile there is a form for; or a student two years above the party asks careful, specific questions about how the sorting works, and won't say why.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D076 · from: loc:main-school-complex · state: live

## idea--the-longer-version
A document the party already owns exists in the Registry in a longer version, and the only difference between the two is a typist who has to be persuaded; or a trolley of the day's work goes past unattended. Reading the clearance markings on a stack of papers can show the shape of something without its words.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D076 · from: loc:the-registry · state: live

## idea--the-plot-room-replay
The party watches their own last operation replayed on the Plot Room's table by people who weren't there, and the reconstruction is wrong in a way only they can correct; or a marker is being moved on the word of someone who left the building an hour ago. A plotter, off the record, could say what the table looked like on a night nobody discusses.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D076 · from: loc:the-plot-room · state: live

## idea--the-operator-recognises-them
An exchange operator recognises the party's voices before they give a name, and mentions kindly, once, that somebody else has been asking to be put through to them.

## idea--the-curtained-bay
A bay in the Erecting Shop that should be empty is curtained off, and the crane time booked against it is signed for by someone who doesn't work there. Or the fitters on a Frame the party knows say things to its pilot's face that no report ever would.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D076 · from: loc:the-erecting-shop · state: live

## idea--the-crew-mess-round
A pilot who turns up at the Crew Mess unasked and stands a round tends to find their Frame gets a night shift nobody allocated.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D076 · from: loc:the-crew-mess · state: live

## idea--the-drain-head-opened
A candidate on a punishment detail has to check the Drain Head after a heavy tide and finds it has been moved from the inside; or a newly posted officer who wasn't warned hears it at high tide and someone has to explain. If anything is ever written for down there, keep it a non-sentient animal like the flood tunnel's centipedes.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D076 · from: loc:the-drain-head · state: live

## idea--the-seaward-fence
The island line's platforms are fenced along their whole seaward side, and a posted notice calls it a safety measure. The fence is never explained: anyone who asks gets the notice.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D076 · from: loc:island-platforms · state: live

## idea--unaccounted-buildings
Two buildings nobody can account for: a windowless grey structure in The Flats, two streets from a row of houses whose residents have never found out what it is, and one building among the stores on the River Island, a block of made ground in the river mouth reached by two bridges.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D076 · from: loc:home-the-flats-row, loc:river-island · state: live

## idea--the-redoubt
The Redoubt, a small artificial fort alone off the north-east shore on ground that used to be much larger, has batteries, a signal mast and one causeway, and what it is watching for is not discussed at the gate. Everyone on Redoubt Shore has an opinion about its garrison.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D076 · from: loc:redoubt, loc:redoubt-shore · state: live

## idea--no-witnesses
The Upper Catchment, the bare cold upland where the reservoirs' streams start, is the first place the Directorate looks when it needs somewhere with no witnesses; the Western Ridge is walked by people who would rather not be seen walking.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D076 · from: loc:upper-catchment, loc:western-ridge · state: live

## idea--the-walled-row
A row of walled compounds on the island's better ground, with private gates and guards, and neighbours who know better than to ask what pays for them. The Anchor House, named for the ship's anchor over its gate, is old shipping money behind bodyguards dressed as household staff; the Tsurumi Compound, the largest house on the row, is the only one whose gate has turned away a Directorate courier and made it stick. Both lead naturally into Kurose's organised crime.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D076 · from: loc:the-anchor-house-437, loc:the-tsurumi-compound-423, loc:walled-house-no-1-422 · state: live

## idea--without-paperwork
The Sawtooth Quays, a comb of finger piers of every age in the inner harbour, move small freight, fish and whatever is being moved without paperwork. On the island, the Quarantine Shed takes consignments whose manifest and contents disagree: most is clerical error, not all, and the rest leaves by a different door. The Bonded Store holds material under the Directorate's own seals rather than customs', a difference never explained to anyone who asked.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D076 · from: loc:sawtooth-quays, loc:the-quarantine-shed, loc:the-bonded-store · state: live

## idea--the-pictures-were-chosen
Partly self-inflicted: one or more of the four founders wanted the pictures changed, because it is easier to be a hero with a stranger's face than to be recognised in the street as the one who dropped the catch.
