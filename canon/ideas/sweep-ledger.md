## idea--the-charters-black-bar
A paragraph of the founding charter painted out in public, with an answer behind it about the founders admitting the institution would need to conscript children. Cut: the charter is a useful document and good lore, but publicly censoring part of it is odd, a secret that is flaunted, and it makes the Directorate seem evil instead of just very secretive.
