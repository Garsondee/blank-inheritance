# Ideas: Hyuck's origin and what became of him

A brainstorm between Ingram and Steve, Hyuck's original player. Nothing in it was decided; what was (Hyuck alive, a test pilot, carried from body to body) is filed under Hyuck.

## idea--hyuck-offended-a-god
Hyuck was anointed by a god called Yabadabba, fell foul of it, and kept stumbling into battles where he was hailed a hero until he had offended everyone it is possible to offend. Before the hammer of retribution landed, messing with teleportation or time magic ejected him several hundred miles and several hundred years into the future, still an idiot.

## idea--hyuck-brothers-skull
Hyuck carries his brother's skull, which reincarnates the brother memory by memory and shares Hyuck's body: really a supernatural construct of Hyuck's own idealised brother, not the true spirit. Hyuck may have been body-swapped along the way, and may be carrying a skull that isn't his brother's.

## idea--hyuck-mad-scientist
Someone tells Hyuck he can make his brother big and strong again, so he tries to put his brother's soul into a Frame. It can't work, but it will screw things up: Hyuck in mad scientist mode, led by the nose.

## idea--hyuck-psychic-cockpit
Because Hyuck is used to moving his mind around, a Frame might be less stressful for him than for other pilots, yet the skull, the spirits and his arrangements make his cockpit a psychic mess, with plenty that can go wrong. His absurd beliefs could latch onto the machine: a trapped deity he must free, a soulmate, a reincarnated pet. Only a Gen 2 has a living core, so it would be a Gen 2.

## idea--hyuck-contained-asset
The Directorate keeps Hyuck contained by making sure he has what he needs while getting the most out of him: a lab animal in a bioprison, or a source of genetic material for pilot stamina and reflexes. The pilot shortage that presses the party into service would force it to bring him out in a genuine emergency, where his instability could cause more problems than it solves. The living Hyuck is a test pilot, not a contained asset, so this stays only a brainstorm.
