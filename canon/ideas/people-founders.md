## idea--the-carrying-in-detail
Gnoll clans count carrying a founder the highest honour, so young volunteers come to Kurose and nobody is made to say yes; the Directorate arranges it quietly, part of why it keeps him close: a well-looked-after asset. Between bodies the spirit waits in its own skull, kept by the clan until a new bearer is found, the young gnoll who offers their body. The wrestle is fought inside, over one night: anyone watching sees a young gnoll lying very still, then waking up. A spirit that loses rides, a voice of memory and advice; one that wins wears the body, and the bearer's soul goes quiet, pushed to the back but not gone. Hyuck has been carried five or six times since Kortos (the GM's choice), wearing every body. Everyone he has beaten is still in there: the bearers he pushed back, the elders who tried to wear him when he was young, and probably his elder brother, whose skull he carried in the old campaign, riding with him now.
