# Ideas: the mayor

## idea--mayor-first-appearance
Seen from a distance first, in Book I, where everyone in Kurose can hear the mayor praise the Directorate: on the radio, in the paper, at a ribbon. The party may meet the mayor in person in Book I as the city's cheerful public face, without learning that their raids are the mayor's initiative: that they won't know for a long time (D084). The blessing comes first and unseen: cover, resources, a public thank-you to "our Directorate cadets". The curse comes when what is good for the city and what is good for their own position pull apart, and the party learns which one the mayor protects first. Where and when are Ingram's to set; so is when they find out.
