## What the rebuild changed

Rebuilt whole in the house style (D056). GM text went from about 6,050 words to 617, plus 306 of check results.

- **Manako owns the tea house** (D034: "the kindly old man who works in the tea shop... running a successful small
  business"). The old draft made him a poor regular at a corner table. His district defers to him: the regulars nod,
  his tea comes unasked (D046).
- **One route to him.** The instrument maker laughs and, on any result, sends them on. The old draft had three
  separate routes (the maker, an ask-around search, a critical success).
- **The docket's joke is the maker's.** "Love, we make the vials. There's nothing in the bubble." The old "obsolete
  catalogue name" device, which needed Manako to decode it, is gone; his job is the plan.
- **His key line opens the plan (D045):** he came back empty-handed, they laughed, he's had a long time to think.
  Then the vial, as D018 has it. He writes the part number on the back of a bill, and they keep it.
- **His line about the Directorate is now a required beat**, as the episode always meant. It is the vault line Ingram
  accepted in D044; `directorate.digs` is therefore told here now (D056 records it).
- **His number goes to one player, at the GM's call, no roll (D045).** The Scene Runner button for it isn't built,
  so the line says to hand the player a note.
- **What he won't say has a real deflection** in his own voice. The old line ("nothing has happened in this city yet,
  he is not acting") contradicted the stencils and the police clash, and was the conspicuous denial D053 warns about.
- **No pauses for effect.** The old read-alouds had three scripted pauses; Ingram's D045 said mystery makes players
  more alert, so he is plainly kind and plainly bitter.
- **Geography (D048):** no "street below the old town". The dogs' street is just a side street.
- **The reagent-stock plant is cut.** It had no payoff anywhere.

## Manako: the leak check (D044's standing rule)

Every line he says, asked "what could a sharp player deduce, and is that earlier than intended?"

- "Manako. I was a chemist... For them, as it happens." Allowed (D045). Deduction: he worked for the Directorate.
  Fine, and true.
- "They did this to me, you know... I came back with nothing, and they laughed." Allowed (D045). Deduction: the
  hazing is old. Fine.
- The plan. Pure procedure.
- "They're not wicked. They're just very good at not talking." and the vault line. Allowed (D016, D044). Deduction:
  the Directorate keeps things locked away. That is the half-right conclusion Ingram wants available.
- "Somebody's sign for something. The city's full of them." His only deflection, about the violet marks. It is
  ordinary, said with a shrug, and points at nothing. A player who later learns the truth can read it as a lie,
  which is fair.
- "They decided I'd made a fool of myself. Maybe I had." About leaving the Directorate. Matches the very hard
  knowledge check (a breakdown, an expulsion) without saying why.
- Never mentioned: the Watcher, the sky, his wife, Cicada, the conference's cause, the revenge list.
