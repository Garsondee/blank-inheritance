## What the rebuild changed

Rebuilt whole in the house style (D056). GM text went from about 3,350 words to 680, plus 259 of check results.

- **It opens at the Candidate Gate**, where The Road In leaves them: the guard with the ledger takes their return
  slips. One sentence, so the gate From Up Here describes is a place they actually pass.
- **Section Nine is the six.** The old draft had "thirty-odd people" in the section and the six sorted on Monday,
  before the screen. Now the section is decided by the screen: the cards are handed out in the corridor afterwards,
  which is what the all-six moment turns on.
- **The founders' names (D048).** The old Shucks answered "that Shucks Mordant, the one from the speech", but Scene 0
  no longer names anyone. Now the table has his badge, a free Society roll to recognise the name, and his own "Shucks.
  Yes, that one." The other three names come out in the first school period. Shucks is shown to the table as "Doctor
  Mordant" until someone learns his first name; one press of the inline key tells them.
- **No local families.** Every character is a newcomer (D026, the induction), so Shucks no longer remembers their
  parents. He asks what they left behind, or about an old injury.
- **The charter moved to Assembly**, where the drill hall hangs it. It was in two places 1.3 km apart.
- **Checks cut:** reading Shucks (folded into asking him straight), making him like you (the private moment already
  does that), and palming the form (the clerk gives a spare to anyone who asks).
- **The location was a file that doesn't exist** (`intake-medicals`); it is now `intake-infirmary`, the one the scene
  set already uses.
- **Craig played Shucks.** A "Watch for" line on the doctor's beat says so, so his character's turn gets room.
