## What the rebuild changed

Rebuilt whole in the house style (D056). GM text went from about 3,390 words to 590, plus 132 of check results.

- **It ends on the night's rests (D050).** Each player picks their own on The City: the ten approved rests (D051)
  and "somewhere new" (D052), which on the first night is the Magic Show, shown as a blurred mystery card. The old
  ending, a closing beat about being sorted together on purpose, was an unratified proposal and is cut; the six
  already know they're all irregular and all Section Nine.
- **Rand pays off tonight.** Vetch is waiting at the top with their requisition, countersigned in green ink: Rand,
  Medical. "Which one of you is Rand? Nobody called Rand works in Medical. I've asked." That is the episode's last
  laugh and its open question, in place of Episode Two's reveal (Episode Two is a ghost, D056).
- **Every earlier choice pays off here, in six carry-in lines:** natural history (the centipedes known), its
  critical success (a show of force works), the workshop induction (+2 on the panels), the range (+1 on a first
  ranged Strike), anyone who missed out on a handlamp at the stores, and anyone Fatigued. Lamps are personal kit
  from the stores; the night lockup issues only waders, so local studies' cost is real down here.
- **The three endings are one choice** (kill, drive off, use the room), each worth full XP, in place of three beats
  and three notes.
- **Reading the water is a real check** with written results, in place of an unwritten GM call.
- **The power check's failure has a real cost:** the nest wakes before anyone is in position (and a critical failure
  leaves the worker off-guard).
