## Ingram's first read, and what each note changed

Her nineteen notes are in `canon/decisions/D048.md`, word for word, and the country's name is D049.

1. **Too much, too fast.** The four founders aren't named, and where the rift stopped isn't said. Rupiah asking the
   gods, and the gods' answer, are gone. In their place is a question: why the Directorate believes there will be a
   next time, and why it crossed the world to wait for it.
2. **Directions.** None left. No beat has one.
3. **The ledger.** Answered in D048 ("What Claude found"). Her First Impact dossier, as first filed, is now a source
   record (`canon/sources/S001.md`), and the rift is told with its detail: the violet light with a black heart, growth
   that sped up in fits and starts, the clerics, wizards and sorcerers it swallowed, the jagged road, the fighters from
   the island and then the region, and the ending nobody who was there could confirm.
4. **Style.** Fuller sentences throughout. Her prophecy wording is kept, word for word, for when it's revealed.
5. **Length.** From 578 words in three beats to 1,296 in seven, about nine minutes: the age, the rift, the Directorate,
   Kurose and its people, Frames, the city now, and the gate.
6. **The old town** is on the waterfront, where the village was.
7. **Her Frame line**, word for word.
8. **What a Frame is**: a walking machine shaped roughly like a person, taller than the warehouses, with a pilot inside
   working its arms and legs.
9. **The direction about the joke** is gone.
10. **"The gods said Kurose…"** went with the prophecy.
11. **Founding Day**, in her words, then the city now: electric light, radio, trams and factories; change so fast it
    made winners and losers; the wealth few can argue with; Minkai's wounded pride; groups who see the Directorate as a
    foreign thorn; unrest; organised crime.
12. **The generals and admirals** now complain in the newspapers about a foreign institution that keeps a private base
    in their harbour, answers to nobody in Minkai, and drills for a disaster that may never come.
13. **Cicada** is out. Unrest and the gangs are in.
14. **The base, the fence and the gate** are described in full: the island, the causeway, the fortress, the fence, the
    barrier, the booth and the guard with a ledger.
15. **The promise line** is gone.
16. **Trainees**: most people think of it as a faff and a bother, a duty that's done once it's done; some volunteer,
    some are encouraged, some are pushed. Then her approved last line.
17. **The direction about the next scene** is gone, and so is every other mention of another scene in the GM text.
18. **No rolls.** The Recall Knowledge answers and their challenge are deleted.
19. **No questions.** The six optional answer beats are gone.
