version 1 · re-read against `canon/reread.md` version 3 · 2026-10-01 · by Claude · state: awaiting Ingram's read

## What the rebuild changed

Rebuilt whole in the house style (D056), from the old draft Ingram read in the Scene Runner.

- **GM text went from 2,915 words to 862**, check results included. Nothing she needs at the table was lost: the
  reasons went to the prep notes, the rules about the world to canon facts, and the history to this record.
- **The Frame sighting is cut.** From Up Here already shows the whole table Frames at work on the docks, patched,
  loud and hard to drive, so the beat had no job left.
- **The disguise line is corrected.** The old note said every character arrives looking terrestrial. D009 and D044
  make it the player's call, and plenty of aliens in Kurose refuse glamour, so the note now says that.
- **The violet mark is required.** It was an optional beat whose note said "guaranteed"; it is now a required beat
  with an "If no arrival fits" line.
- **The letter** is Ingram's approved handout (D038), unchanged except one line: it no longer calls Minkai a kingdom,
  since `minkai.government` is open. It now reads "owed to Minkai's own army or navy".
- **The letters arrive in the same week**, not weeks apart, so "Monday next" means the same Monday for all six.
- **The handout card** is one list of "if they ask about" lines, each detail with its answer, and the secret in the
  GM notes. The old card said the same things three times (reserve detail, GM notes, catchables).

## The questions

**R1, R2. Does every part earn its place?** Yes. The letter is the first handout and plants the stamp; the three
questions give each player their first moment; the small problem is each player's first roll; the violet mark is the
episode's first plant. Cut: the Frame sighting (no job left), `firstThing` and `holdInReserve` (they repeated the
read-aloud and the handout).

**R16. Directions.** Two, both about pace: one question at a time, and hand the story back to the player. Both keep a
six-player montage short, which is the scene's main risk.

**R17. Negatives.** One: "Say nothing about them" for the letter's details, because pointing at a plant spends it.

**R18. Preparing for what the shape rules out.** No. There are no NPCs to answer questions.

**R3. How the scene was made.** Nothing in the GM text mentions decisions, dates, other scenes' insides or tools.
"Show the table" names the Runner's own button, which is what she presses.

**R12, R19, R20. Giving away too much.** The table learns the stamp exists, not why (`directorate.why-brought-forward`
is `told: later`). The marks are described, never explained. No line tells the players what anything means.

**R4, R5, R6. Rolls.** Every player rolls once (the small problem, any skill, DC 15). The mark is a free knowledge
check, DC 17. Critical failure on the small problem is funny, never harmful; failure gets them there late; critical
success gives the player something worth remembering. The mark's critical failure gives a confident wrong belief.

**R7 to R10. People.** No NPCs, on purpose: the players invent their own journeys.

**R15, R21. Read aloud.** Two short sentences, full and plain. The letter is Ingram's.

**R22. Coined words.** None new. "Frame" was explained in From Up Here.

**R23. Length.** 25 to 35 minutes, set by six players' turns. The GM text is short because the players do the talking.

**R26. Most important first.** Every beat line starts with what the GM does. Each note's first line is the one that
breaks the beat if missed (the plant, then the fallback, then the secret).

**R27. Said once.** "Nothing rides on these rolls" is said once, on the small problem; "keep them apart" once, in how
to play it and the ending's stop line; the letter's details once, on the handout card.

**R28. Budgets.** Every field is inside its budget. Longest beat line: 25 words. Longest note: 45 words. Whole scene:
639 words of GM text, plus 223 of check results.

**R11. What a player would retell.** Their own arrival, told in their own words.

**R13. Canon.** Cited: `directorate.sends-for-trainees`, `directorate.brought-forward-stamp` (D038),
`directorate.why-brought-forward` (D002, D011, D020, D038), `glamour.aliens-outsiders`, `glamour.refusers`,
`cicada.violet-marks` and `cicada.marks-meaning` (both provisional, from this scene).

**R14. Time.** Day 0, morning. "Monday next" and "by Monday" agree with Intake Medicals on Day 1.

**R24. Places.** Every arrival point is on the map (the departure's places); the scene ends converging on the
Candidate Gate.

**R25. Ingram's detail.** D038's letter is used in full: the stamp, the printed slip, the private copies, the
typewriter, Form 1-A.
