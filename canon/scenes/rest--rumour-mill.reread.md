# Re-read: The Rumour Mill

version 1 · re-read against `canon/reread.md` version 2 · 2026-09-29 · by Claude · state: awaiting Ingram's read

Built on Ingram's rules in D051, word for word there: three sets an episode; a player names any skill they can
justify and rolls blind; critical failure three wrong, failure two wrong and one true, success two true and one
wrong, critical success three true; short, one sentence; some inviting a guess at what else is true; at most one per
set touching the plot. The Runner deals them (`core/rumours.mjs`).

**Does every part earn its place?**
- **R1, R2** The round is the scene; the deck fills the gaps between rounds. Every event gives a player something to
  say, roll or keep.
- **R16, R17, R18** One direction: how the round is run, which the GM needs. No "don't".

**Is it clean?**
- **R3** Nothing about how it was made. Rumours that touch the plot carry the canon fact they touch, GM-only.

**Does it give away too much?** The reason this haunt was written with the most care.
- **R12** This block's rumour mill is about the screening tests, and the players' own blood is the one subject that
  must not leak. Every rumour about "the marker" is a lie or a harmless truth (the blood draw is short and unexplained;
  a second medical in spring; the fare paid home). No rumour says anything about anyone's results, and `your-theory`
  lets players guess aloud while nobody confirms anything.
- **R19** Plot rumours, one set at a time:
  - E1 set 1, a **lie**: the founders are buried under the painting (misleads about `founders.alive`).
  - E1 set 2: **none**, to keep the medical set clean.
  - E1 set 3, a **truth already told**: nobody has said why the Directorate came to Kurose
    (`directorate.why-kurose-unexplained`, told from the start). It turns a known fact into a question.
  - E2 set 1, a **lie**: a Frame hidden in the machine-shop pit. It brushes `frames.gen-2`, held for later, and
    misleads about where anything newer would be. **For Ingram:** whether even a false hint at a newer Frame is too
    early.
  - E2 set 2: **none**.
  - E2 set 3, a **lie**: the founders never existed.
- **R20** Nothing tells the players which rumours matter.

**Do the players roll?**
- **R4, R5, R6** Every player rolls in the round. Three more events roll (`obis-expedition`, `two-knocks`,
  `the-came-true-column`), with all four degrees.

**Who's in it?**
- **R7, R8** Hana, Obi and Mrs. Ueda each have a conversation map with likely questions in their voice.
- **R9** **Missing:** stat blocks, proposed levels only, waiting for the NPC generator.
- **R10** In `notes`: reporting it, stealing the notebook, lying about the medical, splitting.

**Is it well said?**
- **R15, R21** The read-aloud is full sentences. Rumours are one sentence each, as asked.
- **R22** "The marker" is explained where it is first heard (Hana's answer).

**Does it hold up?**
- **R11** A player's own made-up rumour coming back changed, from somebody else.
- **R13** Rumour truths agree with canon or with the other haunts written the same day (the slate, the fitness list,
  the observer's twenty years, the card game's age). Shucks Mordant as head of the medical side is told at Intake
  Medicals, before this scene. **Open:** Episode Two's three sets were written before Episode Two is rebuilt, and want
  another look when it is.
- **R14** Day 1, night, in the plan.
- **R24** Row B, Block 8, as the map has it.
- **R25** D051's rumour rules, all of them.
