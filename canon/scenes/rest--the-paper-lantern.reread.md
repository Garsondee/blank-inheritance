# Re-read: The Paper Lantern

version 1 · re-read against `canon/reread.md` version 3 · 2026-10-05 · by Claude · state: awaiting Ingram's read

The eleventh night rest, added at Ingram's request (2026-09-29): the civilian counterpart to the Crew Mess, off Directorate ground. Read
whole, against every question, the day it was first sited on the real map (a building of The Slope, picked by Ingram in the Location Editor).
This is the first re-read it has had.

**Does every part earn its place?**
- **R1** Every event gives a player something to roll, say, hear or keep: a round to stand (a roll), Sanzo's story and Tama's question (a
  prompt each, and a step with a regular), the song (a buff), the walk back (a roll), the argument (a prompt), the loose tongue (a thing
  learned), the door (a roll, and a fight if it goes wrong). The four beats are the room, each player's evening, the deck and last orders.
- **R2** Cutting any one event leaves the deck thinner, not broken, which is what a deck is for.
- **R16** Two directions survive and both are delivery that matters: "give them a moment to picture it" before the evening question, and
  "put back any the table never saw". The "press his button" lines in the notes are the Runner's own control, said once where it is used.
- **R17** The negatives that stay each rule something out the GM would otherwise run: no roll on the two prompts that are only
  conversation, no roll to be refused the back room, and that curfew costs an awkward morning and no more.
- **R18** Nothing prepares for what the shape rules out.

**Is it clean?**
- **R3** **Found and fixed:** the walk-back note named a decision number ("D053"). It now says only that curfew is a minor offence and what
  that means at the table. Nothing else mentions how the scene was made.

**Does it give away too much?**
- **R12** The loose tongue (unmarked cargo leaving the old harbour sheds at night, no manifest) hands the table one small unexplained
  fact and answers nothing: he can say when, not why. **For Ingram:** this fact is in no canon file. It is a plant for later, written with
  the scene, and nothing yet depends on it. Keep it as a seed, name what it is, or cut it.
- **R19** The informant is the one answer the scene gives, and it is deliberately one-sided: a drunk's half-fact, not a fact about the
  plot. The rest of the room's talk is Kiku's, Sanzo's and Tama's, in their voices.
- **R20** No line says what the base's growth means. The argument asks the table the question and Kiku listens.

**Do the players roll?**
- **R4, R5, R6** Three events roll (`stand-a-round`, `the-walk-back`, `trouble-at-the-door`), each with all four degrees: a critical
  success gives something extra (the corner booth, a wave through the checkpoint, a replacement drink), a failure moves the night on, a
  critical failure escalates but never closes a door (a sore subject at the counter, a mark in the file, the scuffle aimed at them). The
  three roleplay prompts and the song have nothing to roll on purpose.
- **R5** **Missing, for Ingram:** the deck has no dilemma. D051 asks for dilemmas that mirror the city changing; `the-argument` is the
  nearest thing, and it is a prompt with no cost either way. The Crew Mess has one (`the-petition`). Not written here: it is a creative
  call, and the base creeping into the quarter is the obvious seed for it.

**Who's in it?**
- **R7, R8** Kiku, Sanzo and Tama each have a conversation map: what they give freely, what they will not say and why, what a door at
  each attitude opens or closes, and at least five likely questions answered in their own voice (a test holds that).
- **R9** All four who can be dealt with now have stat blocks from the NPC generator (`data/npcs`, drafted). Perception, Will and Deception
  DCs, from those blocks: Kiku, level 2, 18 / 18 / 13; Sanzo, level 1, 17 / 20 / 14; Tama, level 0, 16 / 16 / 10; the off-duty soldiers,
  level 1, 17 / 14 / 11 (untrained Deception is 10 plus Charisma). In the scene "Kiku's Will DC" is therefore 18, and lying to Kiku is
  Deception against her Perception DC of 18.
- **R10** In `notes`: leaving without paying, starting the fight, lying about being candidates, asking for the back room early, splitting up.
  The soldiers' fight is theatre of the mind with fists only, and they give up once the loudest is down.

**Is it well said?**
- **R15** The read-aloud was read aloud: three short paragraphs that end on Kiku weighing the room for exactly one second.
- **R21** No clipped fragments in anything said to the table.
- **R22** "Noren curtain" and "shamisen" are placed by what is said around them (a curtain over a doorway, someone playing not quite in
  time). "Izakaya" appears only in the GM's own lines.
- **R23** A haunt is long in deck and short in beats; the four beats are four lines.
- **R26, R27, R28** Each beat starts with what the GM does; each rule is said once, where it is used; every field is inside the style
  guide's budget (the prose tests hold the fields, and pass).

**Does it hold up?**
- **R11** Kiku sizing the group up in exactly one second, or the toast that turns out to be a sore subject.
- **R13** Facts agree with canon: curfew is a minor offence; the level-based DC at level 1 is 15; a night's rest here is a quarter of maximum
  HP with the morning Fortitude save. No founder, no Watcher, nothing about Frames.
- **R14** Day 1, night, in the plan; `story time` reads "tonight, tomorrow" and nothing disagrees.
- **R24** **Checked against the map, now that it has one.** The building is in The Slope (a district of terraces and retaining walls). The
  scene never names a district: "the entertainment quarter" is what the plan, Kiku's file and this scene call the
  corner of the city, not a district id, and The Slope's terraces fit "a side street". Nothing contradicts, so no wording changed. The plan's
  "not yet sited" line was stale and now says where it is. **For Ingram:** if the Slope is not the entertainment quarter to you, the wording
  in the plan, the scene, the scene-set blurb and Kiku's file all change together.
- **R25** D050, D051, D052 and D053 read: a quarter of maximum HP and the morning Fortitude save (D050); the haunt as a menu with a deck of
  rolls, a buff, prompts, an informant and a danger event, and attitudes that move (D051); doors, a shared faction, upgrades and setbacks and a
  fight with ways out (D052); curfew as a very minor offence (D053 and `design/directorate-discipline.md`). Missing from the deck: a dilemma and a plot event (see R5).
