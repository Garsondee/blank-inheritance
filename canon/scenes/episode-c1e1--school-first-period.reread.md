Rebuilt whole in the house style (D056). GM text went from about 2,240 words to 432, plus 248 of check results.

  Episode 3 or 4 reveal of why the Directorate came here. Gone.
- **The old town is on the waterfront** (D048). The old Survival line put it below a hill; now the map is read by its
  water and its outfalls, which also sets up tonight.
- **The rooms' costs are wired.** A failure in local studies makes them last into the stores, where the handlamps have
  run out (a real cost tonight, in a dark drain). A critical failure in either room sends them into tonight's job
  Fatigued. Passing the induction gives +2 on the drain chamber's panels. The old draft's "lose the afternoon's
  slack" no longer had anything to lose.
- **The tram map is an artefact**, so a critical success leaves the player holding something.
- **Two notes for the table's history:** four of the players played the four founders, and Steve played Hyuck, so the
  mascot joke is his.
