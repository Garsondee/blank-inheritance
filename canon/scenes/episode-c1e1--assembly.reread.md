## What the rebuild changed

Rebuilt whole in the house style (D056). GM text went from about 2,135 words to 296, plus 174 of check results; the
address is about 330 words read aloud.

- **The address carries D053.** In character, the Deputy Director says the Directorate is a school and a service at
  once; that afternoons are theirs to choose between the gymnasium, the range, the library or a lesson; and the two
  rules: curfew is a small thing, a refused order is the most serious thing they can do, and means explaining
  themselves to someone like him.
- **The dig is gone from the speech.** The old address said the city "was built on top of a dig" and it was "on the
  municipal seal". That contradicts canon: Kurose was a fishing village, the Directorate has never said why it came,
  and the digging is known to few (`directorate.why-kurose-unexplained`, `directorate.digs`).
- **No "your parents".** Every character is a newcomer, so the anthem is a song "everyone over thirty knows", not
  one their families sang.
- **Vetch collects Section Nine** at the end and names himself. The episode now meets him in person four times:
  here, Kit Issue, Canteen, and at the hatch.
- **The charter lives here only**, with its four signatures (D044) as a free check.
- **The Deputy Director says his name.** It was a placeholder; I've proposed Seiichi Takamine (see below).
- **Rumours moved to the Canteen**, the real crowd scene, where the Runner's rumour mill deals them in one press.
