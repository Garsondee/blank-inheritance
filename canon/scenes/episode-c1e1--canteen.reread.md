## What the rebuild changed

Rebuilt whole in the house style (D056). GM text went from about 1,800 words to 368, plus 127 of check results.

- **The drains are payback for the vial.** Vetch gave Section Nine the worst job because of the requisition; a
  critical success reading him shows the stores receipt clipped to his board. He never says so.
- **Vetch is the duty officer reading the evening's orders**, and two other sections are read first, so his looking
  up for Section Nine lands.
- **The job is at twenty-one hundred from Dock C.** The old order said "report by nineteen hundred", which clashed
  with The Errand's deadline and the requisition.
- **Taka gets the last word**, after the order, and the drains question comes after the drains are mentioned. The
  old scene let the table ask him about the drains before anyone had been sent there.
- **Cut:** the drains knowledge check (the second period's natural history lesson does that job now) and the
  meet-someone roll (Taka is the scene's social heart). No rumours here: they belong to the Rumour Mill tonight.
- **The technical-track candidate is gone**; the Young One stays as one line at the next table.
- **The radio plant (D044) is kept**, word for word as accepted, with its secret.
- **Taka asks each of them where they're properly from**, so every player gets a turn at the table before the order.
