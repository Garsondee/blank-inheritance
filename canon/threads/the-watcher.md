The generation ship's broken eyes, loose over Kurose. Each new generation of its children answers the party's last tactic, and what it learns is what they show it.

## By Book
- I: Three strange, flowing children (Episode Two); children that hunt whatever moves (Chapter 2); Breakers that go for the same joint on every machine (Chapters 3 and 4); at the Square the party is there before they form. In Book I they form only where Sap was released.
- II: Seekers, then G4s: their machines, then their people; it watches the convoy (Chapter 8).
- III: The Directorate itself, then the Reel.
- IV: People-shaped; takes the exotic matter (Chapter 13); feeds itself; builds a weapon (Chapter 14); Second Impact (Chapter 15).
- V: Still over Kurose, still escalating; its fate is open.
