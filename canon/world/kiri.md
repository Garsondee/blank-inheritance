Kiri knows Manako is dying, and nobody else in the Cadre has done the arithmetic. She is quietly planning who takes over from him: what she is building to outlast him is entirely loyal and also a betrayal in embryo, and if the party works it out, they have a lever on the most dangerous person in the organisation.

## kiri.how-she-met-him
Kiri was a working illusionist on the guilty-pleasure circuit when Manako came to one of her shows. Afterwards he showed her the lens and asked her to look, and she looked: a professional deceiver shown something real by a man who had told the truth for forty years and been called a liar for it. Like all four lieutenants, she isn't a believer in the ordinary sense: she is a witness, who couldn't un-see what she was shown.

## kiri.wants-good-shows
Kiri wants the shows to be good. She is the only person in Cicada who cares about the craft, and she is quietly insulted that the movement treats her work as a recruitment tool rather than a performance that happens to be true.

## kiri.fights-on-her-own-stage
Kiri is an illusionist with a knife and no scruples about either: misdirection first (figures that aren't there, a room that isn't the shape they think, a door where a wall is), then mental damage, then a blade, close, quick and apologetic. She is a duellist who fights on a stage she built, not a support caster. When she is hit, the figure struck is revealed to be somebody else: a local, paid or charmed, standing in since before the party arrived, and the party is left with an injured bystander on the floor and no idea where she is. Her escape is Curtain: the lights go, and when they return the person on the ground is the stand-in. Her counter: stagecraft needs a stage. Both the Understudy and Curtain need an accomplice placed in advance, so if the party picks the ground, she has neither, and is an elderly performer with a knife.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D073, D081 · from: d-cicada:the-four-in-full, d-cicada:the-four-who-actually-turn-up · known: nobody · told: later

## kiri.voice
Kiri addresses everybody as an audience: "ladies and gentlemen" to six teenagers in a drain, entirely sincerely. She announces what she is about to do before she does it, which is stage patter and a real tell the party can learn to read. She compliments them, and means it, while trying to kill them. Her line is "Watch my hands": a courtesy that has never once helped anybody. Her other tell is that she stops performing: insult Manako in front of her and the theatre drops out of her voice completely, the most frightening thing she does, and it takes one sentence.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D073, D081 · from: d-cicada:the-four-in-full · known: nobody · told: later

## kiri.shares-a-teacher
Kiri, stage name (it means mist), has a dull real name in a Directorate file somewhere. She shares a craft, and possibly a teacher, with Rupiah, who can identify her technique from a witness's description: the Directorate's public face is useful, and it implicates his own past. Kiri is the cleanest case in the cast that the Faithful/Cadre line runs through people, not only through groups.
