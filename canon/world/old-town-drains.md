# The Old Town Drains

## old-town-drains.smuggled-crates
The jam in the compactor feed under the old town is a stack of smuggled crates that someone has hidden in the drains. It is a hint of the criminal side of Kurose.

## old-town-drains.what-is-in-the-crates
The crates hold untaxed goods: cases of rice wine, cartons of cigarettes with no tax stamp, tins of lamp oil, and a bundle of blank customs forms that are already stamped. Every crate carries the Old Landing chandlers' stamp and a tally chalked on the lid, and they are packed in straw. They say that someone is smuggling and nothing more; no violet paint is in them.

## old-town-drains.the-old-hatch
Someone has cut the padlock on the old hatch in the quay and put on a newer one. The Directorate's night crews have not used this chamber for years, so a smuggler could store crates here and expect nobody to look.

## old-town-drains.the-nest
The giant centipedes nest in the straw the crates are packed in. The smugglers did not know it was there, and the crates are the reason the nest woke when the party powered the line.

## old-town-drains.the-salt-dogs-store-here
The Salt Dogs, the tug crews who run the harbour at night, use the old town drains to carry crates from the quay past the customs hall and into the city without being inspected. They will come to find out why the line was powered and the crates were opened.
