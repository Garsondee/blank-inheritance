## deep-storage.what-it-is
Deep Storage is so called because it is a bunker warehouse where anything unusual is stored while it isn't being used: many underground floors, most of which need raised security clearance to reach.

## deep-storage.the-way-down
Deep Storage lies under the working crane yard of the Container Ground, on the island's eastern apron. Above ground there is only a padlocked equipment shed wedged between the container stacks, easy to miss and meant to be, holding a single freight lift whose floor panel skips several numbers without explanation. Below, the air is cold and smells of oil and old paper. Each floor is one long corridor of numbered doors, most unmarked, a few stencilled with a unit or a clearance mark. Some floors are lit and swept; others stay dark until someone finds the switch, and haven't had a visitor in years. The floor plans disagree with each other.

## deep-storage.who-gets-in
A custodian runs Deep Storage day to day and decides what counts as "currently being used": that judgement, and whom it favours, is the place's real content. Below the second underground floor, access goes by Clearance: Duty Section gets a supervised look, Officers get a requisition honoured without a countersignature, and anything past that needs the custodian's personal word. The custodian remembers everyone let onto a floor above their rank, and the debt is called in later, not always by the custodian. If anyone is caught taking something from Deep Storage without signing for it, security tightens for everyone: more inventories, a second lock on the lift, and a custodian who stops answering questions they used to answer for free.

## deep-storage.what-it-keeps
Deep Storage was dug out and widened in stages as the Directorate gathered things it didn't want destroyed and didn't want found. It went under a cargo yard rather than inside the Directorate's own complex on purpose: nobody audits a warehouse basement as carefully as a garrison. For as long as the hangar has existed, Frame hardware too compromised, obsolete or politically awkward for the active roster has been moved to Deep Storage quietly. Which units, and how many, is not decided.

## deep-storage.unfiled-manifest
At least one manifest in Deep Storage was never filed at any clearance level at all, which is a worse problem than a redacted one. What it covers is decided only when the party goes looking.
