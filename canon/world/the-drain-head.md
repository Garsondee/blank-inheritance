## the-drain-head.the-cover
The Drain Head is a sealed cast-iron storm-drain cover, flush with the road in the Landing End and stamped with a maintenance code. It sits a little proud of true, as if lifted and reseated more than once by somebody in a hurry, and no visit has been scheduled against its code in some time. At high tide, on a still day, something moves underneath the Drain Head loud enough to hear from the street. Reported up the chain, it is always told a visit is scheduled.

## the-drain-head.not-the-old-town
The Drain Head is part of the island's own drainage, not the mainland's: whatever is down there is a separate problem from the drains under the old town. No civilian knows it is there, and to anyone outside the Directorate it is only a maintenance problem.
