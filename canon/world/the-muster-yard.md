## the-muster-yard.the-board
The Muster Yard, at the centre of the Landing End, is crossed twice a day by everyone on that half of the island. Its duty board, the width of a bus under a tin hood, is a grid of wooden slot-tiles holding a fortnight of names, and its sound is the sound of the yard: tiles taken down and put back, like a slow game of dominoes. Anything on the duty board is known by everyone within the hour. A duty change isn't delivered privately: strangers read it before the person it belongs to reaches the yard, and a change made there is public and stands for the fortnight.

## the-muster-yard.hatch-and-clock
The clerk behind the hatch in the wall keeps the board, with a stamp and a ledger, and moves a tile only on the right paper, stamped. He isn't difficult, only correct: pressure doesn't work, paperwork does. Above everything is the yard clock, which is never wrong and never quite agrees with anyone's watch. Evening muster is measured by it and no other.
