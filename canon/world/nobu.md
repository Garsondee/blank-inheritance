## nobu.the-funding
Nobu secretly funds the column, through the crime lords, as private amends for not backing Manako up. He believes the money he sends to the column is amends; in fact it makes him the operation's own supply line. Directorate procurement money, washed through the crime lords' organisation, pays for Cicada's column. If that organisation is the oldest enemy of the four who founded the Directorate, the Directorate is paying for what is poisoning its sky.

## nobu.quartermaster
Nobu runs Kit Issue and the budgets: harried, and generous in small, unexplained ways ("Right. Section Nine. I've got you."). He is who the party argues with about repairs. His generosity is impulsive, never deliberate: he does the generous thing too fast to examine it, doesn't make eye contact while he does it, waves off any thanks at once and changes the subject. Shucks watches a kid closely while being kind; Nobu looks away. He reads as a man compensating for something, not a man at peace with the institution. Shucks is warm because he has infinite time; Nobu is generous despite having none.

## nobu.the-debt
Nobu stood by and said nothing when it mattered, watched a man's life come apart, and kept his job. He has risen through procurement since: he runs Directorate procurement (materials, permits, budgets) and now controls the pool of technician-days that repairs and upgrades the Frames, so he is who the party argues with about repairs, and who the Caretaker has to beg for every accommodation. She thinks him unusually kind. He is paying a debt she knows nothing about. He believes he is making amends. A man with a guilty conscience and signing authority is exactly who the crime lords recruit.

## nobu.opinions-on-gear
Nobu has issued the kit list ten thousand times and has genuine favourites: asked what he would take himself, he tells you at speed, and he is right. A short, specific, well-aimed request gets results; a vague one loses to the queue behind you.

## nobu.rules-on-the-sit-in
Nobu controls the technician-day pool, and Bobbin is occupying a Frame, so Nobu must rule on the sit-in. The man secretly funding the enemy has to decide whether to authorise a forced extraction that would kill a pacifist. He will do the right thing for entirely the wrong reason, and nobody will ever know why.
