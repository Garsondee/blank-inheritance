## column.the-plant
The column is a secret industrial plant in the city that makes Sap at industrial scale, running continuously, and pours it into the sky as a black column of nanoparticles that rises from the plant and spreads upward into the cloud, visible only through a lens, day and night. It has run for about five years. It is a working chimney, pouring food into the sky day and night: building material, not fuel.

## column.second-link-and-the-runaway
The column is the second link in the Watcher's supply chain: first one man it taught to make Sap, then this plant, then its own children making their own. The runaway is the moment it no longer needs any human to feed it. Before the runaway, cutting the column really does starve the Watcher; after it, the plant is one supplier among many, and taking it down is a win over the people, not the appetite. The column is about five years old by design: if it had run at full scale for forty years the Watcher would already be saturated and the escalation curve would mean nothing.

## column.taking-it-down
Taking the column down is a real triumph and should be played as one, and it raises more questions than it answers: how much of what is overhead is this plant's fault, and will cutting it slow anything down at all?
