## anabala.recruited-the-four

## anabala.flew-it
Anabala could fly the Gen 2 she built when almost nobody else could, and even she suffered Frame Sickness. Almost nobody else could fly it, so the programme was mothballed until Shucks's screen found the pilots who could. The Directorate's record of those trials is thin on exactly who succeeded.

## anabala.macthata
In the old campaign she was Anabala Macthata, a mitflit of ifrit heritage, first built as a strange robot: a pact-broker ("I can find things and I can make things disappear. You owe me one favour."), with air-and-fire kineticist powers, Loremaster and Investigator training, many languages including Androffan, and a habit of shrinking things into bottles. She ran the mitflit government at Gauntlight Keep.
