## shucks.rand
Shucks's right hand is a prosthetic. His real right hand, Rand, is a detached familiar loose in the city, and he doesn't know where it is from day to day.

## shucks.maiden-names
Shucks has the intake forms ask for mothers' maiden names so he can trace the marker through families. Nobody else remembers why the forms ask.

## shucks.old-handwriting
Rand writes in Shucks's own handwriting from before the hand came off. Shucks writes left-handed now, so nobody alive knows that hand, but anything the founders signed two hundred years ago shows it, the charter included.
status: decided · source: D034 · known: nobody · told: later

## shucks.rand-signs
Rand countersigns Section Nine's vial requisition the night they file it, with Medical's stamp from Shucks's desk and in Shucks's old handwriting, because it enjoys making the rules bite the officer who hazed them. Nobody called Rand works in Medical.
status: decided · source: D034, D043, D046 · known: nobody · told: later

## shucks.rand-signature
When Section Nine climb out of the drains, Vetch is holding their vial requisition, countersigned in green ink over Medical's stamp: Rand, Medical. Nobody called Rand works in Medical; he has asked.
status: provisional · source: episode-c1e1--flood-tunnel · known: few · told: in:episode-c1e1--flood-tunnel

## shucks.rand-reads-the-desk
Rand reads everything on Shucks's desk. The night after Intake Medicals it read his notes and found six carriers of the marker in one intake, which it had never seen.
status: accepted · source: D043, D046 · known: nobody · told: later

## shucks.rand-signs-whatever
If Section Nine files no requisition, Rand countersigns whatever paper of theirs goes in that night instead: the docket handed back, or the receipt for a vial they bought. On the back of a docket it writes "Received, one (1)."
status: provisional · source: episode-c1e1--the-tram-back · known: nobody · told: later
