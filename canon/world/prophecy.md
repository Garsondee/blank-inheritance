## prophecy.asked

## prophecy.answer

status: decided · source: D025, D026, D048, D049 · known: few · told: later

## prophecy.divination-fails

status: decided · source: D026 · known: some · told: later

## Notes
When it's revealed (around Episode 3 or 4, D048), it answers why the Directorate is in Kurose instead of Kortos. Ingram's own wording is the model: "And the gods said yes, but in hundreds of years of time and thousands of miles of distance. In the tiny fishing village of Kurose in (Golarion's equivalent of Japan)." The country is Minkai (D049).
