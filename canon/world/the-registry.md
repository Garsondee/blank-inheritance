## the-registry.what-it-is
The Registry keeps the records for the whole island: forty typewriters going at once around a light well of grey daylight, a print room at the back smelling of ink and hot metal, and everything on a trolley going somewhere. Nothing is handed over without a signature, and whoever takes it reads the name upside down and remembers it.

## the-registry.typed-per-clearance
A document the Directorate issues at several clearance levels is typed separately for each one in the Registry's print room, never censored afterwards. The lower-cleared copy is its own document, not a marked-up copy of the higher: the words its reader isn't cleared for were never set on that page. A fuller version is a different object, made by people, so getting one means persuading somebody or intercepting a trolley; there is nothing to decrypt. The Registry's typists know every version of everything and hold no clearance worth speaking of. They are the best-informed people on the island and are paid as if they were the least, an absurdity the Directorate has never noticed.
