## the-rock.secret-prison-and-laboratory
The Rock is the Directorate's military prison and laboratory: a heavily armoured bunker complex cut into the same natural rock as Central, and reached only by a tunnel from Central, with no door of its own. A warden runs the prison side and a research director the laboratory side. Neither is one of the Directorate's known faces. Almost nobody knows The Rock exists. Students and most staff know nothing of its purpose, and most current staff have never heard its name: its existence is the secret, not just what is inside it. Nobody finds The Rock by chance. A person reaches it only when someone who already has access brings them through the tunnel from Central, or when their rank is high enough that it stops being hidden from them.

## the-rock.on-the-surface
Above ground The Rock is nothing much: a carpark serving Central to its north-west, and bare reinforced ground with a few low vents to the south-east.

## the-rock.nine-doors
Nine round metal doors, each several metres across, lie flush with the ground on The Rock's south-east side, ringed in hazard paint gone dull with age. They open straight upward onto cells below, sized for something as large as a Frame.
