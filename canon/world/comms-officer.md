## comms-officer.radio
The Comms Officer is a woman who works the radio off the plotting floor, a voice the party hears more than any other once they go out on operations. She reads out the contacts' designations ("track forty-one") to the party, and knows exactly how out of date the city's picture is, as it comes in off the plotting floor. Beyond that she is deliberately a blank slate: her history, manner and what she wants for herself are open on purpose, to be built through play.

## comms-officer.on-their-side
The Comms Officer is the first adult in the campaign whose instinct is to protect the party, and, unlike the Section Officer, she is on their side. When something unidentified arrives she says she doesn't know what it is and disagrees with the Section Officer's order to hold, because she doesn't want six trainees killed, not because she is protecting a roster. She tries for a better answer and cannot get one quickly. She has no authority to make them obey and knows it, so she gives them everything she has instead of an order. Her one fixed trait is the care.

## comms-officer.how-she-works
She reads every name on the board before a sortie, so she knows theirs before they have met her. Being called by name over a radio by somebody you have never spoken to is her, doing her job. She will put herself outside procedure to protect somebody and take the consequence herself rather than pass it on. Anybody who knows what a correct transmission sounds like can hear her do it.
