## search-ship.not-the-same-wreck
Kortos and Kurose are connected by the prophecy and by the same makers, not by the same wreck: the generation ship at Kortos and the search ship under Kurose are two different ships, a thousand years apart. The city was built on the first dig, which found the search ship's hull underneath, and what the Directorate learned guarding the Kortos site told it what it was standing on when it dug up the hull.
