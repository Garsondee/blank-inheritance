# The Young One
kind: person

## Pencil

## the-young-one.as-the-party-meets-him
The Young One is a Directorate Frame pilot a year or two ahead of the party, a little competitive ("You're Section Nine? Rough. We got harbour patrol."). His name isn't chosen yet. He first appears in the canteen as an unremarkable pilot-track student, who eats in the same room as the party and has an opinion about their assignment. He is an elf. At the canteen he wears the candidate tunic buttoned to the throat exactly as issued, neat hair, a level half-smile: nothing in him hints at what he is. The uniform worn perfectly is a man hiding behind conformity.

## the-young-one.turned-at-a-show
The Young One is Cicada's source inside the Directorate, the fourth member of the Cadre's leadership when masked. He went to one of Kiri's shows, was shown the Watcher undeniably, was given the honest choice the cult always gives, and accepted what he had seen: that is how he was turned, as Nozomi was nearly turned. He is the Nozomi mirror: the same kind of show, the same revelation, opposite choices. She walked out and refused the draft, and the party exists because of her; he stayed inside and kept flying.

## the-young-one.why-he-stayed
The Young One stayed because he was useful where he was, because he told himself he could do more from inside, and because he was frightened. He has never worked out which is the real one.

## the-young-one.intelligence
What Cicada gets from the Young One is intelligence: Directorate movements, rosters, deployments, which sites are watched and when. He isn't muscle. He is why the heists work, and what he passes on may also reach the crime lords, which he doesn't know.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D073, D019 · from: d-cicada:the-four-in-full · known: nobody · told: later

## the-young-one.doctrine-afterwards
The Young One is twenty and untrained for it when a heist goes wrong and he kills someone in panic, unintended. Then he has to become a person who meant to: the doctrine comes afterwards, constructed backwards to survive having done it, the talk about witness needing a real cost. Manako panicked in a lab and built a religion; the Section Officer was hazed and passes it down; this boy panics in a warehouse and builds a belief: nobody has done anything evil, they flinch, and spend a life justifying the flinch.

## the-young-one.wants-permission
The Young One wants permission: somebody to tell him it was all right. Manako won't and can't, and is visibly embarrassed by him. The party might, which is a dangerous thing to put in their hands.

## the-young-one.how-he-fights
The Young One is young, fast, athletic and reckless: pilot-trained, so competent but not a specialist. He closes and commits, and doesn't manage his action economy. He gets more dangerous as he is hurt, not out of conviction but because he has already done the worst thing he will ever do.

## the-young-one.alone
The Young One has no escape: he leaves when he is finished or is dragged off by people who can't stand him. His counter is that he is alone: no Aid, no flanking partner, no covering fire, because the Cadre won't support him. Focus him and he goes down. He is the one who can genuinely be beaten.

The Young One has a Frame, a card held in reserve: if the campaign wants it, the party fights a Claimant and a Directorate Frame on the same field, and the Frame is on the wrong side, piloted by somebody they've had lunch with.

## the-young-one.voice
Masked, the Young One is over-formal: a role he has only seen in his head, slightly too big for him. Out of the mask he is completely ordinary, and the party should recognise the voice before they are told. He quotes Manako slightly wrong, always, the way a fan misquotes a favourite line; Manako has noticed and winces.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D073 · from: d-cicada:the-four-in-full · known: nobody · told: later

## the-young-one.competitive
The Young One is confident, a little competitive and not actually malicious. He eats in the party's canteen and has an opinion about their assignment. He is the campaign's thesis running in a third generation: Manako panicked in a lab, the Section Officer was hazed and passes it down, this boy panics in a warehouse and builds a belief on it.

## the-young-one.the-rescue
At the end of First Contact, if the party is failing, a combat Frame flown by the Young One arrives and saves them, cool and competent. The party likes him, which is what his unmasking will cost.
