## Pencil

## hulda-brack.the-shop-boss
Hulda Brack is head of the machine shop; every repair crew answers to her. She is loud and tough, and the best friend their machines have. The six repair-crew chiefs answer to Brack, so she is the face of the party's requests to their ground crew. Brack is loud, warm, completely without ceremony and unbothered by rank in either direction. She calls everyone by the machine they are responsible for until they have earned a name, tells a trainee exactly what they did wrong in front of everyone, and then stays two hours past her shift helping fix it. She is a nuar woman built like the gantries she works under: tall, heavy through the shoulders, horns she has never once apologised for, arms like girders and a voice that carries the length of the floor.

## hulda-brack.nuar
Hulda is a nuar: bovine head, heavy horns, and so an alien. She refuses to hide or tone down what she is for anyone, and wears no glamour. On the base nobody stares; out in the city, people would.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D071, D039, D044 · from: cb:cast.1.7, cb:calls.2 · known: everyone · told: later

## hulda-brack.machines-first
Brack's one legible want: her Frames and vehicles come back undamaged, and the pilots too, where possible. The ordering is the joke and also not entirely a joke: she has lost people, and keeps from losing more by being uncompromising about the equipment. Nothing leaves her floor she hasn't signed off, she has never signed off something she thought would get someone killed, and she is visibly, loudly furious at a machine that comes back wrecked, never at the pilot.

## hulda-brack.refuses-officers
Brack has refused officers to their faces: if she says a machine is not going out, it isn't, and rank does not move her.

## hulda-brack.useless-is-right
Admitting you are useless with your hands gets more of her attention, not less. It is the people who say they are handy that she watches.

## hulda-brack.no-secrets
Brack is deliberately not a secret-keeper, and has no twist: the party needs one adult with no hidden agenda or the whole institution reads as a trap. She is the Section Officer's exact inverse: he uses the Directorate's machinery against the party, she uses the same machinery for them, and knows which forms matter. She cannot protect them from him and says so plainly.
