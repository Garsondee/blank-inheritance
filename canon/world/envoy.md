## envoy.the-meeting-in-the-street
The Envoy was a greeting: the Watcher's child, shaped like a disfigured doll, walked into a street to a meeting Manako's small, peaceful circle had arranged, and it was shot because a man was afraid. That man was Hori, a police officer alone at a disturbance, who shot it in fear; one of his rounds killed Aoi, Manako's wife.

## envoy.watcher-learned
The Watcher learned one thing from the Envoy: when it shows itself, it is attacked. So it hides, until being seen costs it nothing. The bullets were irrelevant to the swarm: what died was only the body it had built to be polite in. Then it did the only thing a sensor system can do, it found the pattern: manifest, and they attack, the most consistent signal it had ever received from this world. It learned to hide, to feed, and to defend what it makes.

## envoy.two-institutions
Two institutions looked at the Envoy incident, at two different things. The police examined Hori's use of force, and their court heard Aoi's name. The Directorate took over the alien and the scene, and its report, written there on the day when nobody had named her, filed her as one deceased, unidentified: a fringe group's casualty in a public-order incident, not thought worth the paperwork. Nobody went back to correct it, and not one line anywhere says what Manako lost.
