## mrs-okuda.the-chandler
Mrs. Okuda keeps a ship's chandler's stall on the Old Landing waterfront and sells a bubble sextant, a real nautical instrument, for a price that makes recruits laugh.

## mrs-okuda.violet-primer
Mrs. Okuda sells violet marine primer, which sells slowly except to one theatre customer who buys a barrel every fortnight, pays in advance and gives no name.

## mrs-okuda.her-stamp
The Old Landing chandlers' stamp is on the crates in the flood tunnel and on Daichi's lorry, and crates with it leave by lorry for the Container Ground at night.
