## the-works.the-strip
The Works is the strip of shops on the island's waterfront where the Directorate builds its Frames: a drawing office where every part exists on paper first, plate, hydraulic, instrument and paint shops, stores, its own generating hall, its own lorries and its own ambulance. Every shop on the strip exists to feed the Erecting Shop.

## the-works.erecting-shop
The Erecting Shop is the hall where Frames are built: the largest building on the strip, on the water at its southern end, tall enough to stand a Frame upright indoors and open to the water so that plate is landed at the door and finished Frames leave the same way. A Frame stands in a bay for months with its chest open and staging built round it, and the bay is named for it while it does. The Erecting Shop's overhead cranes are the most valuable things in it and are booked weeks ahead, so the crane schedule is the works' real currency. A bay can't move without a crane, and bumping someone's booking costs goodwill that is written down nowhere and remembered by everyone.

## the-works.the-canteen
Everybody who makes a Frame eats at the Works Canteen, seventy metres of tables in three sittings and the one building on the strip where rank is treated as a suggestion. What a pilot is officially told about a job and what the fitters at the next table say about it are not the same account, and the fitters are right.
