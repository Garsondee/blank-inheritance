# The rift at Kortos
kind: event

## rift.opened
About two hundred years ago, on the far side of the world, something opened in the air above the island of Kortos, near Absalom, and would not close.
status: decided · source: D024, D025 · known: everyone · told: start · when: about 200 years ago

## rift.looked
It was a violet light with a black heart.
status: decided · source: D025, S001, D048 · known: everyone · told: start

## rift.grew
It grew on whatever was near it: slowly at first, then faster, the rate itself slowly climbing, in fits and starts that made it deadly to study.
status: decided · source: D025, D048 · known: everyone · told: start

## rift.swallowed
It absorbed many powerful priests, wizards and sorcerers who tried to stop it.
status: decided · source: D025, D048 · known: everyone · told: start

## rift.path
It carved a jagged path across the island towards Absalom, changing direction seemingly at random.
status: decided · source: D025, D048 · known: everyone · told: start

## rift.converged
Adventurers and magic-users converged to contain it, from across the island and then the whole region, among them the finest of Kortos's neighbouring states.
status: decided · source: D025, S001, D048 · known: everyone · told: start

## rift.four-at-the-end
Four people stood in front of it at the end.
status: decided · source: D025, D048 · known: everyone · told: start

## rift.uncertain-ending
It was forced back and crushed into a small space, and either destroyed or changed into something else. Nobody who was there ever confirmed which.
status: decided · source: S001, D048 · known: everyone · told: start

## rift.nobody-knows-how
Nobody agreed on what it was, or why it happened.
status: decided · source: D024 · known: everyone · told: start

## rift.histories-skip-the-start
The histories go from nothing to catastrophe and never say how it began.
status: provisional · source: D027, episode-c1e1--from-up-here · known: everyone · told: start

## rift.entered-absalom
It was stopped only after it had partly entered Absalom, cutting through a temple to Pharasma.
status: decided · source: D025 · known: everyone · told: later

## rift.cause
The four caused it, by accident: they fumbled a container of exotic fuel on the cargo deck of a crashed generation ship at Kortos, and it breached.
status: decided · source: D029, D032, D040, S001 · known: few · told: later

## rift.how-it-was-closed
Sarenrae sent the four back from Nirvana with a way to close it, and it worked because it was one dropped container.
status: decided · source: D040 · known: few · told: later

## rift.the-fumble
How First Impact began: on the generation ship's cargo deck, a clean throw got the container of fuel clear, and the catch failed, two attempts and two natural ones. The container split open. An "exhaling portal" opened, space curved, and a planar breach flung the four founders across the planes to Nirvana, leaving a violet aperture with a black core in the heart of the ship. When they came back, the rift had carved across Kortos into Absalom.

## rift.arrived-late
When the four came back from Nirvana the rupture was much larger, and they swung in to stop it expanding further. That is why they arrived late to their own disaster: they were on another plane while it grew. This is Ingram's working account, marked "presumably".
