## nirvana.luminara
Luminara is the golden city of Nirvana: buildings that shimmer from within, streets of luminescent trees, singing water and glowing gardens, and at its heart a cathedral built of something between stone and light, its great doors always open. Everyone in it radiates calm.

## nirvana.the-relic
Sarenrae gave the four a relic, to carry to the fissure they had come through and use there, with their collective mastery of arcana and occultism, to seal the breach between planes. It would return to the ether once its energy was spent. She would then return them safely to Gauntlight Keep.

## nirvana.the-way-back
The way back lay behind demon-guarded portal locks, which took arcana, occultism and crafting to unpick, and summoned more demons each time a try failed. The four fought through brimoraks, a babau, a succubus, a Balor and an Erinyes. Beyond the last portal the GM had prepared a boss, "the ancient alien god", which would take a random number of turns to awaken if the four arrived injured. The table never reached it.

## nirvana.the-golden-key
To escape an extra-dimensional prison the four had to remember the tale of Auros, the Great Hunter, who assembled a celestial key from the constellations' guardians, from the Heavenly Bull's horn of first dawn to a final arrow of pure light, and unlock the Gates of Dawn. They had to walk the constellations in the order of his journey.

## nirvana.what-it-is
Nirvana is a plane of light, redemption and serenity, where nature rules and the line between physical and spiritual is "as fine as spider's silk". It is where Sarenrae, goddess of the sun, healing, honesty and redemption, receives those who come.

## nirvana.djarvik
In Nirvana a gnoll sorcerer, Djarvik, who had joined the party late, stood before Sarenrae in spectral form, no longer a totem but as he once was, and she charged Hyuck and him with redemption and reconciliation over their shared past.
