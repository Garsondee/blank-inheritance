## the-administrative-block.the-ladder
The Administrative Block in Island Centre stacks the Directorate's ranks in one six-storey building: typewriters and telephones at the bottom, offices in the middle, and at the top a corridor where the floor stops being lino and becomes carpet. Each landing is quieter than the one below. The Section Officer works there, and so does the man two rungs above him. Candidates never go to the top floor, so being summoned there is the event before a word is said. People are left to wait on a bench outside a closed door, and the Section Officer waits on it too.

## the-administrative-block.decided-upstairs
What is decided on the top floor arrives downstairs as a duty change nobody explains, and is read on the muster yard's board before anyone says it was about them.

## the-administrative-block.the-establishment
The Establishment Offices, five storeys beside the Administrative Block and subordinate to it, hold the establishment: the list of who exists to the Directorate. Every candidate, every posting and every screening result is filed there by somebody who has never met them, so a person's file has a real address.
