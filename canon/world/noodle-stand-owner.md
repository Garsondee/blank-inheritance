## Pencil

## noodle-stand-owner.the-late-stand
A woman works a late stand by the harbour that stays open long after everything else has shut, which makes it where Directorate staff end up when they are off duty and don't want to be on the base. She knows most of them by face and none by rank. She is the proof that there is an outside: a place within walking distance where nobody is anybody's section, where a trainee and a works manager queue in the same line. A party who finds her should be able to come back. She is the campaign's only civilian fixture the Directorate does not own.

## noodle-stand-owner.repeats-nothing
She hears everything said at the stand and repeats none of it, deliberately and as a matter of business, and gives anyone who asks the same flat non-answer. She is not a source and not a hook, and the stand is not a place to farm overheard conversation.
