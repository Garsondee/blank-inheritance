## generation-ship.buried-and-inside
The generation ship lies in a hidden, jungle-choked valley on Kortos, buried and half-swallowed by volcanic eruptions and earthquakes. All that shows is a black obelisk on a rocky promontory, "like a foreboding finger of a long dead god". The four founders took it for a long-overgrown temple. Inside the ship are two reactors, two mitflit quarters, an elevator, the bridge, the captain's and first officer's quarters, a reasoning-core chamber ("a space for briefings and education") and a cargo deck. A computer at the entrance asks a visitor the questions a star-faring people would ask: geometry, gravity, the letters of life, the shells of the elements, the golden ratio.

## generation-ship.opened
First Impact opened up the inner layers of the generation ship, and laid its godlike technology open to the sky for the first time in tens of thousands of years. Scientists and magic-users descended on the site, terrified of another rupture; adventurers descended on it looking for valuables; it became a very dangerous place. The Directorate holds the generation ship's crash site at Kortos, and keeps its technology highly restricted from the rest of Golarion, so that it can't destabilise the world.

## generation-ship.the-way-in
To reach the ship, the four crossed jungle with dinosaurs, quicksand and a titanic flytrap, climbed a slick limestone cliff of rounded holds and vines, and came to a black obelisk at its crown. A computer at its entrance let them in only when they answered riddles of geometry, gravity, the letters of life, the electron shells and the golden ratio.
