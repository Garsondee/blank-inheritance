## caretaker.ume
The Caretaker is Ume, whose name is the plum blossom, the flower that opens in winter before anything else. She runs school welfare and is one of the Directorate's three deliberately unstable poles: she tries, misguidedly, to give the kids something like a normal adolescence. Every accommodation the kids win, every kindness she wins, costs technician-days from the same pool that repairs and upgrades Frames, so kindness has a visible, charged line item.

## caretaker.grief-for-nozomi
Ume was closest to Nozomi through school welfare, and lost her. She carries a private grief and wants Nozomi found as badly as the party does, which makes her the natural ally on that thread and gives her something of her own.
