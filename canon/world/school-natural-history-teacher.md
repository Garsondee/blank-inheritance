## school-natural-history-teacher.collector
The natural history teacher teaches what lives in, on and under the island: the drains, the tide-line, the flooded levels below the old works and the sea around them. A collector: her room is full of trays, jars and pinned things, almost all caught within a mile of where it sits. She is enormously, unguardedly enthusiastic about animals most people would rather not think about. She is the only teacher in the building who will happily tell a class she doesn't know what something is. Her register is the opposite of the history teacher's: she teaches what she has personally caught and is delighted by the gaps. A student who wants a true answer gets further with her, and the campaign shouldn't make that a secret.

## school-natural-history-teacher.the-specimen
She keeps in the cabinet a specimen she cannot classify, and says so cheerfully. It is a plant and nothing more: it is never explained in Episode One or Two, and isn't connected to Frame technology or First Impact. If a player connects it, that is theirs.
