# The stray
kind: thing

## Pencil

## stray.what-it-is
The stray is a piece of the Watcher that the players can befriend, or keep as a mascot, while it is really a piece of the thing threatening the whole city. It is a microphone: the Watcher hears what is said near it.

## stray.where-it-comes-from
After a fight in Chapter 5, one crumb of what they beat doesn't drain away with the rest. It follows one of them back to their quarters and waits to be fed. The player names it.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D071, D042 · from: cb:ch05.table.4, cb:gaps.12, cb:still-yours.10 · known: few · told: in:chapter-05

## stray.raised
If the party feeds it, it grows, shaped by whoever raises it, and later it is the proof that the Watcher can be raised differently. The stray can still become the demonstration that breaks Manako's faith: a piece of the same swarm, raised by somebody else, visibly not what he made. If the party doesn't feed it, it stays small, and hungry.
