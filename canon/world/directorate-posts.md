# The Directorate's posts
kind: group
lane: directorate

The people who hold the island's working posts, mostly unnamed: what each runs and what they are in a position to know. None is a character yet; names and manner are left to be built through play.

## Pencil

## directorate-posts.canteen-manager
Manager, The Works Canteen: Feeds the entire works strip in three sittings and knows, from who sits with whom, roughly everything about how the work is really going. Treats rank as a suggestion and pilots as young people who need feeding.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D075 · from: npc:canteen-manager · known: some · told: later

## directorate-posts.chief-draughtsman
Chief Draughtsman, The Drawing Office: Runs the hundred metres of drawing boards where every part the works makes exists on paper before it exists in steel. Holds, more completely than anyone, the answer to how a Frame is actually put together.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D075 · from: npc:chief-draughtsman · known: some · told: later

## directorate-posts.deep-storage-custodian
Custodian, Deep Storage: Runs the day-to-day of Deep Storage: decides what still counts as 'currently being used', signs off on requisitions, and is the one person who actually knows which underground floors are real storage and which are a number on a door that was never built out behind it. Answers, eventually, to whoever has the authority to decide what Deep Storage is allowed to hold, in practice the Commander's pole.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D075 · from: npc:deep-storage-custodian · known: some · told: later

## directorate-posts.generating-hall-engineer
Duty Engineer, The Generating Hall: Keeps the works in power, four sets, three running, one always stripped down. The person who decides which shop loses its supply first when the load will not stretch.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D075 · from: npc:generating-hall-engineer · known: some · told: later

## directorate-posts.goods-inwards-officer
Goods Inwards Officer, The Bonded Store: Signs for everything that comes onto the island under seal, and decides what goes to the Quarantine Shed when the manifest and the contents disagree. Wholly uninterested in who is asking.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D075 · from: npc:goods-inwards-officer · known: some · told: later

## directorate-posts.hospital-chief-of-staff
Chief of Staff, The General Hospital: Runs The General Hospital day to day, wards, staff, and who is actually allowed to see whom. The hospital nominally answers to Shucks Mordant as the Directorate's Chief Medical Officer, but he is never there in person; this is the person a party actually deals with.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D075 · from: npc:hospital-chief-of-staff · known: some · told: later

## directorate-posts.landing-end-duty-nco
Duty NCO, The Landing End: Whoever is standing duty NCO for the Landing End on a given watch, covers the maintenance log, the drain-cover checks after a high tide, and whatever else turns up on a quiet night shift that technically isn't in anyone's job description.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D075 · from: npc:landing-end-duty-nco · known: some · told: later

## directorate-posts.plot-room-supervisor
Supervisor, The Plot Room: Runs the plotting floor: the table, the markers, the headsets and the people wearing them. Responsible for how out of date the city's picture of itself is at any given moment, and acutely aware of it.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D075 · from: npc:plot-room-supervisor · known: some · told: later

## directorate-posts.range-safety-officer
Range Safety Officer, The Proving Ground: Runs live-fire and manoeuvre qualification at The Proving Ground, the Frame- and vehicle-scale range east of the Gatehouse, not to be confused with The Butts (personnel small arms). Calls the line hot or safe from Range Control, and nothing fires without that call. The one person on the base whose 'no' on a Frame or a pilot is not really arguable in the moment, whatever the reason turns out to be.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D075 · from: npc:range-safety-officer · known: some · told: later

## directorate-posts.range-safety-officer-his-call-is-not-arguable
The Range Safety Officer's call on a machine or a pilot is final. Arguing with it is the single thing that will get a trainee pulled off the ground, and it is not personal when it happens. He will explain his criteria in full, patiently, to anybody who asks before their run. Nobody ever asks before, and he has noticed.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D075, D081 · from: npc:range-safety-officer · known: nobody · told: later

## directorate-posts.registry-clerk
Registry Clerk, The Registry: Works the records floor and the print room where every Directorate document is typed once per clearance level. Has read every version of everything and holds no clearance rung worth the name, an institutional absurdity nobody above them has ever noticed.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D075 · from: npc:registry-clerk · known: some · told: later

## directorate-posts.switchboard-supervisor
Switchboard Supervisor, The Telephone Exchange: Runs the island's switchboard. Every call between any two parts of the Directorate is physically connected by one of their operators, which makes this small team the best-informed people on the island almost by accident.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D075 · from: npc:switchboard-supervisor · known: some · told: later

## directorate-posts.time-office-clerk
Clerk, The Time Office: Keeps the clocking-in cards for the whole strip. Holds, without anyone thinking of it as records, a minute-by-minute account of where several thousand people were on any given day.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D075 · from: npc:time-office-clerk · known: some · told: later

## directorate-posts.the-rock-research-director
Research Director, The Rock: Runs the laboratory side of The Rock, what is actually being studied, and why it needs a prison next to it rather than an ordinary research floor. What that research is remains an open question by design; this NPC is the person who knows the answer, not a vehicle for deciding it here.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D075 · from: npc:the-rock-research-director · known: some · told: later

## directorate-posts.the-rock-warden
Warden, The Rock: Runs the prison side of The Rock, who is held in which of the nine cells, on whose authority, and under what conditions. Deliberately not one of the Directorate's three visible poles (Commander/Caretaker/Transparency Advocate): The Rock is classified Omega-Zero specifically because most Directorate staff don't know it exists, and giving it a publicly-known face would undercut that.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D075 · from: npc:the-rock-warden · known: some · told: later

## directorate-posts.vermilion-block-custodian
Reagent Stores Custodian, The Vermilion Block: Signs for every delivery into the six fenced silos north-west of the Block and every draw back out of them, the one paper trail that actually exists for what has come in from across Golarion in bulk. Not a spellcaster, by design: a custodian who could use what they store would be a security problem the Directorate would rather not create. Knows exactly how much is in each silo and exactly nothing about what any of it is eventually used for, which is precisely the arrangement their superiors want.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D075 · from: npc:vermilion-block-custodian · known: some · told: later

## directorate-posts.vermilion-block-director
Officer in Charge, The Vermilion Block: Runs the Directorate's own magic-using department out of the black-and-red building on Island Centre marked with a red diamond hazard placard. Extremely high proficiency as a spellcaster themself, and the one person on staff whose sign-off actually means something when a reagent shipment, a test, or a request for magical support from another department needs approving. Most staff know the Block exists; almost none of them know this person's day-to-day work, only that requests go through them and answers come back slowly, if at all.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D075 · from: npc:vermilion-block-director · known: some · told: later

## directorate-posts.works-manager
Works Manager, the south-west works: Runs the whole south-western works strip, the Erecting Shop, the Armour Shop and everything feeding them. Decides, in practice, what actually gets built this month and in which bay, which makes this the person whose goodwill determines whether a Frame is ready when it is needed.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D075 · from: npc:works-manager · known: some · told: later

## directorate-posts.school-registrar
Registrar, Main School Complex: Runs placement testing and academic streaming for every candidate who passes through the Main School Complex, scores the tests, keeps the real archive on the second floor, and decides which section a student lands in. Sees the raw data behind every candidate's placement, which is more than almost anyone else on the island sees in one place, and has never been told why the Directorate wants it kept.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D075 · from: npc:school-registrar · known: some · told: later

## directorate-posts.school-civil-defence-instructor
Civil defence instructor, Main School Complex: Teaches shelter procedure, muster points and the four alert states, and has done for eleven years. A serving NCO rather than a teacher, which shows: she drills rather than explains, makes people repeat things back, and is entirely unhurried about doing it again. Nothing she teaches has ever been used, not once in a hundred and fifty years, and she will say so herself without it denting her conviction in the slightest. What she actually wants is not in the lesson. She has applied four times to have the alert-state drill reinstated as a real base-wide exercise rather than a classroom subject, and been refused four times, on cost. Her class is the only place the material still gets said out loud by anybody, and she knows it.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D075 · from: npc:school-civil-defence-instructor · known: some · told: later

## directorate-posts.intake-medicals-clerk
Records clerk, Intake Medicals: Runs Records at the Intake Medicals counter on the combined campus: flat, patient, entirely without irony, and has already explained today's form to more candidates than she's counting. Wants exactly one thing from each of them, the form filled in correctly, and has no other ambitions today. Hands out Form 12-B, whose box four and box nineteen both ask for a mother's maiden name, one copy for Records and one for Welfare, because the two departments haven't spoken since the flood; she isn't embarrassed by this, and explains it the same way every time. Answers the question she was asked and then stops talking. Short and solidly built behind the counter, sleeves rolled to the elbow; nothing about her reads as anything but a career civil servant who has been doing this exact job for a long time. She is a dwarf.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D075 · from: npc:intake-medicals-clerk · known: some · told: later

## directorate-posts.repair-chiefs
Each of the six pilots has the head of their own standing repair crew: six lesser people who may grow into bigger, more interesting roles later. The crews also maintain the pilot's vehicle alongside the Frame, competing for the same technician-day allocation, and they answer to Hulda Brack. Names, personalities and how each relates to their pilot are open.
