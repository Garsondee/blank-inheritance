## deputy-director.reachable
The Deputy Director is intimidating at first, genuinely so in a room, then plainly a good and wise leader. He sits directly beneath the Directorate's own top authority rather than being it, which is what makes him reachable: a person who can be stood in front of, argued with, and surprised.

## deputy-director.manner
The Deputy Director apologises for trivia, a shortage of chairs or a wasted afternoon, which is how you can tell that the things he does not apologise for are deliberate. He will tell somebody they were wrong, that he would give the same order again, and that he is glad they are alive, and mean all three, softening none to make the others easier. He hears the full account before deciding anything, including the parts that make his organisation look bad. Still, completely, a Directorate officer: whatever he concludes privately, the institution's position is the one that leaves the room. He answers a straight question straight, including questions he is under no obligation to answer. Almost nobody asks him one, which he finds more disappointing than rudeness.
