## nozomi.the-interview
Nozomi is detained and being questioned. At a Cicada show she was told she was working for the wrong side, and the next day she turned off her beeper and stopped answering. She tells the party she saw something at a show and couldn't get back in the machine. She gives them Cicada's argument, from someone they trust, before they have any answer to it.

## nozomi.the-show-and-the-refusal
Nozomi was slated as the presumptive first pilot: the seat the party's own pilot ends up filling instead. Weeks before intake she went to what she thought was an ordinary private illusionist show. It was a Cicada recruitment drive: the performers revealed the Watcher filling the room, then demonstrated, undeniably and not as illusion, that it was real. She was shown something true and given an honest choice by people who didn't lie to her. She stayed to hear them out, and a high-level cultist told her, specifically and personally, that she was working for the wrong side: a targeted statement aimed at a presumptive Frame pilot. The next day she declined: she turned off her beeper and phone and avoided being drafted to fight. No violence, no scene, no confrontation on record: just absence.

## nozomi.how-to-play-her
Nozomi is not a traitor, a villain or a coward. She thought it through under real pressure and made a considered decision; she did not panic and was not tricked. She is tired of repeating herself to the wrong kind of asking, not unwilling to talk. A conversation that doesn't ruffle her feathers gets somewhere; one that pushes or treats her like a suspect makes her go quiet, and may burn the chance for anyone else to try. A real, failable social challenge with a genuine cost.

## nozomi.a-fragment-only
What the party can overhear of her search in the intake queue is a fragment, even on a critical success: it makes a sharp player wonder, and is never a resolvable lead. A later beat may not build directly off it without a real, separate scene making it actionable. Her absence is the reason the party exists, not a mystery to be solved in Chapter One.
