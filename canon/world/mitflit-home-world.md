## mitflit-home-world.who-fells-ships
Why ships fall on Golarion is not decided. Ingram's working hypothesis, marked "perhaps": something about Golarion both draws the mitflits and is deadly to exploring ships, perhaps its gods, jealous of their domain, who foresaw that mitflits landing and spreading freely would change the world in ways they didn't want. The mitflits weren't conquerors: they saw a place to live, perhaps without understanding gods or a territorial domain at all. Open with it: which gods, how they bring ships down, and whether anyone in the campaign ever learns it.
