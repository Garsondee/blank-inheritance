## hideo-tanabe.the-conductor
Mr. Hideo Tanabe is the conductor on the tram down from the Upper Wards: he calls every stop in a sing-song, knows half his passengers by name, and reads a worn book of poems between stops.

## hideo-tanabe.the-marks-were-here
Tanabe says the violet marks were on his tram line before he started working it, and he tells passengers not to scrape a cicada emblem because the city thinks them lucky.
