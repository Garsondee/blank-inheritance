## kudo.the-lawyer

## kudo.the-speech
Kudo's voice, as a speech to a Directorate information officer in public: "My daughter was standing eleven feet from a canister. She has been in a cell for four months. She is not charged. She is not ill. Your own physicians say she is not ill. So I'll put it plainly, and you can write it down. What do you think is wrong with her? Because you're all behaving as though you know, and none of you will say it, and she is seventeen years old and she is very frightened." A party that engages him well in that scene earns her name properly, spoken as a person rather than a case file.
