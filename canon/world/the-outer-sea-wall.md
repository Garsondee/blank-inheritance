## the-outer-sea-wall.the-tide-decides
The Outer Sea Wall is the island's seaward defence on its north-east side, concrete armoured with rock, out past the Long Sheds and about twenty minutes' walk from the Muster Yard. The walk out is under water except for about four hours either side of low water, so work there is set by the tide table, not the duty board.

## the-outer-sea-wall.the-weed-line
A dead-level edge of weed at head height runs the length of the wall. Below it everything is black, wet and covered in things that shut when a shadow crosses them; above it is dry concrete. Anything left below the line is gone by the next low water.

## the-outer-sea-wall.nobody-comes
Nobody goes to the sea wall: it is the far side of the island from everything, and for four hours twice a day nobody will see who is there.
