## mitflits.wild
The mitflits of Golarion are small, goblin-like, cute and a nuisance: the "wild" mitflits, the only kind on Golarion, living around the city and the hangars. They have never been hunted, bountied, exterminated or treated as vermin at any point in this world's history; at worst they are a nuisance, and thought rude.

## mitflits.look
Wild mitflits are blue-skinned elfin goblin people, quite goblinoid, but cute rather than gruesome, with bright, intelligent eyes. High mitflits look like high-fantasy elves with blue skin: elegant, androgynous, unblemished and aloof, plainly a star-faring people. Nobody on Golarion looks like that now.

## mitflits.degraded
The generation ship's crew were alien and robotic. Over the ages since the crash their descendants dispersed and forgot their skills, and became weaker, more primitive and more violent: the real reason they are thought "the least of races". Small communities of mitflits survived and kept alive the old ways and the older memories, and that surviving thread of memory is what makes a return home possible. They are wild mitflits like the rest: what they kept is memory, not looks.

## mitflits.helpful
Mitflits have a horrible characteristic: they are extremely helpful when given the right tasks, and fascinated by the production of technology. It is what makes them so easy to use. It is a people's virtue that others exploit, never servility.

## mitflits.rude
Mitflits annoy people the way a rude goblinoid with little respect for authority would in a 1950s Japanese city: they don't keep the same traditions, and they are weird outsiders. They skip the forms, ignore seniority and address people wrongly. The offence is social, never criminal.

## mitflits.gauntlight
Two hundred years ago the four founders befriended a band of mitflits whom everyone else treated as a nuisance or as savants to be owned, gave them a home in Gauntlight Keep, and helped them build a government, with Anabala in charge.

## mitflits.gauntlight-council
At Gauntlight Keep the mitflits took charge in a spirited first meeting; once the rules of meetings were explained they took to them with alacrity, and when they learned they could "vote" by ayes and nays it became bedlam, but the shape of a society started to develop. The council's leaders were Comhphas, Scrawng and Anabala, in a triumvirate, and a busy mitflit marketplace grew in the keep under Miriq the Magnificent.

## mitflits.freedom
Freedom is the mitflits' defining characteristic, and it applies internally: they will never vote as a bloc. Their history is brilliant minds with no social armour, talked into inventing weapons for a greedy lord who grew rich on it, and their split is generational. The elders remember the lord's workshop and know exactly what happens when someone finds their minds useful, so they refuse, and go to extremes for pacifism. The young do not remember: they see a magnificent machine and a city that needs them, and volunteer eagerly, walking into the identical trap wearing a uniform.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D078, D081 · from: v4:9-the-mitflits · known: some · told: later

## mitflits.wont-explain
Mitflits will not explain themselves. Discussing mitflit technology falls under the same social taboo the city applies to magic: wise to conceal, vulgar to flaunt. Pressed, Bobbin will note, without drama, that mitflit technology is many times more powerful than terrestrial magic, because magic is slowly draining from Golarion.
