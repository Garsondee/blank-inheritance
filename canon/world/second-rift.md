# The second rift
kind: event

## Pencil

## second-rift.in-their-time
The promise comes due in the party's time: about three-quarters of the way through the campaign a second rift opens over Kurose, and this time it can't be stopped from here. Stopping it needs the mitflit home world, so the last part of the campaign is a journey by starship.

## second-rift.cause
The second rift is a weapon the Watcher builds from exotic matter, without understanding what it can do.
status: sketch · by: ingram · source: D040, D070 · known: nobody · told: later

## second-rift.can-build
The Watcher is made of its makers' own nanomachines, so it can build what they built, and it builds its weapon from exotic matter without understanding that it could destroy the Watcher itself, the city, Golarion and a stretch of space around it.

## second-rift.the-violet-knot
Through a lens, something new takes shape in the sky over the dig: the eyes folding inward around a violet glow. The founders know that violet: it's what opened the rift at Kortos.

## second-rift.gap-is-after
The Gap is an event that happens after the adventure, perhaps as a result of it. It is not the Second Impact, and it is not a past span of history: stock Starfinder lines that treat the Gap as lost history don't apply here.
