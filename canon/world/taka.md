## taka.a-year-ahead
Taka is a trainee a year ahead of the party who flies a Gen 1. He sits with them at the canteen, sends them off to the drains, and turns up at six in the morning before their first machine to tell them the one true thing nobody official will.

## taka.claims-the-table
At the canteen on the first evening Taka arrives uninvited and claims all six of them as his table, instantly and totally, before the plates are cleared, as settled fact rather than an offer. He seizes on whatever they actually said or did; the warmth is inclusion, never labelling. He gives no specific nicknames: they would presume what six characters who didn't yet exist would be like.

## taka.warmth-and-competence
Taka is warm because it costs him nothing, which is its own tell: he hasn't yet been ground down by the institution the way Shucks and Nobu have. He isn't a third variation on kindness-with-a-cost; if a hidden cost is ever added, that is a deliberate decision. Taka's competence surprises rather than announces itself. Asked about the drill, he gives a beat of silence, something about hydraulic compensation, and a change of subject: the first sign there is real substance under the personality. The goofiness is played completely straight until then, with no foreshadowing winks.

## taka.what-he-knows
A year further in than they are, Taka knows it in his hands, not his vocabulary: what a hangar is like at seven in the morning, what a range officer is really watching for, that everybody over-grips the controls the first time. He remembers nobody telling him any of it, so he gives advice he wishes he'd had, early and unasked. He briefs the Frame drill the next morning, with "mostly stories of previous years going wrong".
