## the-soldier.the-squad
The Soldier is the disciplined leader of an armed Cadre squad. His people use non-lethal force, won't fire on anyone young, withdraw by the book, and won't finish off anyone who's down.

## the-soldier.at-the-envoy-incident
The Soldier is a former Directorate instructor who was a young officer on the Directorate cordon at the Envoy incident. He helped secure the scene and helped file the report that recorded Aoi as one deceased, unidentified, along with the official line that someone had magically summoned a monster, which was what he thought he had seen. Hori fired in a street, and the Soldier filed it: neither knows the other carries the same day. He never knew Aoi's name: nobody told him and he never asked, and that is the part he can't put down. Years later somebody took him to a show, and he saw what had stood in that street, and understood what he had helped bury. Her name was spoken at the police's court proceeding, which he had no part in.

## the-soldier.serving-as-penance
The Soldier serves the man whose life he helped ruin as unconfessed penance. He wants to be caught, though not consciously and he'd deny it: he keeps letting the party live, keeps talking instead of shooting, keeps turning up where they can find him.

## the-soldier.how-he-fights
The Soldier fights like a professional, which in SF2E means playing the system properly: suppressive fire and area denial, smoke and flashbangs rather than frags, grapples, disarms, cover, Aid for his own people, and disengaging the moment the maths turns. He is the enemy who teaches the party how the game is played, and being knocked unconscious by him in a flooded drain is a real, frightening loss though nobody dies. His escape is Withdraw by the Book: announced out loud, executed on a covered route he picked before the fight, by the doctrine the Directorate taught him. The institution's own doctrine, used against it.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D073, D081 · from: d-cicada:the-four-in-full, d-cicada:the-four-who-actually-turn-up · known: nobody · told: later

## the-soldier.never-kills
Every Strike the Soldier makes against a party member is nonlethal: he can drop them and never tries for more. They work it out after the first fight, when they notice nobody was dying.

## the-soldier.wont-fire-on-children
The Soldier's counter: he will not fire on children. The party can walk straight at him and it works, and it should feel bad. It works only while he still believes they are children. It is his code, and what eventually costs him.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D073 · from: d-cicada:the-four-in-full, d-cicada:the-four-who-actually-turn-up · known: nobody · told: later

## the-soldier.voice
The Soldier is instructional, because he was an instructor and can't stop: he critiques the party's tactics mid-fight, helpfully, and calls them "Section Nine", which the party should notice and nobody should explain. He is sincerely recruiting them throughout, with no trick in it. When they get it wrong he says "Again", which is also what he says before making them do it again.
