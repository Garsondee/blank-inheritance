# The Section Officer
kind: person

## section-officer.vetch
Section Officer Vetch runs Section Nine. He is never wrong in his own account.

## section-officer.the-docket
On their first afternoon, Vetch sends Section Nine into the city with a stores docket for COMPOUND, LEVELLING, BUBBLE, ONE (1): the bubble in a spirit level.

## section-officer.hazing
Vetch sends every new section on a fool's errand because it was done to him, and he believes it builds character. The same was done to Manako, decades ago.
status: decided · source: D018 · known: few · told: later

## section-officer.the-drains
At supper on the first day, Vetch reads Section Nine the night's worst job in front of the hall: storm drain clearance under the old town, from Dock C at twenty-one hundred.
status: provisional · source: episode-c1e1--canteen · known: some · told: in:episode-c1e1--canteen

## section-officer.drains-for-the-vial
Vetch gives Section Nine the drains because of the vial requisition, or, if they came back empty-handed, for the laugh. He never says so.

## section-officer.the-job
At Dock C, Vetch gives them the job in one line: power to the drainage line, clear the compactor feed, report to him when it runs. He waits for them at the top.
