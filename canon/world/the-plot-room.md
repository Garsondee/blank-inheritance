## the-plot-room.what-it-is
The Plot Room is the operations floor for the whole city: a table the size of a room with the approaches drawn on it to scale, where everything reported (a sighting, a siren, a unit moving) is pushed into place by hand with a rake. A gallery above lets the people deciding see the whole picture at once, and teleprinters and the switchboard feed it all the time. It is warm from the lamps, and a dozen people with headsets talk quietly and constantly. The table always shows what was true a few minutes ago, and everyone in the building knows precisely by how much. Someone who asks how old a marker is decides better than someone who takes the table as the truth.
