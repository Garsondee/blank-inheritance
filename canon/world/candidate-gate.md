# The Candidate Gate

## Pencil

## candidate-gate.signs-in-and-out
The Candidate Gate is the single staffed checkpoint at the housing rows' only road entrance. Every candidate signs in and out there, badge checked against a roster, curfew logged. The gate's ledger is the one honest record of who was outside the rows and when: any night out becomes a line in a book somebody can later read, and a breach logged there can't be talked away.

## candidate-gate.paperwork-and-proctors
At the gate the roster is always ahead of the person: candidates are expected before they arrive and logged before they speak, and the proctor reads a name off the roster before it is given. Nobody there is watching anybody; the Directorate is simply better at paperwork than at people. The off-duty proctors spend most of their shift under the gate's weather-beaten awning talking rather than checking anyone. They are bored, they are not officers, and they know the housing rows' gossip better than anyone with a rank.

## candidate-gate.the-corrected-figure
On the board screwed to the barrier post, somebody once corrected the second curfew figure by hand, and the board was never reprinted: the Directorate doesn't repaint signs. It means nothing more.
