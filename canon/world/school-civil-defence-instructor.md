# The civil defence instructor
kind: person
lane: directorate

## Pencil

## school-civil-defence-instructor.drills
The civil defence instructor teaches shelter procedure, muster points and the four alert states, and has for eleven years. A serving NCO rather than a teacher: she drills rather than explains, makes people repeat things back, and is unhurried about doing it again. Nothing she teaches has ever been used in a hundred and fifty years, and she says so herself without it denting her conviction.

## school-civil-defence-instructor.refused-four-times
She has applied four times to have the alert-state drill reinstated as a real base-wide exercise and been refused four times, on cost: a budget decision by people who are not wrong that nothing has ever happened, not a conspiracy. Her class is the only place the material still gets said aloud.

## school-civil-defence-instructor.pays-off
She pays off twice: the siren test in the training exercise is the closest thing to the drill she has been asking for, and in the first real contact a player who learned from her knows a real alert from a test. Neither scene remarks on her.
