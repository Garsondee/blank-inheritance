## vermilion-block.the-magic-people
The Vermilion Block is the Directorate's own department of magic users, where its most powerful spellcasting staff gather. Everyone on the island knows the Block exists and what it is called, and will say it's "the magic people" without hesitating; almost nobody knows what happens inside it, and the silos are need-to-know only. Anyone posted there gets the same two-line briefing: sign in at the door, and don't ask what's in the silos. The Block's staff are trained to keep their magic as quiet and unremarkable as the black paint on its walls, and a spellcaster posted there is told so directly.

## vermilion-block.how-it-looks
Nothing else on the island looks like it: black walls, red trim, windows narrower than they need to be, and on the roof a red diamond, the colour of a warning light left permanently on.

## vermilion-block.the-silos
North-west of the Block, behind a second, tighter barbed-wire fence, stand six red metal silos, three large and three small, each stencilled with a stock number and nothing else. They hold magical reagents brought in bulk from across Golarion. The custodian who signs for the silo deliveries is no spellcaster and has no idea what the reagents are for. The director, the one sign-off there that means anything, is the only person on site who might.

## vermilion-block.silos-came-later
The silos came after the building: staff who have been there long enough remember when there were only two. The Block itself was built when the Directorate's magic users outgrew whatever informal arrangement housed them before.

## vermilion-block.just-a-department
The Block is a plain Directorate department doing Directorate work. Its mystery is what a government department is quietly stockpiling, and why; it has nothing to do with the Watcher, the magic shows or the Kortos rift.
