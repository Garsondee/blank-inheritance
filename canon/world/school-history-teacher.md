## school-history-teacher.taught-straight
The history teacher gives the one class at the school where a student comes out having learned something true about how the island got here: the closest thing the school has to an official version, taught straight rather than as propaganda. Whether he knows more than the official version, and says less than he knows, is a GM call. He says "I don't know" plainly, with no embarrassment, and moves on. It is the most useful thing about him and the reason a question is worth asking him.

## school-history-teacher.two-teachers
Local studies is his own subject; the history syllabus is one he was handed. He teaches an approved text straight and does not know it has been edited. He is a different teacher in each room and doesn't know that he is.
