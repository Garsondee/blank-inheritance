# The Mayor of Kurose
kind: person

## Notes
A new character for the plot, whom the party can meet (D068). Ingram's brief: the mayor "can act as both a blessing and a curse for the group, someone who is their own agent and who protects the city and their own position equally." Not yet decided: how the mayor holds office, where and when the party first meets them, and what they ask of the party. Nothing is placed in the plan.

## mayor.crime-initiative
The mayor brings the party in as a sort of magical armed-response team, as part of an initiative to use the Directorate's resources to fix the rampant organised crime in Kurose. The party won't know it is the mayor for a long time.
