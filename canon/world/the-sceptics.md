## the-sceptics.who-and-what-they-concluded
The sceptics are the minority at Manako's conference who did not accept the mass-hallucination explanation. They investigated privately for decades and stayed rational: no worship, no horror. They reached the conclusion before anyone else, and nobody believed it: semi-sentient, not hostile, not a god, benign and malleable. That is right about the Watcher's early state, which is what they studied. It has since become the campaign's adversary, with an objective of its own that has no limit.
