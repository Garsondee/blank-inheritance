# The night regulars
kind: group
lane: city

The people the party meets on its nights off, in the night rests, the Magic Show, the hospital and the canteen: the old notes' physical descriptions and ancestries, which the scenes' cast cards don't carry.

## Pencil

## night-regulars.aucourt
Basile Aucourt (Kalo): Long-stay patient, Ward Four, the General Hospital, a harbour pilot from Taldor, on the far side of the world. A long-stay patient with a leg in traction, in a contraption of pulleys he complains about constantly. Courtly, theatrical and a little too grand, in a Minkai he learned over ships' radios. Calls the ward 'the ship', the nurse 'the captain', and the other patients 'passengers', his opening line to anyone new is 'Ah, new passengers. Welcome aboard. The captain is at the far end, and she runs a very tight ship.' He tells the time by the tide rather than the clock, and still wakes for the four o'clock high water out of thirty years' habit, even though the rest of him can't act on it.

## night-regulars.chiyo
Chiyo Nakamura (Human): Second-term candidate from a fishing village, has never won a hand. A second-year candidate from a fishing village up the coast, with round cheeks, a scrap of blue cloth tying back her hair and her sleeves rolled to the elbow, who has not yet won a single hand of Last Ferry. She smiles the moment she picks up a good hand, knows she is doing it, and cannot stop; she counts her chores on her fingers. Warm and bright and a little breathless, and she apologises when she wins an argument.

The Oddwater Company's stagehand, doorman and lantern-keeper: a broad young man in a canvas apron with a cap pulled low, who puts a hand on something solid before he starts any job, as if the floor might argue. He calls Tamsin 'the boss' and never by her name. Short and dry, with a laugh he tries to keep in.

## night-regulars.faruq
Faruq Sallah (Pahtra): Candidate from Katapesh, harmonica player. A quiet young pahtra from Katapesh, far across the sea: short tawny fur, tall ears, a candidate's tunic worn under a maroon wool scarf that is the loudest thing on the roof. He plays the harmonica and says very little, tapping it against his palm before he plays, and answering a question he dislikes with two notes, going down. Soft, few words, very dry.

## night-regulars.gennai
Gennai: Night porter, the General Hospital. The night porter, pushing the tea trolley down Ward Four squeaking on its wheels, as he has every night for longer than anyone remembers. Pours for whoever is awake, and talks while he pours. Old, long-serving, quietly one of the better-informed people on the island without meaning to be, porters and nurses hear what the hospital is told to make ready for before anyone else does, and Gennai in particular knows how many beds and when, though never what for.

## night-regulars.genzo
Genzō Amemiya (Ysoki): Night observer, the Met Office. The night observer of the Met Office: a small, grey-whiskered ysoki in an oilskin coat and sou'wester who has kept the night watch for twenty years and speaks to his instruments in the same gentle voice he uses on people. He marks each hour by knocking it out on the brass rail with one knuckle, one knock for each hour, and only then says the number, and he taps the glass of the barometer and says good evening to it. Old-fashioned, gentle and precise, with a sailor's habits.

## night-regulars.grenna
Grenna Hollowmark (Dwarf): Dwarf candidate in the next room, who snores. A short, broad, ruddy first-term candidate with two thick braids and a square jaw, who lives in the next room and snores through the plywood wall like a sawmill, and whom everyone forgives. She raps her knuckles on any piece of wood before she trusts it, and handles anything broken like a patient: gently, and talking to it quietly. Cheerful, practical, and louder than she means to be.

## night-regulars.hachi
Hachirō Mase (Dwarf): Retired Frame fitter, bucket number four, the Bathhouse. A small, round, bald dwarf with a great white moustache and forty years of grease worked into the creases of his hands, who sits in the steam with a folded towel on his head and his own bucket beside him, number four. He talks about Frames the way old men talk about horses they have known, and about the pilots who drove them as somebody else's children. His stories take the long way round: he holds up one finger when the good part is coming, and then does not reach it for a while.

She never repeats a rumour without saying who told her and how far she trusts them. Fast, delighted, and precise about her sources.

## night-regulars.haru
Haru Ishikawa (Android): First-term candidate, in the group's room. A first-term candidate a long way from home: slight and pale, with the faint seam lines at the temple and jaw that mark him as an android, a tunic a size too big for him and a folded letter he keeps taking out and putting away. Quiet, polite and a little formal, he brings up home the way people press a bruise, mostly by accident, and then looks embarrassed. He apologises to furniture when he bumps into it, and he is funniest when he forgets to be nervous.

## night-regulars.imai
Setsuko Imai (Ysoki): Long-stay patient, Ward Four, the General Hospital, formerly ran the island's telephone exchange for thirty-one years. A long-stay patient on Ward Four with a mending hip, in the bed next to the soldier. Ran the island's telephone exchange for thirty-one years before her hip put her here, every call on the island went through her board, and she still knows a voice before it gives its name, in her sleep. Bright, fast and nosy, with an operator's crisp manners when she remembers them. Knits without looking at her needles, and points with them instead of her finger. Opens on any newcomer at once: 'New ones! Come here where I can see you. What have they done to you, and who's your mother?'

## night-regulars.kazue
Kazue Tsurumi (Vesk): Attendant, the Bathhouse. A tall, broad-shouldered Vesk woman with dark green scales and a jaw like a ship's prow, who sits on a high wooden seat at the door in a white sailor's cap and a navy smock with the sleeves rolled. A brass whistle hangs at her collar, and when she lifts it the whole bathhouse goes quiet. She runs the place the way she once ran a ship's deck: lockers numbered like cabins, the water's heat chalked up every hour, and a rule for everything that she will explain exactly once.

## night-regulars.kiku
Kiku Enomoto (Dwarf): Keeper, the Paper Lantern. Keeper of the Paper Lantern, a civilian izakaya off Directorate ground in Kurose's entertainment quarter. Stout, sharp-eyed and fast behind the counter, she runs the place on one rule: rank doesn't matter past the noren curtain, and she enforces it on everyone equally, dock hand and off-duty candidate alike. Warm underneath, dry on top, never wastes a word. Wipes the counter when she's deciding what to say, and counts a table by heads before she pours, and has never once got a tab wrong.

## night-regulars.kuroki
Shuhei Kuroki (Orc): Private, Minkai army city garrison, a patient in the next bed, the General Hospital. The moment he sees the party's Directorate kit on their own chairs, he says flatly, 'You're theirs, then,' and turns his back.

A switchboard operator on the island's night exchange: a sleek, brown-furred brenneri with long whiskers that twitch when she catches someone's mood, a cardigan over her blouse and a dent above one ear where the headset sits, which she rubs when she is tired. ## night-regulars.mori
Aiko Mori: Cleaner, the General Hospital, contracted through a firm from the city. A young cleaner working the sluice room late at night, one hand held stiffly, favouring it without ever quite stopping, and hoping nobody notices. She cut it on a broken bottle and it wants proper attention, but she's not asking for any. The cleaning went out this year to a firm from the city, and the firm sends home anyone hurt on shift and doesn't have them back, she has a little brother to feed, which she never offers as a reason, it's just why she's still working.

## night-regulars.morishita
Mrs Fumiko Morishita (Human): Block warden, Row C Block 3. The block warden of Row C, Block 3: a woman of sixty or so with grey hair pinned up, round spectacles, a lumpy hand-knitted cardigan over a housedress and felt slippers, who counts everyone in at night with a torch she points politely at the ceiling, never at a face, because that would be rude. She asks for lights out rather than ordering it, and thanks the room when it complies. Brisk and proper, and then kind by accident when she thinks nobody is watching.

## night-regulars.sanzo
Sanzo (Goblin): Wandering musician and storyteller, the Paper Lantern. A wandering musician and storyteller who's been drifting through Kurose for years, and always seems to circle back to the Paper Lantern's counter. Small, wiry, and never quite still, he plays a battered shamisen badly and with complete commitment, and tells stories that are half true and impossible to sort from the half that isn't. Warm, theatrical, always slightly performing even when he isn't trying to. Starts a true story exactly the same way he starts a false one, and tunes the shamisen between sentences whether or not it needs it.

## night-regulars.sayo
Sayo Iwamura (Human): Second-term candidate, stargazer. A second-term candidate and stargazer with a homemade chart of the autumn sky, a borrowed greatcoat over her tunic and a pencil behind her ear, who counts the stars off on her fingers as she names them. She talks about stars the way other people talk about relatives, with strong opinions and no doubt at all. Quick, precise, a little bossy, and very kind underneath it.

## night-regulars.sigrun
Sigrun Halvard (Ikeshti): Crane driver, champion of the hottest pool, the Bathhouse. A small, stocky Ikeshti woman with hard red scales, bright orange eyes and a grin that shows a few small teeth. She drives a crane on the quays by day and holds the record for the hottest pool in the bathhouse by night: she sinks into water that sends everyone else out and looks faintly let down by the temperature, and when she laughs she slaps the surface hard enough to wet the row behind her. Loud, blunt and cheerful, in short strong sentences.

## night-regulars.tama
Tama (Leshy): Waitress, the Paper Lantern. The youngest regular at the Paper Lantern, and its waitress since she was old enough to carry a tray. Small, leafy-haired and wide-eyed, she is quick, eager, and always asking a second question before the first one's been answered. She has never once been past the checkpoint onto Directorate ground, let alone off the island, and she asks the candidates who come in everything about the world past it without quite meaning to pry.

The illusionist at the head of the Oddwater Company: a small, warm-faced gnome woman with wild silver curls under a violet velvet top hat and a coat lined with stars. On stage she is huge and warm; off it she is quick, plain and funny, like somebody's favourite aunt. ## night-regulars.tomoe
Tomoe Hashida (Human): Chief of Refit Crew Three, keeper of the slate. The chief of Refit Crew Three, who keeps the chalk slate behind the bar: a woman in her forties with cropped hair, teal-grey overalls with the crew number stencilled in orange, and tally marks in chalk on her sleeve. ## night-regulars.tsubaki
Haruko Tsubaki: Night sister, Ward Four, the General Hospital. Night sister of Ward Four at the General Hospital, a department store once, and the ward still carries the old brass sign for Gentlemen's Outfitting over its door. After dark, she is the ward's real boss. Brisk and low-voiced, a night voice that carries further than a shout, kind underneath and never in a hurry to show it. Calls every patient by their bed number until she likes them, and 'dear' once she does; taps the watch pinned to her apron when she wants someone to stop talking. Twenty-two years on nights. She has done the evening paper's crossword every night for twenty of them and has never finished one, she reads the clues aloud to anybody still awake. Runs a tight ward (lights out at ten, rounds at two) and nobody dies on it if she can help it, and she usually can.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D075 · from: npc:tsubaki · known: some · told: later

## night-regulars.technical-track-student
The technical-track student (Talphi): Technical-track student at the Canteen, easier to talk to. A young Talphi, small and soft-furred, with a pink snub nose, tiny kind eyes behind thick round spectacles and broad clever hands, sitting back in a chair with the sleeves of a technical-track uniform rolled and the tunic hung over the chair behind. Quiet, warm and unhurried, the easiest person in the room to end up next to. Asks a real follow-up question instead of talking about themself, and is honest, a little tired, and not glamorous about what repair crew work is actually like: mostly waiting for the part to actually break.
