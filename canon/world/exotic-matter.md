## exotic-matter.what-and-where
Exotic matter is the fuel the makers' ships run on, and what the four dropped at Kortos. Spilled, it opens a rift that grows on everything near it. More was found in the generation ship at Kortos, once First Impact had opened it up. The Directorate knows the search ship may carry the same exotic matter the founders dropped at Kortos, and any it finds is the most carefully guarded thing it owns.
