## founders.the-inquisitors
Soon after the four took to the town of Otari, Inquisitors of Abadar, god of law and money, in blood-red cloaks and led by Inquisitor Sofie Reitz, with Kanika, Halina Benchae, Bronwen Rodford, Laurette Piraudrt, Zibene and Anja Blairr, came asking questions about the mitflits at the keep. They became allies, and the four kept their friendship with Reitz through the hunters, the nuns and the portal.

## founders.the-nuns
Hunters killed a gnoll and took a party of Abadar's nuns prisoner for witnessing it, and a mage, Regina Key, wiped the nuns' memories on William Charles Baldwin's orders. One nun, Esther, escaped and led the four to the hunters' camp. Regina was arrested with the camp, and the nuns' memories never fully came back.

## founders.three-forts
With the keep overcrowded and Otari uneasy, the mitflits' council sent the four to look at three ruined forts as a new home: Fort Ranhold, Fort Croglang and Stonehill Keep. At Ranhold they found insect tunnels and an ooze that had killed the mitflits who had settled it in 4700. At Croglang they found Baldwin Manor, once Croglang Castle, where the four rescued mitflits forced to make weapons, fought through the manor with the Inquisitors beside them, and crossed the Shadow Plane and Abadar's Plane of Order in a single night. Baldwin accused them of killing his family.

## founders.as-they-were
In their adventuring days the four were a gnoll barbarian who talked to his ancestors (Hyuck), a skeleton rogue with a doctor's hands (Shucks), a mitflit who brokered pacts (Anabala) and an elf oracle of the stars who had trained as a performer (Rupiah). The others could be unsure what to make of each other: early on Rupiah worried about wandering into the wild with a skeleton, a strange robot and a gnoll.

## founders.the-plea
On the generation ship's cargo deck, as the breach opened, a mitflit council leader, Comhphas, sent the four a plea: the mitflits at Gauntlight were outnumbered by hunters with dark magic and were building a counterpart portal, and "time behaves differently there; you'll emerge just in time to make a difference." The four went through, and came out in Nirvana, where Sarenrae received them in Luminara, a golden city of light with a cathedral at its heart. She told them they had come "through an act not born of intent but of consequence", named them, and set them to mend the tear they had made themselves, as a chance for redemption, promising to return them afterwards.

## founders.the-contract
A letter in a flowery, elaborate hand, signed "[Elusive Quill]", hired an assassin, Peregrine, "Harbinger of Moonlit Silhouettes and Hushed Laments", to give "eternal repose" to four accidental trespassers on a plan: a beast with a sunlit face and dark coat, a phantasm stitched from obsidian edges, a disciple of hushed echoes, and an enigma in unassuming garments. It describes the four, and who sent it is not recorded.

## founders.old-campaign-closure
Blank Inheritance is an attempt to tie everything up at once: it gives closure to the original adventure, which was never finished and stopped with the four on a different plane, Nirvana, while also being a new campaign in its own right. The old material is source, not a straitjacket, and may be rewritten, enhanced or clarified wherever this one needs it.

## founders.page-forty
The school's approved history spends one page on First Impact, page forty, in the passive voice. It names all four as heroes and omits the cause and what they looked like, and its margin dates do not reconcile. It is not a lie anybody is currently telling: it is a two-hundred-year tidying that nobody alive has had reason to examine.
