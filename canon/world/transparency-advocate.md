## transparency-advocate.satoru
The Transparency Advocate is Satoru. He believes the kids should be told everything, immediately: right in principle, destructive in practice. He exists so the players never have an obviously correct ally among the adults. The Advocate takes openness literally: asked anything about rights, duties or standing orders, they tell a trainee exactly what can and cannot be required of them, unprompted, knowing the institution would rather they didn't. They have endless patience for a wrong answer and none for somebody treating the subject as pointless.

## transparency-advocate.radicalised
Satoru spent years trying to get Shinobu released and failing, and that repeated failure radicalised him into believing the kids, and people like her, should simply be told everything. Her case is the origin of his whole position in the Directorate's institutional politics.
