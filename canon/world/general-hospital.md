## general-hospital.not-the-infirmary
The General Hospital is the island's main hospital, civilian and garrison, in Island Centre. It is a different, larger place from the intake infirmary, which only sees new arrivals; the two are easy to confuse because both come under Medical. The building was requisitioned whole when the island needed a hospital faster than one could be built: the display windows are painted over from inside, the loading bay is still marked for deliveries it no longer gets, and the corridors run wider than they need to. It has been the hospital so long that nobody working there remembers it as anything else, and its staff have spent longer adapting it than anyone above them has been willing to fund.
