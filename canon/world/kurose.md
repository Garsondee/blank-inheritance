## kurose.cicadas-revered
The city treats cicadas as an almost-holy animal, because so much of its world is buried (the two ships, the technology dug out of them) and cicadas spend years underground before they emerge. A cicada statue is built into a wall, a dried cicada is pinned to someone's hat, a cicada is a graffiti tag or a careful stencil. Nobody connects this with the cult that uses the same symbol.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D077 · from: ie:cicada-motif · known: everyone · told: later

## kurose.small-signs
Everyday Kurose, in small signs. Someone mends a window with a cantrip and apologises for it. A shopkeeper blames a caster because a preservation charm doesn't hold like it used to, and is wrong. Sail-rigged traders tie up alongside coal cranes. Cliff-top red tile stands against the smokestack tenement line. A hand-lettered notice asks anyone who spots a mitflit to report it to the Harbour Office, not handle it themselves. Draft paperwork has a queue for every form.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D075 · from: ch1:4-show-never-tell · known: everyone · told: later

## kurose.magic-shows
Showy magic is frowned on in Kurose except in an emergency, so people go to little back-street shows where highly powerful illusionists cast spells around them, the equivalent of a three-dimensional immersive film. It is a guilty pleasure, but most people in the city have been to at least one in their lifetime, and some religious-themed shows are put on publicly during high religious holidays.
status: sketch · by: ingram · source: D078, D002 · from: ledger:2-nozomi-and-the-party-s-early-activation · known: everyone · told: later
