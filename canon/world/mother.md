## mother.the-fragment
A mislabelled fragment in a glass case at Kurose's museum is a fragment of Mother. It is a piece of the generation ship that was never pulverised: small, coherent and sane. It sat mislabelled in a display case for two centuries, and it remembers what the navigation system was for, and what it answered to. Mother may still be alive in it, weakened and unable to control the Watcher directly, but a witness to everything and a source of information the players can reach.
