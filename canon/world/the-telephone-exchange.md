## the-telephone-exchange.every-call
Every call between any two parts of the Directorate is connected by hand in the island's telephone exchange, a small, hot building where four operators work a wall of jacks and cords faster than seems possible. That makes its operators the best-connected people on the island, in both senses. Nobody looks up when someone comes in, and everybody knows they did. The telephone exchange has eleven switchboard positions, and every call on the island goes through one of them. The supervisor's professional ethic is that the operators do not listen. Signals are taught in that room, live, with a spare headset going round.
