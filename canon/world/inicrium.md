## inicrium.what-it-is
Inicrium is "solid magic potential": a dark, glimmering ore that acts as a magical battery, capturing energy from the winds of magic and releasing it on command or when circumstances demand. Past about fifty grams, it collapses into a shiny grey metal ball with its energy dangerously concentrated; around a hundred grams it explodes in a violent magical blast.

## inicrium.shadow-plane
Where magical energy gathers in great concentration, the Shadow Plane, a dim reflection of the world that occupies the same space, can intersect it: shadowy creatures, unexpected portals and odd magic appear until the winds of magic disperse the concentration and the join heals itself.
