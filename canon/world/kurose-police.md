## kurose-police.jurisdiction-quarrel
The quarrel between the police and the Directorate is jurisdiction: the Directorate takes anything alien, the police take everything else, and every incident with both in it is split down the middle and never stitched back together. The police work beside the Directorate on raids because the mayor makes them, and they don't like it. Ask them for anything else and you get nowhere quickly: sharing a raid isn't sharing a file.
