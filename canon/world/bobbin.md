## bobbin.whose-machine
Whose machine Bobbin sits in is not decided. It must never be a player's own: nothing in this campaign takes a player's machine or character away from them. In a Gen 1 Frame, which has no living core, why he can't be pulled out without being killed needs a reason of its own; in a Gen 2, the wild mitflits' discomfort around Gen 2 could be part of the price he pays for the strike. Either way the discomfort isn't resolved quickly.
