## baldwin-manor.volkheed
Baldwin Manor stands above the village of Volkheed on the Isle of Kortos, which was founded in 4071 AR. Inicrium mining began there around 4171 AR.

## baldwin-manor.mitflit-clan
A mitflit clan arrived in Volkheed in 4147 AR and lent its "enigmatic skills and innate magical talents" to the Inicrium trade, becoming part of the village.

## baldwin-manor.croglang
The Inicrium trade paid for a castle, Croglang Castle, which the powerful Baldwin family later claimed and renamed Baldwin Manor. The old history's own dates are inconsistent: the castle is built in 4221 AR and renamed in 4201 AR.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D074, D029 · from: sm:the-trouble-with-mitflits-journals-history-of-baldwin-manor · known: nobody · told: later · when: 4201 AR

## baldwin-manor.manufactory
Baldwin Manor grew into one of the largest magical-weapons manufactories on Kortos, exporting across the sea to Absalom. A profits report for 4721 AR puts the year's net profit at about two hundred thousand gold pieces, a little lower that midsummer for fewer exports and fewer war contracts.

## baldwin-manor.the-family
The Baldwins at the manor were Alastair, a powerful fire caster; Castria Anne, an ice-and-water enchanter; Agatha Laine and Reginald Cavil; and William Charles Baldwin, the hunters' leader. The manor's Master Enchanter was Castria Anne Baldwin, who signed its materials procurement records: mithral, adamantine, cold iron, darkwood and drakescale by the hundredweight, bought from suppliers across the region, with a note about "enchantment surges for upcoming ceremony."

## baldwin-manor.the-siege
An Inicrium battery at the manor pulled the Shadow Plane into the town, and shadow creatures overran it. The four disabled the battery, and the shadows stopped encroaching. Members of the Baldwin family died in the siege, by accident and not at the four's hands.

## baldwin-manor.the-dark-secret
The GM's plan for the manor was that its people would not be innocent: a castle that invites the four in, a siege, and a dark secret in ritual sacrifice of prisoners, "both sides are fantatics".

## baldwin-manor.the-curious-child
The Inicrium battery was made by a mitflit savant child who was just curious. When it was turned on, the town below the castle was overrun by shadow-plane creatures and the castle came under siege; the four worked out how to disable the generator by destroying it, and the shadows retreated. It has nothing to do with the exotic matter.

## baldwin-manor.far-from-the-ship
Baldwin Manor and the ruined generation ship are many miles apart. First Impact happened many miles from the manor, in a hidden, jungle-like valley on Kortos, and the two events are not to be confused.
