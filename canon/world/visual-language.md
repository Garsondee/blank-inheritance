# The visual language
kind: thing

## visual-language.authority-teal
Teal-grey is the colour of authority in Kurose: the stone-cut, bureaucratic, ageing power of the Directorate's buildings, offices and rules.
status: decided · source: D054 · known: everyone · told: start

## visual-language.life-orange
Warm orange, the light of golden hour and of a lit lamp, is the colour of life: it falls on people, and it is what a place looks like when somebody is looking after it.
status: decided · source: D054 · known: everyone · told: start

## visual-language.conformity
Some people in Kurose hide behind conformity and wear the uniform exactly as issued; others stand out like sore thumbs, and are made sore for it.
status: decided · source: D054 · known: everyone · told: start

## visual-language.candidate-tunic
A candidate's uniform is a slate teal-grey tunic with a high collar and brass buttons, worn over a cream shirt, with a small orange tab on the collar.
status: proposal · source: D054 · known: everyone · told: start

## visual-language.crew-overalls
The repair and machine-shop crews wear teal-grey overalls with the crew's number stencilled on the chest in orange.
status: proposal · source: D054 · known: everyone · told: start

## visual-language.worn-as-issued
Officers and wardens wear their uniform as issued, and the people who don't (a waistcoat under the tunic, a scarf, a headscarf) are the ones the room notices.
status: proposal · source: D054 · known: everyone · told: start

## visual-language.aliens-visible
Aliens who refuse to wear glamour are the most visible sore thumbs in any teal-grey room.
status: proposal · source: D054, D024 · known: everyone · told: start
