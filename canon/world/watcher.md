# The Watcher
kind: thing

## watcher.unknown
At the start, nobody knows the Watcher exists: not the Directorate, not the founders, not the party. The party must be the ones to find it -- this is a requirement, not a coincidence of pacing. It does not mean nothing in the fiction has ever shown anyone anything: three sightings happened before the party (see `watcher.sightings`), and Cicada already uses a version of the reveal to recruit (D002). What stays true is that none of those people understood or named what they saw as the Watcher.
status: decided · source: D040, D053 · known: nobody · told: later

## watcher.sightings
Three sightings happened before the party: Manako's private lab spill (with his assistant present), a second time Manako proved it to himself privately, then the canister released at a science convention. The Envoy (Hori and Aoi) was its first real appearance in public.
status: decided · source: D040 · known: few · told: later

## watcher.chemical
A chemical, already used at least three times before the party (Manako's lab, the convention), makes the Watcher briefly visible. The window doesn't last: under an hour at most, and it evaporates quickly, leaving only a hard-to-find residue that carries little information on its own.
status: decided · source: D040, D053 · known: few · told: later

## watcher.not-a-required-sighting
Seeing the Watcher directly isn't something the party needs to do. They can't attack it -- it is not a fight to win by being in the room with it.
status: decided · source: D053 · known: nobody · told: later

## watcher.attack-question
Much later, once its scale is understood, the Directorate's senior leadership argues over how to act against something that cannot be touched: invisible, intangible, and above the city itself. The party may be present to make their own case to the room. Not yet placed on any chapter plan.
status: decided · source: D053 · known: nobody · told: later

## watcher.eventual-public-knowledge
The group cannot defeat the problem on Golarion. By the end of the adventure, everyone will know the Watcher existed, whether they personally saw it or not. This is about what becomes PUBLICLY KNOWN, not about what happens to the Watcher itself -- its own final fate (dispersed, harmless, or taken home) stays open, per D041. Ingram's own hedge: "that's how I see things going potentially" -- a strong working direction, not locked the way this file's other facts are.
status: open · source: D053 · known: nobody · told: later · candidates: "Public, worldwide knowledge by the end" | "Known within Kurose/the Directorate's own people, not necessarily wider Golarion"

## watcher.animals
Dogs, birds and other animals sense the Watcher's swarm, perhaps as a high buzzing that upsets them, and go anywhere else.
status: decided · source: D043 · known: nobody · told: later
