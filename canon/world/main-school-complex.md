## main-school-complex.one-building
The Main School Complex is the island's combined secondary school and technical college, stacked eight storeys into one block because there was never room to spread out. The high-school-age students hold the lower floors, and the technical college above trains the specialists the Directorate wants. They share a front door and mix as little as the timetable allows; higher up, the corridors start asking for a pass. Whatever happens to a cohort at the school, a scandal, a disappearance, a result that doesn't add up, reaches every family on the island within the week, because everyone's children pass through the same building.

## main-school-complex.two-schools-joined
It was built as two institutions on adjoining plots, an ordinary secondary school and a technical college for the shipyards, and merged into one block when the island ran out of room to spread sideways. On the third floor the two buildings' floor levels don't quite line up, and a half-flight of stairs corrects the difference.

## main-school-complex.the-real-archive
Every student's placement test is scored here before the results reach their family. The registrar's office on the second floor keeps the real archive, the underlying test data rather than the grades handed back, and almost nobody in the building has been told what the Directorate does with it.

## main-school-complex.nobody-compares
The registrar's office is where several unusual results in one cohort would first show on paper, if anyone there compared notes across sections. Nobody does yet; whether and when somebody starts is for the table.
