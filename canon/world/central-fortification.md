## central-fortification.the-seat
The flat grey fortress at the island's southern edge is Central Fortification, which everyone calls Central: the seat of the Directorate. From outside it is blank armoured wall, with nothing on it to measure its size against. The armoured covers over its windows and doors slide back only now and then, to let light in for a while. Two roadways enter through its north-west face, each with its own checkpoint and queue, both wide enough to drive vehicles straight inside under escort.

## central-fortification.built-first
Central came first. It was built on a real rock outcrop, the island that stood in the harbour before, and the artificial island was reclaimed around it afterwards, which is why the island's districts fan outward from its southern edge. Most of Central is underground, and its lowest levels are cut straight into the outcrop's rock: the people who work there call it an iceberg and mean it literally, solid rock cut into, not a hollow.
