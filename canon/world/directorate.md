# The Directorate
kind: group

## directorate.founded-after
About five years after the rift, four of the people who had stood in front of it founded the Directorate, so that the response next time would be faster and more decisive.
status: decided · source: S001, D024, D025 · known: everyone · told: start

## directorate.came-to-kurose
About a hundred and fifty years ago, the Directorate crossed the world and settled in Kurose.
status: decided · source: S001, D024, D025 · known: everyone · told: start

## directorate.why-kurose-unexplained
The Directorate has never said publicly why it believes there will be another rift, or why it crossed the world to wait for it in a fishing village.
status: decided · source: D048 · known: everyone · told: start

## directorate.public-view
Kurose sees the Directorate as both a grand mission against a disaster nobody expects to see and a heavy-work corps everybody relies on, and people joke about the gap.
status: decided · source: D025 · known: everyone · told: start

## directorate.newcomers
Hundreds of people from across Golarion moved to Kurose to be part of the Directorate.
status: decided · source: D048 · known: everyone · told: start

## directorate.newcomers-built
The newcomers were engineers, clerks, scholars and pilots, and then the people who fed, housed and sold to them. They built streets and temples of their own.
status: provisional · source: episode-c1e1--from-up-here · known: everyone · told: start

## directorate.brought-wealth
Not everyone trusts the Directorate, but few can argue against the vast wealth and technology it has brought to the area in so little time.
status: decided · source: D048 · known: everyone · told: start

## directorate.answers-to-itself
The Directorate recruits separately from Minkai's army and navy, and operates outside Minkai's national lines.
status: decided · source: D025, D026 · known: everyone · told: start

## directorate.warmongers
The Directorate is a foreign army in the harbour: intruders, outsiders, even warmongers.
status: decided · source: D025 · known: everyone · told: start · held-by: many in Kurose · true: no · truth: directorate.founded-after

## directorate.protectors
The Directorate is Kurose's protector.
status: decided · source: D025 · known: everyone · told: start · held-by: some in Kurose · true: yes

## directorate.base
The Directorate's base is its own man-made island in the harbour, built up around a rock and reached by a long causeway with a railway line beside it. A flat grey fortress with no windows stands at its southern end, among hangars, workshops, offices and barracks, and a high fence runs round the whole island.
status: provisional · source: episode-c1e1--from-up-here · known: everyone · told: start

## directorate.gate
At the land end of the causeway is the gate: a red-and-white barrier across the road, a guard booth not much bigger than a telephone box, and a guard with a ledger.
status: provisional · source: episode-c1e1--from-up-here · known: everyone · told: start

## directorate.sends-for-trainees
Every year the Directorate sends for new trainees.
status: decided · source: D048 · known: everyone · told: start

## directorate.trainee-expectations
Most people expect a spell as a trainee to be a faff and a bother: a duty which, once discharged, is done.
status: decided · source: D048 · known: everyone · told: start

## directorate.how-trainees-come
Some trainees volunteer, some are encouraged into it, and some are pushed into it.
status: decided · source: D048 · known: everyone · told: start

## directorate.who-joins
People apply to the Directorate, or are streamed on to it from school.
status: decided · source: D026 · known: everyone · told: start

## directorate.digs
The Directorate is Golarion's foremost gatherer of alien technology. It digs for it and locks it in deep storage vaults, so that it can't cause another disaster.
status: decided · source: D040, D044, D056 · known: few · told: in:episode-c1e1--the-errand

## directorate.brought-forward-stamp
Every candidate's acceptance letter this year has its printed reporting date ruled through, "Monday next" written beside it in pen, and a red BROUGHT FORWARD stamp. Nothing on intake day explains it.
status: decided · source: D038 · known: some · told: in:episode-c1e1--the-road-in

## directorate.why-brought-forward
This year's intake was brought forward two weeks because Nozomi, the pilot expected to fly first, refused the draft after a magic show.
status: decided · source: D002, D011, D020, D038 · known: few · told: later

## directorate.form-12b
Form 12-B, the intake screening form, asks for mother's maiden name twice: in box 4, and again in box 19, in capitals.

## directorate.missing-pilot-search
On intake morning, staff are quietly hunting for a pilot who hasn't turned up. A careful listener can catch the name Nozomi.
status: decided · source: D011 · known: few · told: in:episode-c1e1--intake-medicals

## directorate.the-marker
Some people carry a heritable marker in their blood. Only people who carry it can pilot the newer, second-generation Frames. All six of the party carry it.
status: decided · source: D020, D021, D034 · known: few · told: later

## directorate.carriers-streamed
Candidates whose screen shows the marker are streamed into one section together, as routine. Six in a single intake has never happened before.
status: provisional · source: episode-c1e1--intake-medicals · known: few · told: later

## directorate.section-nine
The six are Section Nine. Each of them was told, alone, that their screen came back irregular.
status: provisional · source: episode-c1e1--intake-medicals · known: some · told: in:episode-c1e1--intake-medicals

## directorate.school-and-service
The Directorate is a school and a service at once: candidates sit lessons, and they also drill, carry orders and stand watches.

## directorate.free-time

## directorate.curfew-minor

## directorate.orders-serious
Refusing a direct order is the most serious thing a candidate can do. It means a hearing in front of someone senior, to explain themselves.

## directorate.alarm-never-real
