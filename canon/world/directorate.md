# The Directorate
kind: group

## directorate.founded-after
About five years after the rift, four of the people who had stood in front of it founded the Directorate, so that the response next time would be faster and more decisive.
status: decided · source: S001, D024, D025 · known: everyone · told: start · when: about 5 years after rift.opened

## directorate.came-to-kurose
About a hundred and fifty years ago, the Directorate crossed the world and settled in Kurose.
status: decided · source: S001, D024, D025 · known: everyone · told: start · when: about 150 years ago

## directorate.why-kurose-unexplained
The Directorate has never said publicly why it believes there will be another rift, or why it crossed the world to wait for it in a fishing village.

## directorate.public-view

## directorate.base
The Directorate's base is its own man-made island in the harbour, built up around a rock and reached by a long causeway with a railway line beside it. A flat grey fortress with no windows stands at its southern end, among hangars, workshops, offices and barracks, and a high fence runs round the whole island.
status: provisional · source: episode-c1e1--from-up-here · known: everyone · told: start

## directorate.gate
At the land end of the causeway is the gate: a red-and-white barrier across the road, a guard booth not much bigger than a telephone box, and a guard with a ledger.
status: provisional · source: episode-c1e1--from-up-here · known: everyone · told: start

## Notes
Ingram's own phrase for the armed-response team is "Magical SWAT Team" (D068). It stays in this note and out of anything the table hears. Missions against criminals and missions against cultists are the campaign's two main branches of personnel-level combat. The mayor's support for the Directorate is why such missions are joint with the police (`mayor.support-forces-cooperation`).

The Directorate was born from a coalition of the organisations around Kortos, banded together to guard the opened ship, with the four at its heart. ## directorate.clearance
Rank in the Directorate is Clearance, from 0 to 5, and each rank lets its holders do more: 0, Recruits, do as they're told; 1, Cadets, choose their duty and sign for small things themselves; 2, Duty Section, send themselves to a contact and interview detainees; 3, Officers, direct other units and call on a city asset; 4, Command, overrule a decision once a chapter and open sealed records; 5, Prodigies, the city does what they say. It is being believed, made countable.

## directorate.not-a-conspiracy
The Directorate is not a conspiracy. It is an institution that has never been tested, can't admit it, and is slowly harming the city it was built to protect, mostly by doing its job exactly as written. Nobody at the top coordinates its faults: they are separate departments being ordinary. The people of the Directorate are, with real exceptions, decent, tired, fond of the children and bad at their jobs in ways they can't perceive: Shucks, Nobu, Taka, the repair chiefs and the bridge staff are warm, funny and on the children's side. The faults of the institution are none of their doing and mostly beyond their power.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D074, D081 · from: d-directorate:1-summary, d-directorate:4-what-this-is-not, d-directorate:6-running-notes · known: nobody · told: later
