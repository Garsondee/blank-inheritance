# Glamour
kind: thing

## glamour.cheap
Magic that lets someone disguise an unusual appearance is cheap and everywhere: glamoured clothes are sold on the high street.
status: decided · source: D024, D044 · known: everyone · told: start

## glamour.debate
Anyone who looks too unusual for the neighbours tends to wear a glamour in public. Some call that good manners; some say easy disguise has pushed unusual-looking people into hiding when they have done nothing wrong.
status: decided · source: D024 · known: everyone · told: start

## glamour.aliens-outsiders
Aliens are treated as outsiders in Minkai.
status: decided · source: D044 · known: everyone · told: start

## glamour.refusers
Many aliens in the city refuse to disguise or tone down their appearance for anyone, and draw stares when they go out.
status: decided · source: D044 · known: everyone · told: start
