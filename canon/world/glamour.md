# Glamour
kind: thing

## glamour.cheap
Magic that lets someone disguise an unusual appearance is cheap and everywhere: glamoured clothes are sold on the high street.
status: decided · source: D024, D044 · known: everyone · told: start

## glamour.debate
Anyone who looks too unusual for the neighbours tends to wear a glamour in public. Some call that good manners; some say easy disguise has pushed unusual-looking people into hiding when they have done nothing wrong.
status: decided · source: D024 · known: everyone · told: start

## glamour.aliens-outsiders
Aliens are treated as outsiders in Minkai.
status: decided · source: D044 · known: everyone · told: start

## glamour.refusers
Many aliens in the city refuse to disguise or tone down their appearance for anyone, and draw stares when they go out.
status: decided · source: D044 · known: everyone · told: start

## glamour.the-road-in-choice
Whether a non-terrestrial character disguises on the journey to induction is the player's own call. If a player ignores the stricture, the GM rolls with it: other characters may react with a little alarm at an alien walking about, because aliens are extremely rare, but not impossible.
status: sketch · by: ingram · source: D078, D009 · from: ledger:9-disguise-before-induction-the-road-in · known: everyone · told: later

## glamour.native-or-alien
A people is one of Golarion's own exactly when it is also a PF2e ancestry. If it isn't, it is alien.
status: sketch · by: ingram · source: D078, D039 · from: ledger:39-nobu-thought-the-chemical-made-them-both-see-things-golarion-s-own-peoples-are-pf2e-s-ancestries · known: everyone · told: later

## glamour.the-ten-natives
Of the ancestries on offer at induction, the peoples native to Golarion are Human, Elf, Dwarf, Gnome, Halfling, Goblin, Orc, Leshy, Android and Ysoki, which are PF2e's ratfolk under their own name. Dragonkin and Nuar stay alien: a nuar is only a cousin of PF2e's minotaur, and PF2e's dragonet is a different people.
