# A treated lens
kind: thing

## Pencil

## lens.what-it-shows
A small lens of ground glass treated with a chemical, Sap: Manako's own conversion instrument. Look through one and, if the Watcher is attending that place, you see it: a sky full of eyes on stalks and slow tentacles, always moving. Look through it and denial isn't available. The Watcher is never visible any other way, except where the chemical itself touches it. The lens is rational, respects the target's intelligence, and is exactly what a scientist would build.

## lens.always-works
A lens always works. What varies is whether the Watcher is attending that place, and how densely: DC 18, plus a hidden +0 to +8. That's why a lens "mostly doesn't work". The lens's modifier is never shown. The players should be free to build wrong theories about it, as Manako did. They will build superstitions about when it works, and they will be wrong in exactly the way Manako was wrong, for exactly the same reason.

## lens.nobody-sells-one
Nobody sells a lens. To see the Watcher again after Kiri's show, the party has to make their own, which means understanding what the glass is treated with.

## lens.three-ways
There are three ways to get a lens of their own: ask Manako, who explains how to treat the glass; take one from one of the Faithful; or find the shelved sample in the Directorate's own catalogue.

## lens.flood
Cicada gets treated lenses into thousands of hands, and within days crowds all over the city are looking up at the sky full of eyes. The Directorate bans the lenses and starts confiscating them, which looks like a cover-up and makes Cicada look right. It calls the sightings a hallucinogen on the glass, as the convention decided 38 years ago.

## lens.degrees
What a look through a lens shows, by degree of success: a critical success, it is there, stalked and turning, not ending at the edge of the glass, with one stalk turned towards the viewer; a success, a glimpse, movement without an object; a failure, ordinary air; a critical failure, ordinary air, and then something turns to look at them.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D072 · from: d-observer:15-the-lens · known: nobody · told: later

## lens.never-without
The eyes and tentacles are never seen without the chemical, by the party or by the city, however well known the thing becomes. After the party's first sight they can't see it again until they understand the chemical that made it visible. Its cause and origin stay a mystery at the table. Its children are different: built from Sap, they are solid and anyone can see them.
