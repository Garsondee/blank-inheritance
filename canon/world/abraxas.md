## abraxas.host-and-alliance
A very long time ago, hundreds of years before First Impact, Anabala became the host of Abraxas, a powerful female demon who gave her access to great power and brought clarity enough to root around deep in the mind of the mitflit. What the demon found suggested the mitflits could potentially be a powerful people. She is self-interested but not evil, and wants the mitflits to succeed. At first the demon overpowered Anabala, and for many decades the demon was fully in control. Eventually Anabala fought back and turned the tables, making the demon subservient by making it clear that the only path to unlimited information was through Anabala's cooperation, not conquest. Anabala and the demon now have an uneasy alliance: Anabala wants to return the mitflits home, and the demon wants to learn everything it can, literally feeding on that information and becoming more powerful. For now their interests overlap.
