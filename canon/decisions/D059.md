## Settled

1. **Nobu's chandlers are accepted.** Some of what Nobu hands out is bought for cash from the ships' chandlers on the old town's waterfront. A player who looks closely at an extra from him can find the chandler's stamp under the fresh paint (Crafting or Perception, at Kit Issue).
2. **The typed form is a find.** A player who studies the name typed on their Form 12-B can see it came off the same admissions typewriter as their acceptance letter, before they arrived (Perception, at Intake Medicals). The plant on the form stays: the GM says nothing unless someone looks.
3. **The dogs bark at one dark street on the crossing to the drains.** A second sighting of the side street the dogs won't walk down in The Errand, read aloud, with no roll: The Walk Down stays the episode's relax beat.
4. **The Scene Runner remembers for the GM.** It records each character's own result on a beat, names whom each carry-in applies to, shows the party with what is live for each of them, sets up a fight in one press and raises its clock as rounds end, gives an objective when its handout is shown and fires a beat's objective chips when the beat is marked done, asks the scene's write-down questions when it closes, and keeps what the table has been told.
5. **What stays in the GM's hands:** entrances, name reveals, handouts, Hero Points, plants, optional beats, how a fight ends, the hearing, choices the table settles out loud, and the close verdict.
