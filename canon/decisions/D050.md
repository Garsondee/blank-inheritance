# D050 · 2026-09-29 · Episode One ends on a choice of night-time rests, and relaxed scenes draw events from a deck

asked: nothing; Ingram gave this as a change of plan.

## Ingram

> "Change of plan for the end of Episode 1. Having discharged their duties the players will be given a range of fun and interesting rest options, some basic, some more interesting, some with associated skills rolls, some with automatic buffs, some with information and all of these night time recovery options come with varying amounts of HP regain scaling with level (so that they remain just as useful throughout the game). The hospital rest should allow a full and total recovery but no additional buffs. Each of these will be a scene too, so we'll need to generate them and I'll use these scenes to write notes and we'll add stuff to the scene runner so that I've got material to run there and sometimes new and unique stuff to run. There is nothing to say that some events couldn't be randomly picked, we define the things that could happen and then pick randomly and I roll with it as long as each option is unique. The other thing is that we don't have to remove an event if no one has witnessed it, so if a school lesson doesn't get picked it can be shuffled back into the deck and it has a chance to appear later. This is perhaps a better philosophy for these more gameplay centric scenes. Obvious some events will be story driven but I'd like to build a world of RP within those 'random' or relaxation and lower stake scenes too. We probably need a large banner colour for the scene runner and a system for informing me where this scene lies in that spectrum. Try to imagine ten options for the Episode 1 and almost all of them if not all of them would also be options for the end of Episode 2."

## Settled

1. **Episode One ends, once the players have discharged their duties, on a choice of night-time rests.** The range runs from basic to more interesting; some carry skill rolls, some give automatic buffs, some give information.
2. **Every rest restores Hit Points, in varying amounts that scale with level**, so each stays as useful at level 10 as at level 1.
3. **The hospital rest is a full and total recovery, with no other buff.**
4. **Each rest option is its own scene**, generated like any other, for Ingram to write notes on and to run from the Scene Runner, with material to run and sometimes something new and unique.
5. **Relaxed, gameplay-centred scenes may draw events at random** from a defined set. Each event drawn is unique. **An event nobody witnessed goes back into the deck** and can come up later (a school lesson not picked can appear another day).
6. **Some events are story-driven; the rest build a world of role-play** inside the random, relaxed, lower-stakes scenes.
7. **The Scene Runner shows where a scene sits on that spectrum**, with a large banner colour.
8. **Ten rest options for Episode One**, almost all (if not all) offered again at the end of Episode Two.

## Ingram, answering four questions the same day

Where the rest choice sits: **"After Flood Tunnel"** (Flood Tunnel Clearance is the duty they discharge; the choice is
the last thing that night, and nothing existing is cut). Who chooses: **"Each player their own"** (a free departure on
The City; the party may split). The Runner's banner: **"Three bands"** (Story, Mixed, Downtime). How HP regain scales:

> "Fractions of max HP and this is something that we are going to have to carefully monitor, carefully plan and test and also balance as we go along. We're taking a risk here but I want to explain the reasoning for it. I want to have less combat in this adventure than the average adventure, therefore I want to make injuries a little bit more scary. We disallow healing outside of combat (or we consider it as an option) because with it you end up with players always popping back up to full health. It would be interesting to me and hopefully to the players to have to decide between gambling on a buff in their rest or doing the sensible sober thing and going to the hospital. That feels like a choice you'd really give someone. Or if we're talking a little bit more hard core in terms of RP then what if your character just kept going to the bar rather than the hospital. It would say a lot about a character in the long run."

9. **The rest choice comes after Flood Tunnel Clearance**, the last thing that night. Nothing existing is cut.
10. **Each player chooses their own rest**, and the party may split.
11. **Rests restore a fraction of maximum HP**; the hospital restores all of it. The numbers are to be monitored,
    tested and balanced as play goes on.
12. **The reason: less combat than the average adventure, so injuries are scarier.** The choice each night is between
    gambling on a rest's buff and the sensible, sober hospital. Which one a character keeps choosing says a lot about
    them.
13. **The Scene Runner's banner has three bands:** Story, Mixed and Downtime.
