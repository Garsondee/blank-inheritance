## Settled

1. **The rule.** Every objective is given by someone in the world, shown in their own words, makes sense from their
   side, and marks every part required or optional. Three kinds, by who gave it: an order (the chain of command; a
   direct order says so and prints what refusing it costs, D053), a request (anyone else; it can be turned down), and
   own (a character decided it; their player wrote it).
2. **The tracker** is on every player's screen, top right, in three sizes: a tab, one line per objective, and
   explained. It shows the section's objectives and the player's own character's. It folds itself to the tab in a
   fight and opens again after.
3. **The Journal** is a bigger window with four tabs: Objectives, Papers, People and Record. A closed objective
   becomes one paragraph in the Record, written by the GM when closing it.
4. **Players write their own**, for the section or for one character, through a form that will not file without a
   giver, their reason and one required part. "Only me" is kept, honestly labelled: it hides the objective from the
   other players' screens but not from their browsers (GitHub #212).
5. **Episode One has three group objectives**: Report for enrolment (the letter), Collect the levelling bubble
   (Vetch's docket), Clear the storm drain (Vetch's evening order). A regular's request at night is extra.
6. **Optional parts pay only what the giver says or implies**, plus a step of attitude with a regular through the
   existing ▲▼. No hidden XP: accomplishments stay in their scenes.
7. **Manako's "come back and say how it went"** is a loose end in the Record, not a request left in the tracker.
8. **Only the GM ticks a given objective**, from the Scene Runner. Players tick their own.
9. **A soft sound** on give and close only, nothing for a tick.
