# D022 · 2026-09-21 (inferred) · The school is a real place with real lessons, and eight of them are now written

source: split word for word from LEDGER #22

Ingram, directly: *"a rebalance is needed I think. We need to put the school back in this EVA inspired
mecha adventure."* Two school sections per episode, each a choice between two classrooms, each with one
optional skill roll. Eight rooms authored across Episodes One and Two (GitHub #197).

**What this settles as lore, all of it new:**

- **Shucks Mordant teaches a field medicine class at the cadet school on Tuesday afternoons.** Entirely
  unremarkable in the fiction — nobody finds it notable that the intake doctor also takes a lesson. It
  answers one sliver of the standing "what has he done for two hundred years besides intake medicals"
  question and leaves the rest open. It exists for a structural reason: an ordinary teaching Shucks in
  the afternoon is what makes him walking unannounced into the Deputy Director's office at the end of
  that episode land as strange.
- **The history teacher also takes local studies**, and that is the lesson where he is most alive. He is
  fond of the city and weary of the approved history syllabus, which is one coherent person rather than
  two — a man who owns one subject and is merely delivering the other.
- **The school workshop has four metres of Frame forearm and hand on trestles at the back, opened along
  one side, fingers slightly curled.** Hulda Brack will not let day-one students near it and refuses
  plainly rather than mysteriously. Nobody names it. A school workshop with a war machine's hand in it,
  treated as completely routine, is the militarisation of this education stated as an object instead of
  as an argument — which is the point of the school and the Directorate sharing a campus.
- **The telephone exchange has eleven switchboard positions**, every call on the island goes through one
  of them, and the supervisor's professional ethic is that they do not listen. They teach signals in
  that room, live, with a spare headset going round.
- **The civil defence instructor has applied four times in eleven years to have the alert-state drill
  reinstated as a real base-wide exercise, and been refused four times, on cost.** Her classroom is now
  the only place on the island where any of it gets said out loud. Keep the refusals mundane: it is a
  budget decision by people who are not wrong that nothing has ever happened.
- **A natural history teacher keeps three specimens she cannot identify, and has had one of them eleven
  years with the label taken off, because the label was a guess.** Cheerful about it. This is a plant and
  it goes nowhere in either episode — it is explicitly **not** Frame biology, not a Proto-Claimant, and
  not connected to anything the party will meet.
- **The school teaches a sanitised First Impact.** *(Reshaped 2026-09-24 by #27: the page now names all four and omits the cause and their faces.)* Page forty, entirely passive voice, naming nobody, with
  margin dates that do not reconcile. It is not a lie anybody is currently telling — it is a two-hundred-
  year-old tidying nobody alive has had reason to examine, taught by a man who would be delighted to be
  shown otherwise. Older printings in the back cupboard carry a memorial plate of four figures, one very
  small, one not human, with the naming caption reset out of every later edition.

**The cross-episode chain this opens, and the discipline on it.** *(Plants 1 and 2 reshaped 2026-09-24 by #27; the discipline stands.)* Day one a teacher reads "Hyuck" off the
statue plaque and says plainly he does not know who that was, because it is not in his notes. Episode Two
a critical success reaches the memorial plate. And one of those four is teaching in the next room. **Nothing
at the table may connect them.** A player may assemble it; a scene may not hand it over. If a player says it
out loud, write it down and confirm nothing. Tracked as the `arc-the-four-founders` Node.

**Three rules governing every school period, and they are not negotiable:** not rolling is free and is a
normal thing for a student to do; risks and rewards are never announced beforehand; failure costs something
real, in a currency the players can already see at that point in the story.
