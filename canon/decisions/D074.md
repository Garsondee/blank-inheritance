## What came in

- **The Directorate** (`world/directorate.md`, 17 facts, 2 of them open): what the old dossier said is wrong with the institution, turned into pencil: it is no conspiracy, its adults are decent and blind, it was born on Kortos as a coalition guarding the opened ship, the dig is a public-works line item that calls the Watcher, it is secretive and not evil, it makes people disappear by paperwork, it consents to read the children's messages in forms nobody reads and later acts on what it reads, and the deepest vault is the party's goal. Open: who decided the silence about the blood screen, who wrote the monitoring policy, whether the leak is a fault in itself. Beside them: the police and the Directorate quarrel over jurisdiction, Nozomi's locker, and the NPC pilots who drop out one by one.
- **The Frames** (`world/frames.md`, 17 facts): Gen 1 and Gen 2 kept apart as the dossier's own corrections require. Gen 1 is Golarion clankers anyone can drive, on a status ladder with utility duty at the bottom; the Watcher's probing is what sends the party into real combat; Gen 2 comes from the generation ship, has a biomechanical mitflit core, needs a compatibility most humans lack, makes Frame Sickness each time, never takes control, and has an empty seat shaped to a disguised pilot. Beside them: the Watcher fixates on Gen 2 out of recognition (`watcher.recognises-gen-2`), and Cicada misreads that (`cicada.misreads-gen-2`). Anabala gained two open facts (her way home; whether the party can learn she built Gen 2).
- **Shucks and the screen** (`world/shucks.md`, 7 facts): he tests the blood himself, found the marker in the original trial pilots' notes, screens every candidate, is forbidden to explain, and is truly surprised by six; plus how he was in the old campaign.
- **The founders and the old campaign** (`world/founders.md`, 15 facts; new entity files `baldwin-manor.md` 8 facts, `inicrium.md` 3, `nirvana.md` 7): the founders' fame and the tidying of their faces, and the record of the two earlier real campaigns as the founders' history: peace with the mitflits, the Inquisitors and the nuns, the horse, the three forts, Baldwin Manor and its history, the Inicrium battery and the Shadow Plane siege, the jungle ship's approach, the plea from the cargo deck, Nirvana (Luminara, Sarenrae's relic, the demon-guarded locks, the golden key, the unreached boss) and the loose threads. Smaller additions to `hyuck` (5), `rupiah` (1), `mitflits` (1), `mother` (1), `baldwin` (1) and `generation-ship` (1). Players' names are not used anywhere: facts speak of characters.
- **Setting** (`era.md`, 2): the 1950s-Japan look and the uneven technology of the harbour.
- **Ideas** (`ideas/directorate-institution.md`, 15; `ideas/directorate-episode-two.md`, 2): the mirror of Manako and the Directorate, the institution absorbing reform, no side to pick, nobody to unmask, the two themes on both sides, the core theme, the dire trajectory, the founders' players meeting their characters, the Watcher's earlier voice, Hyuck's crash protocol, the pact's stages, Rupiah's unplayed nemesis, hidden true forms, mitflits feeling the ship, and two Episode Two moments (speaking for the Comms Officer; a doctor without an appointment).

Of the `sm` source only the founders' own campaign was mined. The second run of the same published adventure has no connection to this story, and most of the first campaign's folder is Paizo's published adventure, rules journals, sheets, items, macros and tables.

## Conflicts

Nothing contradicting ink or Ingram's words came in. Places where old wording disagreed or overlapped, and what was kept:

- **Where the Directorate was born.** The old dossier's summary says "born on Kortos, about 195 years ago" and "about 150 years ago it moved to Kurose"; canon's ink `directorate.founded-after` says about five years after the rift and `directorate.came-to-kurose` about 150 years ago. They agree (200 - 5 = 195); filed as `directorate.kortos-coalition`, from Ingram's words (D032).
- **Gen 2's source.** The old Gen 2 dossier says three times, in dated notes, that Gen 2 was once written as built from the search ship under Kurose, then corrected to the generation ship (D034). Only the corrected version was filed (`frames.gen-2-source`).
- **The charter's painted-out paragraph, the "berserk protocol" and Mystery Ledger's countersignature "might be nothing"** are all retired in the old documents themselves (D041, D031, D034), so none was filed as a fact; Hyuck's protocol stays the open fact `hyuck.protocol`, with the old proposal as an idea.
- **`directorate.calcified`** is another import's id (D075). It already said what this source's summary says about the untested, boring rite of passage, so only the "decent adults" half was added as `directorate.decent-adults`.
- **Baldwin Manor's dates** are the old history's own and are inconsistent (the castle is built in 4221 AR and renamed in 4201 AR); kept as written, and said so in `baldwin-manor.croglang`.
