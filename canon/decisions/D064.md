2. **Nothing in the story named it**: no scene, brief, episode or fact cited `baggage-inspection`, so no scene changes.

## Claude's readings, marked

- **The new place is `seaward-platform`, "Seaward Platform".** The name is Claude's, from where the strip lies, and is open (`open-questions.json#seaward-platform-name`). It is drafted, a train platform and nothing more: which trains stop there, and how it relates to the Mainland Platforms and the Island Platforms, are not decided.
- **The old text is kept**, in the new location's note, in case any of it is wanted again: the idea of a place where bags are opened, two inspectors on a normal day and six when something has happened, and a numbered tray for what cannot be explained in one sentence, suits the barrier in the Transfer Hall. Moving it there would be Ingram's call; nothing was moved.
- The footprint agrees: 334 metres by 4.3, the shape of a platform and no shape for a shed (`design/location-depth.md` already used this very strip as its example of a footprint too narrow for what was claimed for it).

## Changes

`data/locations/baggage-inspection.json` is removed and `data/locations/seaward-platform.json` made from the same building, with the Landing End's contents list updated. No fact of the world changes beyond that one place.
