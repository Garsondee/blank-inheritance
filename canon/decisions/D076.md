## What came in

- **The seven bible location pages** (b-loc) and their 240 map records (loc) were read in full. The map's geometry, tiers, depths, ranks, biome tags and siting data are the map's own data and stay where they are. What a GM would say about a place as part of the story came in.
- **Eighteen new place files** (`canon/world/`): Central Fortification, The Rock, The Vermilion Block, Deep Storage, General Hospital, Main School Complex, Main Mech Hangar, The Registry, The Plot Room, The Telephone Exchange, The Works (the Erecting Shop and its neighbours, with the Works Canteen), The Administrative Block (with the Establishment Offices), The Muster Yard, The Outer Sea Wall, The Drain Head and The Candidate Gate. Each holds pencil facts, with `known` set to who on the island could say it, and open facts where the old pages deliberately left a blank (what is in the nine cells, what the silo reagents are for, what is in Central's rock, who oversees the hospital, the statue's material).
- **The Gnoll Statue** is not a file of its own: it is Hyuck's statue, so its pose, the unsettled dignity question, its civic-furniture role and Hyuck's old catchphrases went into `hyuck.md`, and the founders-arc candidate into an idea. One line about the Old Landing's customs hall went into `kurose.md`.
- **One ideas file, `canon/ideas/places-hooks.md`**: the scene hooks and "what if" lines the old place records carried (summoned to a deep level of Central, a visit to The Rock, a Vermilion Block posting, Deep Storage requisitions, the hospital ward, the Registry's longer version, the Plot Room replay, the Drain Head, the unaccounted buildings, the walled row, and more).
- **Not brought in as story:** the map's district and ward descriptions, the Row and Block housing, the shops and amenities, the staff-housing rows, the walled houses, the stub bunkers and watch posts marked needs-lore, and the room text of places Episode One already stages. All are listed in Coverage.

## Conflicts

- **"The Rock" as a name for Central.** The Central Fortification page lists "The Rock" as an alternative name for Central; the map and the other page use it for the prison. Kept The Rock as the prison; the name question is an open fact, `central-fortification.name`.
- **The statue's identity "almost certainly Hyuck".** The old statue page calls it near-certain, not confirmed; canon already holds `hyuck.statue-truth` (D043, decided), so the inference was not imported.
- **Statue plaque "worn past reading".** The old page's first wording is superseded by the legible plaque (`hyuck.statue`, D027, D028); not imported.
- **The statue shows one of Hyuck's earlier bodies.** Claude's own proposal (LEDGER 41); imported only as pencil, `hyuck.statue-an-earlier-body`. It sits beside D043's "Hyuck as he was", which it neither contradicts nor confirms.
- **Shucks oversees the General Hospital.** Claude's own unconfirmed proposal; kept as an open fact (`general-hospital.who-oversees-it`), not as a fact.
- **The Directorate is "about 195 years old".** The Central and Rock pages say so; canon dates it from "about five years after the rift" (`directorate.founded-after`), so the number was not imported.
- **Four hundred or forty.** The Drill Hall record says four hundred people sit down; canon's `directorate.intake-size` has about forty candidates with the whole base turned out. The room's capacity is not a story fact; not imported.
- **"Kurose's entertainment quarter" for the Paper Lantern.** The map record notes that the scene's wording and the Slope are not reconciled. That is a siting question for Ingram on the map, not a story fact; not imported.
