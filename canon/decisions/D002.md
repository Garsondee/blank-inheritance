# D002 · 2026-09-10 · Nozomi and the party's early activation

source: split word for word from LEDGER #2

**Date:** 2026-09-10
**Asked because:** the master timeline's one-line entry ("cannot get back in the machine") didn't say
whether her situation was the actual cause of Day 1's timing or an unrelated parallel thread.

**Q:** *"The timeline says Nozomi, 'slated as the presumptive first pilot,' attends a targeted magic
show weeks before Day 1 and 'cannot get back in the machine afterward.' Is this the actual reason the
six PCs are being recruited right now — they're filling the hole she left — or is her situation a
separate thread that doesn't directly explain the party's recruitment?"*

**A:** *"It links into the way The Watcher cult works. The city has people performing 'magic shows'
privately because showy magic is frowned upon except in an emergency in my version of Golarion and
Japan. So people go to little back street shows to have highly powerful illusionists cast spells around
them and enjoy the equivalent of a 3D immersive film. It's considered a guilty pleasure but most people
in the city will have been to at least one in their lifetime, some even do more religious themed ones
that can be done publicly during high religious holidays.
So... our pilot goes to one of these shows but they are actually attending a recruitment drive by the
cultists. They reveal The Watcher who is filling the room, then they demonstrate without a trace of a
doubt that the thing they can see is real and not illusion. But they then tell the people in the crowd
that they have a choice, they can leave and tell people what they've seen here or they can stay and
learn more. Our pilot stayed, heard them out, was specifically told by a high level cultist that they
were working for the wrong side and then the next day they turn off their beeper / phone and avoid
being drafted in to fight. The group goes into action earlier than they normally should. The other
pilot ends up in questioning and temporary detainment, that's an event where someone could go and talk
to them and find out a little information from someone who is begrudging but willing to say a little
about what they saw because they've already had to say all of this to their questioners. The skill here
is talking to them in a way that doesn't ruffle their feathers, otherwise they will go quiet and won't
cooperate."*
