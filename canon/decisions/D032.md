**What this settles:**

1. **William Charles Baldwin is alive.** He was insanely rich, and wealth bought him a way to stay alive (how is open). **This is a secret, kept until the right moment.** He is a **criminal lord who arms criminal gangs with incredible weapons, and those weapons are still made by essentially enslaved mitflits.** The four founders are his natural enemies, and meeting him is personal for the players: four of them played the four.
2. **Mitflits are extremely helpful when given the right tasks, and fascinated by the production of technology.** It is their "horrible characteristic": it is what makes them so easy to use.
3. **Mother may still be alive in the museum fragment** (Ingram's "perhaps"): weakened, unable to control the Watcher directly, but a valuable witness and source of information.
4. **The cargo-deck breach was First Impact.** An accident that rippled outwards to become the adventure.
5. **The exotic fuel was for the shuttle.** The shuttle is buried deep under the rock of Kortos, away from the generation ship's ruin. The four never reached it. **A thread to tie up eventually.**
6. **There is no bisected specimen.** The rupture **opened up the inner layers of the generation ship itself.** Scientists and magic-users descended on the site, terrified of another rupture; adventurers came for valuables; it became a very dangerous place. **The organisations around Kortos banded together, and the Directorate was born to keep the site secure, prevent a future rupture, and stop the uncovered technology disrupting the world.** The technology leaked outwards anyway.
7. **Gen 1 Frames are Golarion technology only**, informed by looking at the ship's godlike technology. They are **not biomechanical**: just mechanical clankers, hard to work with. **Gen 2 Frames are Anabala building mechs from actual mitflit technology**: built on the real thing, **more like being inside a giant humanoid** ("more like EVA and less Giant Robo"). Never confuse the two.
8. **The search ship's call is why the Watcher is at Kurose** (was a #31 proposal; now canon).
9. **Sap is the generation ship's stock for its repair nanites** (was a #31 proposal; now canon).
10. **Abraxas is female.** Anabala is played by a woman who plays her as a woman, with a pact with a demon, and they are all female.
11. **Hyuck's protocol** would exist, but only with Gen 2. Open, to pin down much later.
12. **Build the Watcher's file** (a GM-only record per PC of what the Watcher has actually observed).
13. **The stray is open**, a less developed concept for later. *(Plainly: the stray is a small, broken-off piece of the Watcher, from v4 §6.)*
14. **Anabala must be present for Abraxas to emerge.** **Pippa may choose to keep playing Anabala in the new campaign, as one of the students.** Open, solvable later.
15. **The Envoy, confirmed and extended:** an accident. The officer was trying to kill what he thought was a threat, and his bullets killed the Watcher's child and also killed Aoi. **Manako broke down at the scene. Hori rushed Aoi to hospital. In the confusion, the cult's members scattered, Manako with them.**
16. **Strip the cut systems** (Resonance, Bleed, the five ground roles) out of the v4 bible and Chapter One, for now.
17. **Whose machine Bobbin sits in** is open, to resolve later.

**Consequences worth writing down, because they move old canon:**
- **Gen 1 Frames have no living core and no seat that shapes itself.** Everything written about a living core making a seat shaped to its pilot's true form (LEDGER #5, the "metal fatigue" breach, the Directorate's fault "the Frames are alive") now belongs to **Gen 2 only**. Mystery Ledger #4 (the same hull breach across every Frame for decades) moves to the Gen 2 prototypes, or is cut.
- **The Directorate's founding has two reasons in sequence:** a Kortos coalition securing the site (about 195 years ago), then the move to Kurose for the prophecy (about 150 years ago), where it found the search ship.
- **The "Gap-derived" technology leak** (the phones, and anything else too advanced for the era) is ship technology leaking outwards from the Kortos site.
- **The #31 "Baldwin line" becomes Baldwin himself.** Descendants may exist too; the man matters more.

**Filled in by Claude — proposals, veto freely:**
- **How Baldwin stays alive:** he was on Kortos when the site opened, with hunters and a weapons manufactory, so he was among the first to loot it. Ship technology (medical, repair nanites) keeps him alive, and his mitflit slaves, fascinated by the tech, keep it running. Alternatives: bought magic, or a bargain. Open (`open-questions.json#baldwin-longevity`).
- **Baldwin is the crime lord Manako works for**, at arm's length, as D12 requires. Nobu's laundering, the Fixer's "crime partners" and the heist crews all run through Baldwin's organisation. His gangs carry mitflit-made weapons. The mitflits gathering in Kurose to go home are easy prey for his workshops.
- **Baldwin's front** might trade under the old castle's name, Croglang, a name the four founders (and their players) would recognise.
