## Settled

1. **The pilot goes ahead, one scene per output, in play order.** It starts with From Up Here, Scene 0, the true first scene. Other scenes may be edited in the same output only for consistency, or to improve the throughline.
2. **A scene is approved when Ingram has read all of it and says it's good.** Until then, what's in it is provisional. Improvements she makes while reading go into the scene, and whatever the re-read should have caught goes into the re-read itself (`canon/reread.md`).
3. **The long-term story stays fluid.** Later chapters are sketched, not fixed, so the players keep driving it.
4. **Rolls, organically.** Check every scene for whether a skill roll would improve it; whether a dilemma resolves through rolls; what a critical failure escalates to; what a failure's way forward is; and what extra a critical success gives.
5. **Stat blocks are made with Ingram.** Non-combat NPCs get no special actions or features. An NPC generator of archetypes will be built, from an analysis of how SF2E builds its stat curves. The four founders are level 15: impossibly powerful against a level-1 party, and still surpassable by the group in the end. The flashbacks will need PC stat blocks for Rupiah, Shucks, Anabala and Hyuck, built from their old character sheets.
6. **Everything goes through the new tree, not just the story.** Old material is quarantined so old and new can always be told apart, and nothing slides out of sync unnoticed. This is meant to be the last big refactor.
7. **Foundry lore journals are in the first cut.**
8. **Claude chooses the names.** The new tree is `canon/`.
9. **The Casebook stays** as the way the whole story is presented, and grows as episodes are written and approved, showing what's written in detail and what isn't.
10. **Every scene gets a rough day and time** in the chapter plan. It may be "flexible" or "unknown".
