# D030 · 2026-09-24 · Where the old adventure stopped, the rupture they came back to, and what Blank Inheritance is for

source: split word for word from LEDGER #30

**Ingram, 2026-09-24, replying to the reconstructed campaign timeline** (`source-material/the-trouble-with-mitflits/campaign-timeline.md`), which found that the last sessions took place on another plane:

> "Yes, the adventure stopped with the players on a different plane of existence Nirvana. Presumably when the players go back to Golarion the First Impact Rupture was much larger and they swung in to try to stop it expanding further. We never finished that adventure and Blank Inheritance is an attempt to tie EVERYTHING up all at once, give closure to the original adventure whilst also creating a new one."

**What is settled:**
- The original campaign ended, unfinished, with the four in **Nirvana**.
- Blank Inheritance's purpose includes **closure for that campaign**: it ties up everything the old table left open, while also being a new campaign in its own right.

**What is marked "presumably"** (Ingram's own hedge, so treat it as the working assumption, open to revision):
- When the four came back to Golarion, the First Impact rupture had grown much larger than when they left.
- They "swung in" to stop it expanding further.

This fits the existing account: the four stood in front of the rift at the last (#25), and the rift grew in fits and starts while magic-users converged from across the region (`dossier-first-impact.md` §3a). It adds a reason the four arrive late to their own disaster: they were on another plane.

**A reading, not decided:** the old campaign's portal plea said *"Time behaves differently there; you'll emerge just in time to make a difference"*, and the party went through a portal from the starship's cargo deck to Nirvana. That could be how the rupture grew unchecked while they were gone. Neither the order (starship, then First Impact, then Nirvana) nor the time-slip has been asked or confirmed.

**Also standing, from the same message:** the old material is **source, not a straitjacket**. It may be rewritten, enhanced or clarified wherever Blank Inheritance needs it (restating #29 point 1).
