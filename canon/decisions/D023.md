# D023 · 2026-09-21 (inferred) · Correcting a Frame Sickness leak into Episode Two

source: split word for word from LEDGER #23

**Ingram, correcting the Zero Six Hundred scene:** *"it's only Gen 2 Frames which give bad frame
sickness because they are biomechanical — the Gen 1 Frames don't give this problem. The only people
aware of Frame Sickness are Anabala and the higher up members of the Directorate. We'll tell the
players about frame sickness when we offer them Gen 2 Frames which is likely to be somewhere in
Episode 4-5 region of the adventure."*

**What was wrong.** Episode Two's Zero Six Hundred had Taka get up at five in the morning to warn the
party about Frame Sickness before their Gen 1 familiarisation. That was wrong three separate ways:
Gen 1 machines do not cause it, Taka has no way of knowing it exists, and it spends a reveal that
belongs two chapters later.

**Where the error came from, because it is a repeatable one.** `dossier-second-generation-frames.md`
§ Frame Sickness says it is "a well-known, in-fiction phenomenon — pilots are warned about it in
advance, it is not a hidden gotcha." That is true *of Gen 2 pilots at the point Gen 2 is issued*. It
was read as true in general and applied to a scene two chapters early. **A fact about who knows a
thing is not the same as a fact about when they learn it**, and the dossier stated the first without
the second.

**What the scene is now.** Taka still gets up at five, still pretends he happens to be there, and
still tells them the one thing nobody official will — it is just something a trainee one year into
Gen 1 heavy plant actually knows. Everybody over-grips the controls their first morning, everybody's
hands seize up by about eleven, and the second day is worse than the first. Not dangerous, only
embarrassing, and you cannot hold a spoon at dinner. The line about the second one being worse than
the first survived the correction intact, because it is true of muscle ache.

**An Episode One seed was removed rather than corrected.** The canteen had just gained a throwaway
Taka line planting the warning a fortnight early. There is nothing to seed, so it is gone.
