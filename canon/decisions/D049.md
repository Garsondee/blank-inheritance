1. **The country Kurose is in is Minkai.**
2. **Where Scene 0 first names Minkai, a bracket says it is Golarion's Japan.** It is the one real-world name in the speech. Ingram asked for it, so it is an exception to D024's rule against naming real places at the table, and the rule stands everywhere else.
3. **Whether Minkai is a kingdom or an empire now is not settled.** Golarion's own Minkai was an empire; older material calls the country "the kingdom". Scene 0 says neither. The country's facts now live in `world/minkai.md` (they were `world/kingdom.md`), and the question is `minkai.government`, to settle before The Road In's letter is rebuilt.
