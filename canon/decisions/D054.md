# D054 · 2026-09-30 · The campaign's visual language: teal-grey for authority, orange for life; conformity and sore thumbs

asked: nothing; this came in Ingram's /goal for the artwork round (art for every Episode 1 scene, haunt and NPC), after she had seen a first set of scene art.

## Ingram

> "If you see a chance to start building the visual appearance of a uniform then do that and then from that point onwards try to keep that sort of thing in mind (especially colour), I'd love to start seeing visual themes emerging and we're going for wonderfully contrast based world where the teal grey of our scenes represents the bureaucratic, dying, chiselled in stone power of authority and the orange warmth of the golden hour is a life giving warmth because it is. Some people who hide behind conformity and others who stand out like sore thumbs and end up sore because of it."

> "You can feel free to use any concept for characters ancestry wise, including inventing your own."

> "If you spot awkward forced catchphrases in NPC descriptions please find a more fun or subtle way to hint at the information that was previous being shouted."

Earlier, on a scene-art batch she had generated herself to show the house style: "All scene graphics should be 2:1 and we should try to follow roughly this art style."

## Settled

1. **Teal-grey is the colour of authority**: bureaucratic, dying, chiselled in stone. It fills institutional scenes and the walls behind institutional people.
2. **Orange golden-hour warmth is a life-giving warmth**, literally. It is the light on people, and it is what a place looks like when someone is looking after it.
3. **Some people hide behind conformity** (they wear the uniform as issued) **and others stand out like sore thumbs** and end up sore for it. Art should let the second kind be seen at once.
4. **Any ancestry is allowed for a character**, including ones Claude invents.
5. **Scene and location art is 2:1**, in the matte-painting, gouache-and-watercolour, "1990s Japanese animation background art" style, with no people in it.
6. **Forced catchphrases in NPC descriptions are replaced** by a fun or subtler way of showing the same thing.

## Changes

This decision is recorded with its own facts in canon/world/visual-language.md: the three principles are decided, and the uniform's details are Claude's proposal until she reads them. Fifty-four voice-note rewrites are listed, with before and after, in the review page. Location art was made for the eleven remaining haunt places, and twenty-eight haunt regulars were given portraits and NPC files.
