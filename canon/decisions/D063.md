# D063 · 2026-10-05 · The deck's skill keys: wider, a nameplate for the name, and a subtle colour for the attribute each adds

asked: Ingram asked for the skill keys on the main deck to be reshaped and recoloured, then, while it was being built, said how she wanted them laid out.

## Ingram

> "Skill roll buttons could do with having a wider ratio, a plate to show the text on (lots of text is cut off currently) and we should recolour them in a subtle way that indicates which main stat they are effected by so that they are grouped into sections with other skills which use the same stat like Wisdom or whatever."
>
> "No need to physically group them, no need to have a colour legend, avoid gaps or empty spaces since that's wasteful."
>
> "BY avoid gaps I mean there is no point physically grouping buttons thus causing some buttons to be blank right."

## Settled

1. **Each skill key is wider, with a nameplate for its name.** A recessed plate runs across the top of the key with the skill's whole name on it; below it sits the number that will be rolled, beside its proficiency pips. The longest name, "Performance", keeps about 10px to spare on its plate at the deck's authored size.
2. **A key is coloured, subtly, by the attribute its skill adds**, as sf2e's own skill table gives it: the plate is washed with the colour, and two ribbed grips, one at each end of the number's row, are painted in it. Strength is oxide red, Dexterity field green, Intelligence steel blue, Wisdom sea green, Charisma heather plum, and Constitution khaki (it has no skill; Fortitude's key in a check window wears it).
3. **The colour is the only grouping.** The keys stay in alphabetical order, A to M on the left bank and N to T on the right, nine to a bank, three by three, with no blank key and no colour legend. A hand on a key tells the central instrument which attribute it adds ("Untrained · Wisdom").
4. **The banks were widened to give the keys their width**: each black key bank from 272 to 304 authored pixels, the deck from 1600 to 1664, and the banks' own margins trimmed. A key went from about 73 by 40 pixels to 88 by 39.
5. **A player's check window uses the same keys.**
6. D062's tactile nubs and side ribs became the two ribbed grips.

## Claude's readings, marked

- **What the wider deck costs**: nothing where the deck is fitted by height (a 16:9 screen with Foundry's sidebar closed, or Ingram's 2560 by 1271 window). Where it is fitted by width, as on a 1920 by 1080 screen with the sidebar open, the whole deck draws about 4% smaller than before; the keys are still wider on screen than they were.
- **The colours** are muted service paints, mid-tone and greyed, chosen to stay apart from the deck's one warm accent (the orange of hover and firing) and from the check console's bright result lights.
- **Perception and the saves** are coloured only in a check window (Perception and Will Wisdom's, Reflex Dexterity's, Fortitude Constitution's). The deck's round defence buttons were not asked about and are unchanged.
- **The cut-off text**: in the local Foundry no skill name was cut off on the deck before this change, at Ingram's window or at 1920 by 1080; the plate keeps every name whole with room to spare either way.
- A first version grouped the keys into four attribute columns with named legends, which left two blank keys; it was replaced the same day, at Ingram's word, before it shipped.
