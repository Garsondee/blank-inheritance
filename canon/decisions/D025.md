# D025 · 2026-09-24 · The rift's path, the prophecy, and how Kurose sees the Directorate

source: split word for word from LEDGER #25

**The rift.** Spilled exotic matter — fuel for godlike technology — fed a violet-black portal
interface, which grew on the matter near it: slowly at first, with a rate of expansion that was itself
slowly increasing, in fits and starts that made it deadly to study. It absorbed many powerful clerics,
wizards and sorcerers trying to stop it, carved a jagged path across the landscape changing direction
seemingly at random, drew in the finest magic-users of Kortos's neighbouring states, and was finally
stopped having already partly entered Absalom, after cutting through a temple to Pharasma.

**The prophecy — the Directorate's reason for being.** *"Appalled at the devastation Rupiah asked the
gods if there would ever be another such rift created on Golarion and the answer was yes, in the city
of Kurose many hundreds of years in the future. Rupiah, Shucks, Hyuck and Anabala agreed to be there
when this event would unfold to prevent it happening a second time. That is the Directorate."*

**What the public believes it is for.** Both a grand mission against a foretold rift nobody expects to
see and a heavy-work corps everybody relies on — and people joke about the gap.

**Why Kurose, publicly.** The Directorate made Kurose: a fishing town until it arrived about a hundred
and fifty years ago; its money, harbour works and Frames built the modern city.

**How the founders' side is seen.** *"They are future saviours, they are meant to be saving the world
against something which hasn't happened yet and which cannot be precisely predicted in time."* Many see
them as intruders, outsiders, even warmongers, because they run a military base; some as protectors.
They operate outside the kingdom's national lines, grating on its army and navy — an extra-territorial
splinter in the side of a country that feels it can more than defend itself.
