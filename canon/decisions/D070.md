# D070 · 2026-10-07 · The whole story in one store, on one timeline: only Episode One and the induction are ink, everything else is pencil until Ingram goes through it, strict rules for where story material goes, and the present year is 4921 AR

asked: how to store, show and let Ingram edit the plot of the whole adventure along a horizontal timeline. Claude researched it and wrote a plan; she answered three questions while it was being planned, wrote notes on the first draft, and approved the second. The design is `design/plot-timeline.md`.

## Ingram

Her request:

> "Examine the Casebook. We have a plot which weaves a lot of things in and out, plots rising and falling, mysteries being revealed. A document is one way to display all of this but I'd like you to think about an alternative way to store, display and allow me to edit the plot of the entire adventure and I'd like it to be along a horizontal timeline where I can see the proposed layout of events chronologically, both past (lore/history), present (current material, at this point that only includes Episode 1) and finally our sketched out plans for the future. Do some research first, do some deep thinking, what's a fun interface that we could build with the goal of making it easy for me to read, understand and edit the contents manually and one that also works for you. How can we store this huge complex plot in a sensible way that means you can keep it all organised for me, avoiding documents going stale, making sure that when you reach for context you get all the pieces you need. I'm offering the chance to consolidate all the story elements of the adventure so think about what you'd do for that too. No work, just make a plan."

My thinking is that if you can find a better way to store the adventure and then create strict rules about how to store adventure story elements and ideas, brainstorming, lore discussions, things like that then we can get this under some sort of control."

She then approved the second draft as written.

## Settled

1. **The plot of the whole adventure is shown, read and edited on a horizontal timeline:** the past (lore and history), the present (Episode One) and the sketched plans for the future, in the order things happen.
2. **One store.** `canon/` is the only place a story fact is written. The timeline, the Casebook, Foundry's lore and Claude's context are all made from it.
3. **The timeline is a page on this PC**, served from the repo, with a read-only copy on claude.ai. **It may also come to live inside Foundry**, as the Location Editor did, so it is built to be able to.
4. **It looks like a clean planning tool**, in the Casebook's colours, without in-world dressing.
5. **All of the story material comes into the one store, consolidated into a more organised form.**
6. **Only Episode One and the induction are ink. Everything else is pencil until Ingram has gone through it.**
7. **There are strict rules for where story material goes:** story elements, ideas, brainstorming and lore discussions. They are the filing rules in `canon/README.md`.
8. **The present year is 4921 AR.**

## Claude's readings, marked

- **This amends D047.** Its item 9 stands: the Casebook stays, as the whole story to read like a document. The timeline is its picture, and the place to edit. Both are made from canon, so neither can run stale. D047's "triage in play order" (`design/story-data-refactor.md` §6.3) gives way to "all of it, in pencil": everything comes in now, and pencil is what Ingram goes through at her own pace. `canon/README.md`'s rule "decided by Ingram or plainly marked as waiting for her" stands, and pencil is the marking.
- **What counts as Episode One:** its scenes from Scene 0 to Flood Tunnel Clearance, the night's rests and the Magic Show; their checks, fight, hazard and objectives; and every fact they tell or rely on, the GM's secrets included. Episode One's play material outside the beats counts too, so two kinds of fact no beat cites yet stay ink: **`founders.level`** (Shucks's level-15 stat block in Intake Medicals and Assembly is built on it) and **the three island facts from her grading of The Road In** (D065: `directorate.island-ways-in`, `directorate.island-air-and-sea`, `directorate.island-security`). The six visual-language facts (D054) are on show from Scene 0 (`told: start`), so they count as told in Episode One.
- **What counts as the induction:** character creation, the backgrounds and classes, and FORM 1-B. As it stands it relies on no canon fact of its own. The backgrounds of Kurose's own proposed for it (D066) would rest on twelve facts, and Scene 0 tells all twelve.
- **"As they stand":** Episode One's facts keep their status. Its provisional facts (in scenes that wait for her full read) and its proposals stay what they are.
- **Her own earlier words outside Episode One go to pencil too**, marked `by: ingram`, as she asked: they wait for her second look like everything else. That includes the mayor lore she gave earlier today (D068). She can ink a whole group of her own words at once.
- **Open questions are neither ink nor pencil.** An open fact stays open wherever it is, because it is a decision she still has to make.
- **From now on,** lore she states is recorded the same turn in her words, and the facts that only restate them are ink: stating them is going through them. Whatever Claude reads into them, adds or proposes is pencil, `by: claude`, until she inks it. An idea she offers as an idea ("we could…") goes into `canon/ideas/` as a live idea. This is Claude's reading of "until I have a chance to go through it"; she can turn it round.
- **The year:** AR is Absalom Reckoning, the old campaign's calendar (its final date was 26 Sarenith 4721 AR). So the old campaign is exactly two hundred years back; `era.two-hundred-years` says "about two hundred years", which stays true and is not changed. Whether Kurose itself counts its years in AR, and whether any scene or paper shows the year, is not decided.
- **The pencil line, the formats and the page** are Claude's design (`design/plot-timeline.md`), not hers.
