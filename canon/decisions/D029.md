# D029 · 2026-09-24 · The old campaign is source material; the Inicrium battery was the shadow generator at Baldwin Manor; First Impact was at the generation ship

**Ingram, 2026-09-24, unprompted, while asking for the old Foundry server's data to be brought into the repo.** Not an answer to a question. It is logged here because it settles part of #6.

**1. The original campaign is source material.**

> "Grab everything you can and place it into a new directory within the ideas/lore side of this project and mark everything as source material from the original Abomination Vaults adventure which set the ground for this new adventure."

Done as `ideas/story/source-material/`. Its main folder, `the-trouble-with-mitflits/`, holds the founders' original PF2e campaign (Hyuck, Shucks, Anabala and Rupiah were its PCs). Source material is not canon: this ledger and the dossiers win wherever it disagrees.

**2. The Inicrium battery, Baldwin Manor, and First Impact.**

> "The Inicrium battery is an event which caused the Baldwin Manor to become overrun with Shadow creatures. Eventually they deactivated the battery which stopped the shadows from encroaching, this wasn't First Impact which happened later in the ruin of the first spaceship, the generation ship."

**What this adds to #6.** The "power generator" of #6 **is the Inicrium battery**. Inicrium is the old campaign's "solid magic potential", a magical battery material (`source-material/the-trouble-with-mitflits/journals/inicrium.md`). The exploiting lord's castle and manor house is **Baldwin Manor**, formerly Croglang Castle, the Baldwin family's seat at Volkheed on Kortos. The old campaign also has a Baldwin magical-weapons manufactory and a Baldwin who hunts mitflit "savants". Kept from #6: the town was overrun by shadow-plane creatures, the siege happened, a mitflit savant child built the device, and it is **a separate incident from First Impact**. #6 says "disable the generator by destroying it" and this entry says "deactivated the battery". The two are read as one event, with no contradiction intended.

**New:** First Impact happened **later**, "in the ruin of the first spaceship, the generation ship". The crashed spaceship at Kortos (`dossier-first-impact.md` §3) is the **generation ship**, the first spaceship.

**Not decided, and not invented here:**
- whether the rest of the Baldwin material (the family, the manufactory, William Charles Baldwin's hunters) carries over
- how long after the battery First Impact came
- what "first" spaceship implies about any others
- the old campaign's own "Kortos Cataclysm" (an Inicrium explosion a thousand years before play), which is a different event from both and is unplaced.
