Where two items disagree it is a conflict for her, never a silent pick.
- "Make other things work with it" means: the Approvals Desk reads canon's open facts, Sign-Off cites canon instead of the node registry, the open-questions file loses its story half, and the Casebook is generated from canon or retired. Each is its own change with its own check, in that spirit.
