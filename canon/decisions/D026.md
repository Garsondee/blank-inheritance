# D026 · 2026-09-24 · When the rift comes, the hull, famous founders, two services, and a starship final act

source: split word for word from LEDGER #26

**Asked 2026-09-24**, straight after #25. All four answers override older canon where they differ.

**When the rift comes.** *"Nobody knows because the conditions aren't right for it to happen (that's why
auger and fortune telling spells aren't able to produce a magical answer), but the players and the
antagonists will bring together the right mix of things for this event to occur."* About three-quarters
of the way through, a second rift opens, and this time it is unstoppable from here. The party must take
a spaceship to **the mitflit home world** and bring back the means to stop the exotic-matter runaway.
The cause is left open on purpose: Cicada's worst member detonating stolen mitflit spaceship fuel on
purpose, an accident, or a tragic misunderstanding. **The last quarter is a starship campaign** — the
players as command staff on an enterprise-sized intergalactic starship with a crew of thousands.
**GM-only: "Record this, but don't tell the players too much."**

**The hull** is under Kurose, and it is why the gods named Kurose.

**The founders are famous.** This overrides the "erasure" plant (`design/campaign-arc.md`, now marked);
the Episode One/Two content that depends on the four being nameless is listed there for revision with
Ingram. **A standing option, not scheduled:** *"if I get extremely ambitious with this adventure then we
may swap the players seamlessly from being in the future to doing stuff in the past as Rupiah, Hyuck,
Anabala and Shucks. Literally without warning, we drop them into a flashback where they are literally
playing PF2E rules… so we can have the players go through some of the founding of the organisation…
Huge fun!"*

**Two services.** The kingdom's national service (army and navy) is what everyone's parents did; the
Directorate recruits separately, by application or streaming, outside the kingdom's lines.
