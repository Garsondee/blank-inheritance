**Date:** 2026-09-19

> You made an error: The players will initially be given utility mechs and they will get to choose

**What this settles, corrected from #20:**

- **The party starts on Gen 1 — the classic cargo-cult fleet, utility-configured — chosen from a real
  range of different utility mechs.** Gen 1 already existed as "riveted plate, boiler technology"
  (`dossier-first-impact.md` §8); what's new is that it runs on an internal status ladder — utility
  work is the unglamorous, low-status assignment within Frame service itself, distinct from the
  base's actual combat-Frame corps, and it's given to candidates not yet considered skilled enough for
  "harder work." This is a SECOND, Frame-scale version of the same undervaluing Episode One already
  does at personnel scale (the storm drains) — not the same beat, but the same shape, recurring.
- **The Watcher probing the city's defences — not Shucks's discovery — is what pulls the party into
  their first real Frame combat.** It forces the Directorate to send out whatever's ready, utility
  mechs included, because there isn't enough combat-configured Frame capacity to spare. This is
  ordinary institutional logic under pressure, not the party being singled out — and it's a real,
  concrete engine for the campaign's own already-agreed Episode Two shape (`ch1-v2.md` §8: "a training
  exercise... interrupted by a real call-out... before they are ready"), which previously had that
  shape but no stated cause.
- **Shucks's DNA discovery does not change their assignment on the spot.** It's a long-burn plant
  (Mystery Ledger #1, Ch6 payoff, unchanged) that eventually explains why these six specifically get
  offered Gen 2 — much later, after real Frame-combat experience, not as an immediate reward.
- **Gen 2 is offered many levels later, and it's a genuine choice with real trade-offs, not a strict
  upgrade.** A heavily-upgraded Gen 1 Frame can be better in some ways than an early Gen 2 unit —
  deliberately, "just in case people become really attached to their mechs in their original forms."
  Both paths need to work. The exact mechanical shape of that trade-off is not decided — see the open
  question this adds.
