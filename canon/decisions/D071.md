# D071 · 2026-10-07 · The Casebook's outline comes into canon, all of it in pencil
kind: import

asked: nothing. This is Claude's record of bringing the hand-written Casebook outline (version 10, `tools/casebook/content/`) into canon, as D070 directs: "All of it, in pencil". It holds no words of Ingram's, and it is never authority for ink: everything it adds is pencil (`sketch`) or an open question.

## What came in

- **Chapters 2 to 20 as beats** (`plan/chapter-02.md` to `chapter-20.md`): every row of each chapter's table, every choice (the ten forks marked `fork: yes`), the Watcher's move, the flashbacks, and the question each chapter ends on.
- **The five Books** (`plan/books.md`), with the rank ladder folded in.
- **The world**: 26 new files (the generation ship, Mother, the mitflits and their home world, Sap, the lens, the column, exotic matter, the stray, the Envoy, Hori, Aoi, Baldwin, Abraxas, Nozomi, the four lieutenants, Taka, Nao, Rika, Bobbin, Hulda Brack, the Commander, Section Nine) and pencil added below the Pencil line of 20 existing ones.
- **Eleven threads** (`threads/`), from the weave, with clocks for the Watcher's generations, how it knows, the horror's three steps, Second Impact, the lieutenants' exits, the road to Cicada's author, the money, the flashbacks, Rand's paper trail and Clearance.
- **Twenty-two questions** (`questions/`): the five Book questions, the eleven conclusions and their three roads, and the Mystery Ledger's plants that Episode One carries.
- **Six ideas** (`ideas/`): the mayor's first appearance, a Book III flashback, three clues no built scene plants, and a reading of the title.

Everything is `by: claude`: the Casebook is Claude's wording, even where it retold Ingram's decisions. Where it cited one of them, the item cites it too, so `story context` puts her words beside Claude's. Every item says where it came from (`from: cb:...`), and `tools/story/import/casebook-coverage.mjs` proves every one of the Casebook's 729 items has a home or a reason below.

## Conflicts with ink, and how each was settled

Nothing that contradicts canon came in as a fact.

- **"In every shop window"** (Book II's breather, the glossary, Who's who) against D048's "some supporters' shop windows": kept only as the cut fact `founders.every-shop-window`, so the wording can't come back.
- **"Six newcomers arrive", "newcomers to Kurose, all volunteers"** against D065 (not all outsiders; none worked for the Directorate): kept only as the cut fact `party.all-newcomers`.
- **The Envoy "about fifteen years ago"** against the open `cicada.envoy-when`: the Envoy is dated with both candidates, and Chapter 2's old file says "an old incident".
- **FORM 1-B's "Clause 68.5"**: the agreement as written (drafted, switched off) has no such clause, so the number was left out.
- **"Four hundred candidates" at Assembly**, in Chapter 1's outline, against `directorate.intake-size` (about forty): Chapter 1 wasn't imported, so nothing came in.
- **Ranks written "Recruits · Clearance 0"**: rewritten with a comma, since "·" separates canon's fields.
- **The generation ship's date**: the sources say only "tens of thousands of years ago"; the timeline draws it at about 20,000.
- **Two Casebook facts that were really events** (Shucks explaining the marker, Gen 2 offered) are Chapter 4 beats; the marker itself stays the ink fact `directorate.the-marker`, revealed by a beat.

## Dates added to ink

Two ink facts gained a `when:` taken from their own words, nothing else changed: `directorate.founded-after` ("About five years after the rift") and `directorate.came-to-kurose` ("About a hundred and fifty years ago").
