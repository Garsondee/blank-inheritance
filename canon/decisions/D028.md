# D028 · 2026-09-24 · The Envoy, Aoi and the crime lords; the founders are alive; Scene 0 and player moments

source: split word for word from LEDGER #28

**Ingram, 2026-09-24, answering four questions while drafting Scene 0.** Authoritative over older wording.

**1. The Envoy incident — the inciting incident of the whole campaign.**

> "the incident with the alien sent by 'The Watcher', it's first tangible child, sent to make contact and shot at by a police officer. It was killed, but the police officer missed and hit the cultist standing behind the alien. That cultist was the partner/wife of Manako - it's this inciting incident which drives the rest of the plot and escalates everything. Manako's original aims and process had been peaceful. Due to an accident which no one planned for Manako's love is killed and he starts to fall in with bad crowds like the cities crime lords. Manako provides the crime lords with things, the crime lords give Manako the ways to get noticed. Manako start gathering a following because he can show people something that is really happening but only possible to discover using the same chemical he worked hard to understand originally."

**What this corrected in the older notes:** the round no longer "passes through the Envoy, which did not care" -- the Envoy was **killed**, and Hori's shot **missed** it and hit the woman standing behind it. **Aoi is Manako's wife** (she, not they), one of his small peaceful circle, who had come to meet it -- not an incidental bystander. Before the Pivot the circle was small and peaceful; the crime-lord trade and the real following come **after**. Kept: ~15 years ago, Hori drove her to hospital, Hori is the only person alive who knows her name, the Directorate filed her as an unnamed cultist casualty, and the Observer's lesson ("manifest, and they attack").

**Open, and recorded rather than invented:** what exactly Manako makes for the crime lords in the first half of that trade (`dossier-cicada.md` §14 Q6).

**2. The four founders are all alive, and on the base.**

> "it's a bit embarrasing having semi-immortal people hanging around so it's not waved in anyones faces. They are all alive, they all work on the base, they are all part of The Directorate except for Anabala and Shuck's Right Hand which are much more free agents. Rupiah works in the administration of the organisation, Shucks is head of medical and often involved in the science and Hyuck is the mad test pilot of some of the Directorates worst Frame concepts that never saw the light of day. A lot of this should be slowly revealed to the players, except for Shucks who gets revealed at the start."

**Corrects v4 §5, which said Hyuck was dead.** He is alive, and a test pilot. **Supersedes #27's namesake reading of Shucks:** the public knows the founders are long-lived and still about; Shucks is revealed to the players at the start (the opening speech names him, and his badge at Intake Medicals says MORDANT). #27's "famous names, sanitised faces" otherwise stands -- the pictures still show four tall humans, and the teacher is still sure the gnoll statue isn't Hyuck.

**3. "Name places."** Scene 0 names Golarion, Kortos, Absalom, Pharasma's temple and Kurose. The no-references rule is about the *real* world; in-world places are to be named.

**4. Scene 0, then each player's own opening.** A big speech to get players up to speed (`data/scene-briefs/episode-c1e1--from-up-here.json`), then The Road In goes round the table: how the character looks, how we first see them "as if they were joining the episode of a TV show", a small problem on the journey into the city, the skill they used, a roll against a normal DC, and a few sentences of how it unfolds, told by the player. **This is a standing pattern to use "on a fairly regular basis"** -- recorded as "Design pattern: player moments" in `design/campaign-arc.md`.
