## Settled

1. **Everything the GM reads in the Scene Runner puts the most important thing first**: in the scene, in each beat,
   in each field, in each sentence. It is written as an expert in adventure prep would write it for Ingram: concise,
   precise, plain, never rambling. The house style is `design/gm-facing-prose-style.md` §0.
2. **Episode One is rewritten as a whole, in that style.** Nothing in it is sacred and the existing scenes are a
   draft. Characters may be renamed, and new content and bridging material may be added, as long as the throughlines
   that move the plot keep going. This pass is not limited to one scene per output (D047 §1).
3. **Episode Two is a ghost.** Episode One owes it no continuity.
4. **The scenes are written for Ingram**, to help her run the campaign week after week: as a friend and advisor
   would prepare them.

Claude's reading of "with your recommendations", marked as a reading: the proposal's four open questions are taken as
answered yes. The budgets and the five note labels (Do, If…, Secret, Plant, Watch for) are the house style; the Frame
sighting in The Road In is cut; Episode One is rebuilt rather than given a quick tidy.
