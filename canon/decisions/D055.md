## Settled

1. **The induction form gains one page of questions addressed to the players themselves**, not to their characters, placed just before the end of the form (between Questions and Review).
2. **What the page asks:** the things the player does and doesn't enjoy in adventures; what they hope to do with their character; what sort of a force they want to be in the story; and things like those.
3. **What the page is for:** the players help define what a fun experience would be. The answers are input to how the GM builds the game around them.
4. **Induction by post is an option.** Ingram sends the players the questions (by Discord); they send back what they like and an image of their character; and they are signed up. It is for people who might struggle to do it on a computer in the moment. It goes on the checklist of things to do before launching the campaign, not into the build yet.
5. **A checklist of things to do before launching the campaign is to be produced**, and the by-post option is on it. It is `design/launch-checklist.md`.
