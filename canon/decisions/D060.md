## Settled

1. **A scene's people wait in the wings of its stage, on the GM's screen alone.** Everyone the scene's brief names who is not on the stage has a faded card standing where they would stand, and a face in a tray along the top of the stage. A click brings them on their own way; a right-click offers walking on quietly, being announced, or the splash; a drag onto the floor brings them on where they are dropped. Nothing about them reaches a player's screen until they are brought on.
2. **Anyone else is found from the tray**: anyone in any scene's cast, as that scene knows them, and any character or NPC in the world.
3. **A card's menu gives the GM two more things**: send them off (back to the wings, if their scene names them) and tell the table their name.
4. **The Scene Runner's On stage row, Summon anyone fold and Rules box are gone**, with the header's Summon and Rules keys.
5. **Rules in this scene**: the Runner offers every rule the scene's own words name (the creatures its fight brings, the conditions and actions its beats, checks and clock mention, rules such as difficult terrain and afflictions), each opening the system's own card or the SRD article. Any other rule is found in the Palette ("rule flanking"), where every condition, action and SRD rule is also a command.
6. **A cast member can be marked `present`**: already standing there when the scene is first opened.

What is read aloud, a person's lines and anything in quotation marks are not read for rules.
