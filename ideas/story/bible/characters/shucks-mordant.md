# Shucks Mordant

**Category:** character
**Status:** partial — his table-facing voice was fully built before his deeper history was known;
the two now need to be held together without letting the second change the first prematurely.
**Sources:** `data/scene-briefs/episode-c1s1--intake-medicals.json` (his full voice/cast entry),
`ideas/story/blank-inheritance-master-timeline.md`, `ideas/story/dossier-cicada.md` §6,
direct conversation, 2026-09-09.

## Read this first

Chief Medical Officer at Intake Medicals, already built and voiced: warm, unhurried, deadpan, two
hundred years old, gives instructions as single words ("Arm. Light. Breathe."), kind about things
that are dangerous to know and honest that he's forbidden from saying more. Found 2026-09-09, reading
the master timeline: **he is one of the Directorate's four actual founders**, present ~200 years ago
at the Kortos incident that produced the Watcher, and — separately, decades later — **he is on
Manako's revenge list**, named as "the institutional author of his ruin. Supervised the disgrace
personally." None of this should change how he's played in Session One. It's later information,
carried quietly, not a reason to make him cagey now — see the guardrail below, which mirrors Nobu's.

## What's established

- **Voice, already fully built** (`episode-c1s1--intake-medicals.json`): warm, unhurried, deadpan,
  asks a question and actually waits, gives practical instructions before kind ones and means both,
  compares candidates to relatives he processed decades ago, will not explain what the DNA flag means
  ("he is forbidden, he thinks the rule is stupid, and both show to anyone who reads him"). "Two
  hundred years old and has not run out of interest in people, which is the rarest thing about him."
- **One of the Directorate's four founders, ~200 years ago.** Anabala recruited him (with Hyuck and
  Rupiah) to intervene on behalf of an enslaved mitflit clan; that intervention is the Kortos incident
  that pulverises part of the buried ship into the Watcher. The four found the Directorate ~195 years
  ago specifically so the response is faster next time, then write and bury *children with guns*
  about themselves. *(`blank-inheritance-master-timeline.md`)*
- **On Manako's revenge list**, alongside Nobu: "SHUCKS MORDANT — the institutional author of his
  ruin. Supervised the disgrace personally." This places him as a direct participant in Manako's fall
  (§3 of the Cicada dossier), roughly 45 years ago — meaning he was already ancient when it happened.
  *(`dossier-cicada.md` §6)*
- **Not established either way: whether he knows he's a target**, or remembers Manako's disgrace as
  anything more than routine institutional business from decades ago. Don't assume; his existing
  voice reads as a man untroubled by anything in particular, which may simply mean he doesn't know.

## Tone and register — what NOT to do

**This is later-campaign information. Don't let it leak backward into how he's played now.** Same
principle as Nobu's file: he shouldn't read as a man with a secret or a man in danger during Session
One. His established warmth and deadpan calm are the correct read at Intake Medicals regardless of
what's coming — don't retrofit tension, wariness, or extra weight onto scenes already built.

**Don't assume he remembers or regrets supervising Manako's disgrace.** It may have been entirely
routine to him — an unremarkable disciplinary matter, one of thousands over two centuries — which
would itself be a real, sad detail (a moment that ended one man and barely registered for the other),
or it may weigh on him. Neither is decided. Don't write him guilty the way Nobu is guilty; that's
Nobu's register, not necessarily his.

**His founder status is not common knowledge to check against casually.** Nothing states whether the
city at large knows the Directorate had four founders, or that Shucks — the intake doctor everyone's
kid sees on day one — is one of them. Treat it as true but not necessarily publicly known unless a
scene establishes otherwise.

## Open questions

- Does he know he's on the revenge list?
- Does he remember Manako at all, and if so, how?
- What was his role in "supervising the disgrace" — a direct decision, a signature on someone else's,
  something else?
- What has he actually done for two hundred years besides staff intake medicals — is that his whole
  role in the Directorate, or one duty among others?
- How does founder-Shucks relate to founder-Anabala and founder-Hyuck now, if at all? Rupiah is
  independently confirmed still active (Founding Day appearances) — is Shucks in similar ongoing
  contact with the others, or estranged, or something else entirely?
