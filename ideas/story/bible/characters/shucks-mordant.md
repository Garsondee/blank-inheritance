# Shucks Mordant

**Category:** character
**Status:** partial — his table-facing voice was fully built before his deeper history was known;
the two now need to be held together without letting the second change the first prematurely.
**Sources:** `data/scene-briefs/episode-c1e1--intake-medicals.json` (his full voice/cast entry),

## Read this first

Chief Medical Officer at Intake Medicals, already built and voiced: warm, unhurried, deadpan, two
hundred years old, gives instructions as single words ("Arm. Light. Breathe."), kind about things
that are dangerous to know and honest that he's forbidden from saying more. Found 2026-09-09, reading
the master timeline: **he is one of the Directorate's four actual founders**, present ~200 years ago
at the Kortos incident that produced the Watcher, and — separately, decades later — **he is on
Manako's revenge list**, named as "the institutional author of his ruin. Supervised the disgrace
personally." None of this should change how he's played in Episode One. It's later information,
carried quietly, not a reason to make him cagey now — see the guardrail below, which mirrors Nobu's.

## What's established

- **Voice, already fully built** (`episode-c1e1--intake-medicals.json`): warm, unhurried, deadpan,
  asks a question and actually waits, gives practical instructions before kind ones and means both,
  compares candidates to relatives he processed decades ago, will not explain what the DNA flag means
  ("he is forbidden, he thinks the rule is stupid, and both show to anyone who reads him"). "Two
  hundred years old and has not run out of interest in people, which is the rarest thing about him."
- **One of the Directorate's four founders, ~200 years ago.** Anabala recruited him (with Hyuck and
  Rupiah) to intervene on behalf of an enslaved mitflit clan at Baldwin Manor. First Impact was a
  separate event, months later and many miles away, at the ruined generation ship, while the four were
  fetching exotic fuel for the mitflits who lived there; it tore the ship's navigation and sensor system
  loose as the Watcher (LEDGER #31). The Directorate was founded ~195 years ago to keep that site secure
  and prevent another rupture (LEDGER #32). *(`blank-inheritance-master-timeline.md`)* *(Corrected
  2026-09-25: this ran the Baldwin Manor rescue and First Impact together as one event, and restated the
  founding charter's black-bar answer, since cut, LEDGER #41.)*
- **Behind the Deputy Director's door, end of Episode Two** (LEDGER #43): he tells the Deputy Director that all six
  carry the marker, and asks that they be kept together and flying. He doesn't say what the marker is for.
  *(Accepted, LEDGER #46: he has recognised his own old handwriting on the override order, knows his detached hand wrote it,
  and takes responsibility for it. `bible/characters/rand.md`.)*
- **On Manako's revenge list**, alongside Nobu: "SHUCKS MORDANT — the institutional author of his
  ruin. Supervised the disgrace personally." This places him as a direct participant in Manako's fall
  (§3 of the Cicada dossier), roughly 45 years ago — meaning he was already ancient when it happened.
  *(`dossier-cicada.md` §6)*
- **Not established either way: whether he knows he's a target**, or remembers Manako's disgrace as
  anything more than routine institutional business from decades ago. Don't assume; his existing
  voice reads as a man untroubled by anything in particular, which may simply mean he doesn't know.
- **He is literally an animated skeleton — confirmed 2026-09-10, not a figure of speech.** Started as
  a rogue with real medical skill, and became Dr. Shucks Mordant on the strength of it. Every kid who
  meets him at Intake Medicals sees an actual walking skeleton in a white coat and stethoscope — the
  portrait already assigned to him (`artwork/character-portraits/shucks-mordant.png`) is literal, not
  stylized. His "two hundred years old" line is not an idiom either.
- **The right hand is a detached familiar, and doesn't know it's supposed to be his.** Some while ago
  he put his hand into a beam of powerful necromantic energy; the hand survived the experience by
  becoming its own thing — a small, independent, commandable familiar, and (as an emergent property of
  whatever that beam actually was) **it can fly, using a kite.** It is not currently attached to his
  arm: in-universe, his sleeve currently ends in a **very convincing prosthetic**, indistinguishable at
  a glance. **A Perception or Medicine check on meeting him is the honest way a table notices** — this
  is a real, checkable detail, not narration the GM just hands out.
- **The hand has gone its own way, and Shucks doesn't mind.** It is, in his own words effectively,
  "a conspiracy theorist" — investigating what's actually happening in the city entirely on its own
  initiative, without his help or knowledge, and **unable to talk normally** (how it communicates, if
  at all, is open — see below). He doesn't know where it currently is. It always comes back eventually,
  once hungry, the way a stroppy cat does. He is genuinely unworried about the whole arrangement.
  **Its name is Rand** (Ingram, LEDGER #34: *"Right hand... Rand... Get it?"*). **It writes**, in Shucks's own
  handwriting from before it came off; he writes with his left hand now. It is who countersigned Section Nine's roster
  (`arc-the-countersignature`; Claude's answer at Ingram's request, accepted in LEDGER #46). Full file: `rand.md`.

### The Hand — a sub-character in its own right

A small, mobile, nonverbal investigator, independently pursuing its own theory of what's going on in
the city, with no loyalty check-in to Shucks and no coordination with the party unless a scene puts
them in its path. Its "conspiracy theorist" framing plus genuine access (it's been loose in the city,
unsupervised, for an unstated length of time) makes it a plausible source of a real, oddly-specific
scrap of true information at an unexpected moment — flying past on a kite, or turning up somewhere it
has no business being. Not built as a scene or an NPC stat block yet; flagged here so it isn't lost,
and as a candidate for its own file if it becomes a recurring presence rather than a standing detail
about Shucks. **It now has its own file, `rand.md`** (LEDGER #34), because as the countersignature's author it recurs.

## From the old campaign (source, not canon)

*Added 2026-09-24 (LEDGER #29–#31). Shucks was a player character in the original campaign, and **Craig**, who
played him, is at the new table and plays him again in any flashback. The record lives in
`ideas/story/source-material/the-trouble-with-mitflits/player-characters/shucks-mord.md` and `shuck-s-right-hand.md`.*

- **Shucks Mord**, "Mordant" on his token and in this campaign. A skeleton rogue with the Tomb Born background, a
  ruffian who later took a Ghost dedication (ghostly flight and grasp). He carried "Dr Wint's Patented Battle
  Medicine", and had Undead and Subterranean lore.
- **The Hand** became a character of its own in the old campaign's twelfth session ("Shuck's Gets Given a Hand"): a
  scout and an agent of chaos. The table remembers it grabbing faces. It fits this campaign's version, the detached
  conspiracy theorist investigating the city on its own.
- **He was at First Impact**, fetching exotic fuel for the mitflits who lived beside the generation ship, and at
  Pharasma's temple when the four stopped the rupture (`ideas/story/dossier-first-impact.md`).
- **Revealed at the start.** Of the four founders, he is the one the players meet openly on day one, and his player
  is at the table when they do (LEDGER #28, #31).

## Tone and register — what NOT to do

**This is later-campaign information. Don't let it leak backward into how he's played now.** Same
principle as Nobu's file: he shouldn't read as a man with a secret or a man in danger during Episode
One. His established warmth and deadpan calm are the correct read at Intake Medicals regardless of
what's coming — don't retrofit tension, wariness, or extra weight onto scenes already built.

**Don't assume he remembers or regrets supervising Manako's disgrace.** It may have been entirely
routine to him — an unremarkable disciplinary matter, one of thousands over two centuries — which
would itself be a real, sad detail (a moment that ended one man and barely registered for the other),
or it may weigh on him. Neither is decided. Don't write him guilty the way Nobu is guilty; that's
Nobu's register, not necessarily his.

**His founder status is known, and not advertised (decided 2026-09-24, LEDGER #28).** The city knows the four
founders by name and knows they are long-lived and still about the base; nobody makes a fuss of it, because
having semi-immortal people hanging around is faintly embarrassing. **He is the one founder revealed to the
players at the start**: the opening speech names him, and his badge at Intake Medicals says MORDANT. If asked,
he confirms it deadpan and gets on with the medical. He is head of medical and often involved in the science.
His detached right hand is a free agent, like Anabala.

**Being a literal skeleton doesn't change the tone rule.** His warmth and deadpan calm are still the
correct read — a kind, unsettling-looking mentor, not a horror element. The "kids notice the prosthetic
isn't real" beat should land as a small, checkable oddity in an otherwise gentle scene, the same
register as everything else already built at Intake Medicals, not a jump-scare.

- What has he actually done for two hundred years besides staff intake medicals — is that his whole

  grounding `ideas/story/bible/locations/general-hospital.md` (the fortress island's main hospital,

  otherwise; `data/locations/general-hospital.json`'s `npcRefs` is a one-line change either way.
  **One partial answer authored 2026-09-21, and it is a small one:** he teaches a field medicine
  class at the cadet school on Tuesday afternoons
  (`data/scene-briefs/episode-c1e2--school-period-two.json`). It is presented as entirely
  unremarkable — nobody in the fiction finds it notable that the intake doctor also takes a lesson —
  and it exists for a structural reason rather than a lore one: an ordinary teaching Shucks in the
  afternoon is what makes him walking unannounced into the Deputy Director's office at the end of
  that episode land as strange. It answers "one duty among others" for exactly one duty and leaves
  the rest of the two hundred years as open as it was.
- How does founder-Shucks relate to founder-Anabala and founder-Hyuck now, if at all? Rupiah is
  independently confirmed still active (Founding Day appearances) — is Shucks in similar ongoing

  this adventure, so it can't be something he stumbled into.)*
