# William Charles Baldwin

**Category:** character (antagonist)
**Status:** partial. That he is alive and what he does are settled (LEDGER #32). How he stays alive, and when he is
revealed, are open.
**Sources:** `ideas/story/bible/LEDGER.md` #29, #31, #32; the old campaign's record
(`ideas/story/source-material/the-trouble-with-mitflits/`: `npcs/the-hunters.md`, `npcs/the-baldwin-family-and-allies.md`,

## Read this first

**GM-only, and a secret kept until the right moment** (Ingram, LEDGER #32). Nobody at the table learns that William
Charles Baldwin is alive until the campaign chooses to reveal it. His name, his face and his survival are all held
back; his organisation can be felt long before he is.

**He is alive two hundred years later**, like the four founders, and for a similar reason: he could afford to be. He
is a **criminal lord who arms criminal gangs with incredible weapons, and those weapons are still made by essentially
enslaved mitflits.** The four founders (Rupiah, Shucks, Anabala and Hyuck) are his natural enemies, and **the moment
he reveals himself is personal for the players**: four of them played those four in the original campaign.

## What's established

- **The old enemy.** In the original campaign he led the hunters who captured mitflit savants for their minds, and ran
  the Baldwin family's magical-weapons manufactory at Baldwin Manor (formerly Croglang Castle, at Volkheed on Kortos).
  His note, carried by the mage Regina Key and signed "WCB", read: *"Mitflits are there in force now, including many
  'savants' ripe for the taking. I am gathering forces for a procurement operation."*
- **The grudge.** At Baldwin Manor an Inicrium battery pulled the Shadow Plane into the town, a shadow siege followed,
  and **members of his own family were killed by accident, not by the four.** He blamed the four anyway. Written on
  the old campaign's map, in a Baldwin's voice: *"These criminals broke into my ancestral home. They slaughtered my
  family or had some part in their demise. They have taken things from this house that don't belong to them. That's
  my fucking horse's leather on that Gnoll."* (Hyuck had paid a thief to steal a hunter's horse and had its hide made
  into armour.)
- **The last thing the old table saw of him** was an army prepared on the Gauntlight map, hunters, infantry, archers
  and mobs, set against the mitflits in the keep. That battle was never played (`open-questions.json#gauntlight-siege-outcome`).
- **Alive, by wealth** (Ingram): "He was insanely rich, that might give him access to ways to stay alive."
- **What he is now:** a criminal lord. He arms criminal gangs with incredible weapons, **built by essentially enslaved
  mitflits.** It is the same trade as two hundred years ago, grown.
- **Why mitflits, still** (Ingram, LEDGER #32): *"Mitflits have this horrible characteristic, they are both extremely
  helpful when given the right tasks and also fascinated by the production of technology."* He knows that better than
  anyone alive.

## Proposals (LEDGER #32, veto freely)

- **How he stays alive.** He was on Kortos when First Impact tore the generation ship open, with hunters, money and a
  weapons works on the island, so he was among the first to loot the site. Ship technology (medical, repair nanites)
  keeps his body going, and his mitflit slaves, fascinated by it, keep that technology running. Alternatives: bought
  magic, or a bargain with something. (`open-questions.json#baldwin-longevity`)
- **He is the crime lord Manako works for**, at arm's length, as Manako's slide requires. Nobu's laundering, the
  Fixer's "crime partners" and the heist crews all run through his organisation. Manako doesn't know who is at the top.
- **His workshops feed on the gathering.** The mitflits arriving in Kurose to all be in one place when they go home
  are poor, badly housed and eager to be useful: the easiest prey he has ever had.
- **His front trades under the old castle's name: Croglang.** Players who remember the old campaign will know it long
  before any NPC says "Baldwin".
- **The weapons.** Personnel-scale gang weapons far beyond the era, built from leaked ship technology by mitflit
  hands. That makes them a steady supply of good SF2E fights, and each captured weapon is evidence of where it was
  made.

## The reveal

**Keep it until the right moment.** Signs can build long before, each deniable on its own: impossible weapons in gang
hands, missing mitflits from the gathering, a front company called Croglang, and a crime lord nobody has ever seen.
When he does reveal himself, the four founders will know him on sight, and so will their players.

## Ties

- **The four founders:** natural enemies. For Hyuck it's also about a horse.
- **Anabala:** he enslaved her people then, and he enslaves them now.
- **Manako:** his chemist, at arm's length, neither fully knowing the other (proposal).
- **The mitflits:** the gathering is his supply. Bobbin, who remembers "the lord's workshop", remembers **him**.
- **The Watcher:** open. If he runs on ship technology, it may already be able to see through him.
