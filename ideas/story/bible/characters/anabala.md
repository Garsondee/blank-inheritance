## Read this first

**GM-only, and the most dangerous secret in the founders' file.** Anabala is a very early mitflit who has lived
through the ages because she carries **the knowledge demon Abraxas**, who is female too (LEDGER #32 item 10). The
demon is **not mentioned openly for a long time** (Ingram, LEDGER #31). Nothing at the table names Abraxas, hints at
her, or lets an NPC know of her, until the party causes her to surface, and **Anabala must be there when that happens**
(LEDGER #32 item 14).

**Her old enemy is alive too** (LEDGER #32, GM-only, secret until the right moment): see "Baldwin" below.

## What's established

- **One of the four founders**, and the one who recruited the other three: moved by what a human lord (William
  Charles Baldwin) was doing to her people, she brought Hyuck, Shucks and Rupiah to intervene. Every downstream
  catastrophe traces back to a request she made in good faith (v4 §14). *(LEDGER #32: Baldwin is still alive, GM-only;
  see "Baldwin" below.)*
- **A very early mitflit.** She is one of the earliest of her people after the generation ship fell, far older than
  First Impact. She remembers more of what the mitflits were than anyone alive.
- **High Priestess and Prophet of the mitflits**, and the only one who knows everything about where they came from.
  A few of her priestesses know part of it; everyone else gets the piece they are ready for (LEDGER #15).
- **Wants her people home.** The gathering in Kurose, mitflits arriving to all be in one place when they leave, is her
  long plan (`bible/species/mitflits.md`). *(Rupiah's diplomacy is why the gathering can happen at all, where others met
  mitflits with violence (LEDGER #8, `mitflits.md`); the plan to bring them home is Anabala's.)* **Anabala works with the Directorate, and has her own ultimate objective:
  using mitflit technology to get a way home for her people.** Building Gen 2 for the Directorate was her price for
  access to the original ship's technology, which the Directorate keeps highly restricted from the rest of Golarion so
  it can't destabilise the world (LEDGER #34). See "Gen 2" below. What the way home actually is has not been decided.
  *(Proposal, LEDGER #34, veto freely: perhaps the search ship under Kurose, which lifts at Chapter 15 in the
  twenty-chapter campaign draft, or perhaps the shuttle under Kortos, `open-questions.json#kortos-shuttle`.)*
- **Built Gen 2** (LEDGER #32 item 7). **Gen 2 is Anabala building mechs from actual mitflit technology.** Gen 2 is
  built from the generation ship's technology: the original ship, the First Impact crash site at Kortos, which the
  Directorate holds and keeps restricted from the rest of Golarion (LEDGER #20, #34). *(Corrected 2026-09-24, LEDGER
  #34: this said the search ship under Kurose. That was Claude's inference; Ingram's own words put Gen 2's technology at
  the First Impact crash site (#20) and in "the original ship" (#34).)* She could fly what she built when almost nobody
  else could, and even she suffered Frame Sickness. The Directorate's record of those trials is thin on exactly who
  succeeded (`dossier-second-generation-frames.md` §3). See "Gen 2" below. *(Corrected 2026-09-24, LEDGER #32: this
  read "Proved Gen 2 could work", as though she were its first pilot only. She is its builder.)*
- **Today: a free agent who works with the Directorate.** She is not part of the Directorate, unlike the other three
  founders (LEDGER #28), but she works with it, and she has her own ultimate objective (LEDGER #34; the first bullet
  above). The old friendship with Shucks still holds: easy, affectionate, and about terrible things (v4 §2).

## Gen 2: she built it

Ingram, verbatim (LEDGER #32, Casebook N4):

> "Gen 1 mechs are Golarion technology only, informed by looking at the insanely futuristic godlike spaceship tech
> uncovered. Gen 2 mechs are Anabala trying to use actual Mitflit tech to build mechs, which is something we shouldn't
> confuse between the two. Gen 1 aren't biomechanical, they are just mechanical and clankers and hard to work with.
> Gen 2 is built on the back of the real tech so more like being inside a giant humanoid, more like EVA and less Giant
> Robo like."

And on why she built it for the Directorate (LEDGER #34, Casebook N25):

> "Anabala works with the Directorate but also has her own ultimate objective, using Mitflit technology to get a way
> home for her people. Making Gen 2 Frames for the Directorate gave her access to the tech from the original ship,
> something which is highly restricted to the rest of Golarion to stop it having a destabilising effect on the world."

- **Gen 2 is hers.** She built mechs from her own people's technology. Gen 2 is built from the generation ship's
  technology: the original ship, the First Impact crash site at Kortos, which the Directorate holds and keeps restricted
  from the rest of Golarion (LEDGER #20, #34). Gen 1 is Golarion's, informed only by looking at that same ship. Never
  confuse the two. *(Corrected 2026-09-24, LEDGER #34: this said the search ship under Kurose. That was Claude's
  inference; Ingram's own words put Gen 2's technology at the First Impact crash site (#20) and in "the original ship"
  (#34).)*
- **The bargain** (LEDGER #34). Building Gen 2 for the Directorate was her price for access to the original ship's
  technology, which the Directorate keeps highly restricted from the rest of Golarion so it can't destabilise the
  world. That is how her machines became the Directorate's programme: LEDGER #20 has the programme as the Directorate's
  own, and #32 makes the machines hers. Her own objective runs alongside the bargain: a way home for her people.
- **What only Gen 2 has:** a living core, a seat that shapes itself to its pilot's true form (LEDGER #5), the "metal
  fatigue" breach, and Frame Sickness. Only Anabala and senior Directorate know Frame Sickness exists (LEDGER #23).
- **It nearly failed.** Almost nobody else could fly it, and the programme was mothballed (LEDGER #20) until Shucks's
  screen found the pilots who could.
- **What the Gen 2 core is** (LEDGER #34, Casebook D13). The Gen 2 core is biomechanical mitflit technology: robotic
  parts that are living flesh melded with technology. It is not a living mitflit (LEDGER #34). These mechs aren't usable
  by just anyone. She built it, so she knows what it is. Her own people react to it: wild mitflits are uncomfortable
  around Gen 2 Frames, and mitflits don't instantly understand where Gen 2 comes from (LEDGER #34). *(Answered
  2026-09-24, LEDGER #34: this said what the core is stays open, as the Casebook's D13 held by
  `bible/species/mitflits.md`'s `arc-mitflit-frame-core-link`, and not to write her as knowing the answer until it was
  decided. That Node is now closed.)*
- **Maybe a small part of Mother is in every Gen 2**, the "cerebral glue" that makes the technology work. "Maybe" is
  Ingram's word, and it is open (`open-questions.json#mother-in-gen2`). Mother is the old campaign's incorporeal
  construct, perhaps the generation ship's reasoning core (`open-questions.json#mother-and-the-watcher`). *(Proposal,
  LEDGER #34, veto freely: if so, Anabala took the pieces from the generation ship's reasoning core, under the access
  she bargained for.)*
- *(Proposal, LEDGER #34, veto freely.)* **Flashback Two moves to her.** In `blank-inheritance-v4.md`'s Chapter Six,
  Flashback Two is the old party learning what the core is and choosing to bury it, two hundred years ago. The proposal
  moves it to when Anabala built Gen 2, decades ago: the day she decides what the Directorate will be told about the
  core.
- Full treatment: `ideas/story/dossier-second-generation-frames.md`.

## Abraxas (GM-only)

Ingram, verbatim (LEDGER #31):

> "Anabala was a very early Mitflit and because of her connection to the knowledge demon Abraxas she lived through the
> ages. At first the demon overpowered Anabala and for many decades the demon was fully in control, eventually Anabala
> fought back and turned the tables and made the demon subservient by making it clear that the only path to unlimited
> information was through Anabala's cooperation, not conquest. Eventually they would have an uneasy alliance with
> Anabala wanting to return the Mitflits home and the demon wanting to learn everything it could, literally feeding on
> that information and becoming more powerful."

- **The demon ruled her for many decades** before she turned the tables. She did it with an argument, not a fight:
  the only road to unlimited information runs through her cooperation.
- **The alliance is uneasy.** Anabala wants her people home. Abraxas wants to know everything, and grows by feeding on
  information. Their interests overlap for now.
- **Earlier canon** (LEDGER #7) said the demon searched "the mind of the mitflit", found the mitflits could be a
  powerful people again, and wants them to succeed. That still fits: a knowledge demon with a whole species' memory to
  feed on has good reason to want that species to flourish.
- **She is female** (LEDGER #32 item 10, resolved 2026-09-24). Ingram: *"In our version, she, Anabala is played by a
  woman who wants to RP as a woman (Anabala) who has a pact with a demon (Abaraxas) and they are all female in this
  version."* Write Abraxas as she/her. *(Corrected 2026-09-24: this read "The pronoun is open", since LEDGER #7 called
  the demon female while Paizo usually writes Abraxas as male, and this file said "it" until Ingram decided.
  `open-questions.json#abraxas-pronoun` is resolved.)*

  The demon comes out; the character stays Pippa's. So the emergence takes nothing away from a player, even if Pippa

### The aligned pair

Ingram: *"The demon and The Watcher are naturally an extremely dangerous yet aligned pair, we need to remember that."*
A mind that feeds on knowing everything, next to a swarm that sees everything (`dossier-observer.md` §18). If Abraxas
gets out, the two find each other. **Plan every late-campaign beat with that pairing in mind.**

## Baldwin: the old enemy, and the present one (GM-only)

**William Charles Baldwin** had her people making weapons at Baldwin Manor, and she brought the other three founders
to stop him. **He is alive** (LEDGER #32 item 1), and a secret kept until the right moment
(`open-questions.json#baldwin-reveal`). He is now a criminal lord who arms criminal gangs with incredible weapons,
**still made by essentially enslaved mitflits**: her people, again. The four founders are his natural enemies, and she
is the one whose people he is still using. Ingram names the trait he exploits: *"Mitflits have this horrible
characteristic, they are both extremely helpful when given the right tasks and also fascinated by the production of
technology."*

- *(Proposal, LEDGER #32, veto freely.)* The mitflits gathering in Kurose to go home, her own long plan, are easy prey
  for his workshops.
- Whether she knows he is alive is not stated.
- His file: [`william-charles-baldwin.md`](william-charles-baldwin.md).

## From the old campaign (source, not canon)

- **Anabala Macthata**, a mitflit (Ifrit heritage). She was first built as a "strange robot" before being rebuilt as a
  mitflit, which suits "star-faring robots originally" (LEDGER #6).
- A pact-broker. The line the table remembers: *"I can find things and I can make things disappear. You owe me one
  favour."* Her player sketched a homebrew pact-binder with a four-stage curse (`journals/abraxa.md`), an obvious
  seed for the demon. *(Her old sheet's Silent Heart tattoo, a Paizo item of Abraxas's faith, says "his" for the demon.
  That is Paizo's pronoun, not this campaign's.)*
- Air-and-fire kineticist powers, Loremaster and Investigator training, Legal and Mitflit lore. She speaks Androffan
  among many languages, and has the ancestry feat Nanite Surge. She shrinks things into bottles.
- **She ran the mitflit government** at Gauntlight Keep.
