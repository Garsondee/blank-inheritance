## Read this first

The island's combined secondary school and technical college — Ingram's own framing, "a Tokyo
style dense inner city urban university and high school combination." Rank 2: the second most
important structure named so far, after Central Fortification. Site tier, standing depth, in
Island Centre (`item-176`), the fortress island's own downtown.

The real-world footprint this was adopted from reads as a large retail box (flagged in the
draft note as `[real-world: IKEA]`, with an explicit instruction not to let that show). Nothing
in the promoted lore references retail, furniture, or anything adjacent — the size and plain
rectangularity are reused as "a dense campus stacked to fit an urban block," not explained away.

## What's established

- **Two institutions sharing one building.** High-school-age students hold the lower floors; a
  technical/university tier above trains specialists. They share a front door and a timetable,
  and otherwise mix as little as the school can arrange.
- **This is where ordinary academic streaming happens** — the exact mechanism `design/induction.md`
  names as the cover for something else ("being streamed by aptitude is what schools do... the
  mystery moves to the right place"). The registrar's second-floor office holds the real test
  data behind every placement. The location's own GM-only text is deliberately restrained here:
  it states the mechanism exists and that nobody currently cross-checks it, and leaves whether/when
  a pattern actually gets noticed as a table-level call, not a scripted beat.
- **Consistent with the island's military/school split** (LEDGER #13): this building is the
  school end, not sited with the causeway/Roadgate/hangar cluster. Nothing in its lore places it
  at that end or blends the two registers into one location.
- **Two placeholder NPCs**, per standing convention (unnamed, uncast): `school-registrar` (runs
  placement testing, holds the real archive) and `school-history-teacher` (teaches the one class
  built to double as the campaign's own backstory delivery, if that still-unaccepted idea from
  `ideas/entries/school-schedule-system.json` gets picked up later — this NPC exists so that idea
  has someone to be taught by, without the class-period system itself being assumed built).
- **History section:** built as two separate institutions merged into one block when the island
  ran out of room to expand sideways — a physical detail (a half-flight of stairs correcting a
  floor-level mismatch) rather than an abstract fact, so a GM has something concrete to describe.
- **Consequences section:** a scandal or a wrong result here reaches every family on the island
  within the week, because every family's children pass through the same building — the
  mechanism by which anything that happens here has campaign-wide reach without needing an
  authored plot to carry it there.
