## What this is, and why it doesn't bend

This is the single most load-bearing document in `bible/`. Everything else here — species, characters,
locations — is a write-up compiled from sources. **This file is one of the sources.** When a question
gets asked directly and answered directly, the answer becomes real the moment it's given, and this is
where that moment is recorded so it is never lost, never re-asked, and never silently contradicted by
a later guess.

**Why this exists at all:** the mitflit incident that started `bible/` in the first place (see its
README) was a *guess* standing in for an answer nobody had actually gotten. This file is the opposite
failure mode closed off — once a question has been asked and answered here, there is no excuse left for
guessing at it again.

**The rules — read these before touching this file:**

1. **Check here before asking anything.** If a question this specific has already been asked, the
   answer is below. Asking it again wastes Ingram's time and, worse, risks getting a slightly different
   answer the second time and now having two contradicting "truths" on record.
2. **Log a question here the moment it's asked** — verbatim, not paraphrased, under a new entry with
   today's date. If the answer isn't back yet, that's fine; the question still gets its place in order.
3. **Log the answer here the moment it's given** — verbatim, or, if very long, faithfully transcribed
   in full rather than summarized. A summary loses exactly the texture that makes an answer specific
   enough to write from.
4. **Never edit a past entry's question or answer text.** This is a historical record, not a living
   document to tidy up. If a later answer changes or supersedes an earlier one, that is itself a new,
   dated entry — cross-reference the old one, and mark the old one **SUPERSEDED**, but never rewrite or
   delete what it originally said.
5. **Every entry gets a "Filed to" line** — which bible files, dossiers, or data files were actually
   updated as a result. An answer that lives only in this ledger and nowhere else is half-filed.
6. **If something written elsewhere in the repo ever contradicts an entry here, this file wins.** Fix
   the other file; do not "fix" the ledger to match a guess.

### 1 — Shucks Mordant's physical body

**Date:** 2026-09-10
**Asked because:** his portrait (a bare skeleton) had been assigned from the art in-tray, but nothing
in the bible said whether that was literal or stylized — a real, immediate risk of writing him wrong at
the table.

**Q:** *"Shucks Mordant's portrait (which I assigned from the in-tray) is a literal bare skeleton in a
lab coat and stethoscope. The bible file never says whether that's how he actually looks in-fiction. At
Intake Medicals, do the kids see an actual visible skeleton, or is 'skeletal physician' just
character-voice shorthand for extremely old and gaunt?"*

**A:** *"Literally an animated skeleton who was a rogue but also had a lot of medical skill, ended up
being called Dr Shucks Mordant. Decided to put a hand into a beam of powerful necromatic energy and I
house ruled it that the hand became a detached familiar that Shucks could command to do things. It
gained the ability to fly by using a kite. In this universe it's not on Shuck's actual arm (Shuck's
currently has a very convincing prosthetic, add this as a perception or medical skill role when meeting
them) and I have it in my head that Shuck's right hand is actually now a conspiracy theorist (whilst
being completely unable to talk normally) who is trying to uncover what is happening in the city
without the help of Shucks. Shucks seems unworried about this state of affairs, he is unaware of the
hands location but like a stroppy cat it'll always come back at some point once hungry."*

**Filed to:** `ideas/story/bible/characters/shucks-mordant.md` (full new section + open questions about
the hand's future); `artwork/character-portraits/shucks-mordant.png`'s assignment confirmed accurate,
not stylized.

### 2 — Nozomi and the party's early activation

**Date:** 2026-09-10
**Asked because:** the master timeline's one-line entry ("cannot get back in the machine") didn't say
whether her situation was the actual cause of Day 1's timing or an unrelated parallel thread.

**Q:** *"The timeline says Nozomi, 'slated as the presumptive first pilot,' attends a targeted magic
show weeks before Day 1 and 'cannot get back in the machine afterward.' Is this the actual reason the
six PCs are being recruited right now — they're filling the hole she left — or is her situation a
separate thread that doesn't directly explain the party's recruitment?"*

**A:** *"It links into the way The Watcher cult works. The city has people performing 'magic shows'
privately because showy magic is frowned upon except in an emergency in my version of Golarion and
Japan. So people go to little back street shows to have highly powerful illusionists cast spells around
them and enjoy the equivalent of a 3D immersive film. It's considered a guilty pleasure but most people
in the city will have been to at least one in their lifetime, some even do more religious themed ones
that can be done publicly during high religious holidays.
So... our pilot goes to one of these shows but they are actually attending a recruitment drive by the
cultists. They reveal The Watcher who is filling the room, then they demonstrate without a trace of a
doubt that the thing they can see is real and not illusion. But they then tell the people in the crowd
that they have a choice, they can leave and tell people what they've seen here or they can stay and
learn more. Our pilot stayed, heard them out, was specifically told by a high level cultist that they
were working for the wrong side and then the next day they turn off their beeper / phone and avoid
being drafted in to fight. The group goes into action earlier than they normally should. The other
pilot ends up in questioning and temporary detainment, that's an event where someone could go and talk
to them and find out a little information from someone who is begrudging but willing to say a little
about what they saw because they've already had to say all of this to their questioners. The skill here
is talking to them in a way that doesn't ruffle their feathers, otherwise they will go quiet and won't
cooperate."*

**Filed to:** `ideas/story/bible/characters/nozomi.md` (new file); `ideas/story/dossier-cicada.md` §7
(the magic show's real "leave or stay" choice mechanic, folded in as a general recruitment-show rule,
not just Nozomi's case); `ideas/story/blank-inheritance-master-timeline.md`'s "Weeks before Day 1" row
expanded.

**A:** *"Alien robotic crew members who crashed on Golarion thousands of years ago. They dispersed
and they forgot their skills. They became weaker and more primitive and more violent. But small
communities of Mitflits did manage to survive in some places and keep alive the old ways and the

self interested but not evil and wants the Mitflits to succeed, Anabala is after all considered a
high priestess and prophet of the Mitflits."*

**Filed to:** `bible/species/mitflits.md` ("What's established", "Ingram's own take" section rewritten
