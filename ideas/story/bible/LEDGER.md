## What this is, and why it doesn't bend

This is the single most load-bearing document in `bible/`. Everything else here — species, characters,
locations — is a write-up compiled from sources. **This file is one of the sources.** When a question
gets asked directly and answered directly, the answer becomes real the moment it's given, and this is
where that moment is recorded so it is never lost, never re-asked, and never silently contradicted by
a later guess.

**Why this exists at all:** the mitflit incident that started `bible/` in the first place (see its
README) was a *guess* standing in for an answer nobody had actually gotten. This file is the opposite
failure mode closed off — once a question has been asked and answered here, there is no excuse left for
guessing at it again.

**The rules — read these before touching this file:**

1. **Check here before asking anything.** If a question this specific has already been asked, the
   answer is below. Asking it again wastes Ingram's time and, worse, risks getting a slightly different
   answer the second time and now having two contradicting "truths" on record.
2. **Log a question here the moment it's asked** — verbatim, not paraphrased, under a new entry with
   today's date. If the answer isn't back yet, that's fine; the question still gets its place in order.
3. **Log the answer here the moment it's given** — verbatim, or, if very long, faithfully transcribed
   in full rather than summarized. A summary loses exactly the texture that makes an answer specific
   enough to write from.
4. **Never edit a past entry's question or answer text.** This is a historical record, not a living
   document to tidy up. If a later answer changes or supersedes an earlier one, that is itself a new,
   dated entry — cross-reference the old one, and mark the old one **SUPERSEDED**, but never rewrite or
   delete what it originally said.
5. **Every entry gets a "Filed to" line** — which bible files, dossiers, or data files were actually
   updated as a result. An answer that lives only in this ledger and nowhere else is half-filed.
6. **If something written elsewhere in the repo ever contradicts an entry here, this file wins.** Fix
   the other file; do not "fix" the ledger to match a guess.

### 2 — Nozomi and the party's early activation

### 13 — Where the military end stops and the school begins

island. Entry 12's "genuine, deliberate mix" is achieved by **adjacency across an island**, not by
mixing the two registers in one place: a candidate walks from one to the other.

This is a refinement of entry 12, not a contradiction of it, and entry 12 stands.

one campus on one island but are NOT co-sited); the 22 lore entries in

**Filled in by Claude — proposals, veto freely:**

- **Why the charter's current answer fails.** It was written about 195 years ago, before any Frame existed and before the prophecy named Kurose, so it can't foresee child pilots. Its authors are alive and on the base, so "nobody alive has read it" can't be true. The party are volunteers of any age, not conscripted children. And a painted-over paragraph framed in the lobby is an advertisement, not a secret. Options: cut the mystery; or change what's under the bar to something tied to today's spine. The strongest candidate is the founders' own account of First Impact, painted out once to keep the heroes clean. Ingram's call (`open-questions.json#founding-charter-rethink`).
- **How H3 can happen with a cause.** The city only ever sees it through a treated lens. In Chapter 10 Cicada gets treated lenses into thousands of hands, and the city looks up through them. The Directorate bans the lenses, which makes Cicada look right. Without a treated lens it is never visible.
- **Why the party can't simply look again (H1).** The show's lenses are collected at the door, and nobody sells one. To look again they have to make their own, which means understanding what the glass is treated with.
- **How I read "mysterious" (H1).** Mysterious at the table: the show explains nothing, and nobody in the fiction offers a cause. The GM-only reason (sensor filaments and sensor clusters, `dossier-observer.md`) stays written down, so the mystery has a real answer. Say if you meant the design should leave it unexplained too.
- **What "never visible" covers.** The eyes and tentacles in the sky. Its children are different: they're built from Sap, so they're solid, and anyone can see them.
- **How the party comes to understand the chemistry (H1).** In Chapter 7 the old man, delighted to be asked, explains it. Other routes are a stolen lens and the Directorate's own catalogue.
- **The dilemma.** Its first three points stay as consequences. The temptation (using the Watcher's eyes) needs a mechanism before it's used; the likeliest is the open question of a piece of Mother in every Gen 2.
