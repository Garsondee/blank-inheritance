# The Trouble with Mitflits

> **SOURCE MATERIAL — from the original campaign, not Blank Inheritance canon.**
> This is the original Pathfinder 2e *Abomination Vaults* campaign that set the ground for Blank Inheritance. Its
> player characters became the Directorate's four founders. Nothing here is canon on its own: where it
> disagrees with `ideas/story/bible/LEDGER.md` or the dossiers, **those win**. See `../README.md` for the rules.

*Extracted 2026-09-24 from `old server data/abomination-vault/` (Foundry world title: "The Trouble with
Mitflits").*

---

## The game

Ingram ran Paizo's *Abomination Vaults* megadungeon in Pathfinder 2e and steered it off the published rails
early. The mitflits the adventure sets up as vermin in the Gauntlight ruins became the heart of the story instead:
- the party made peace with them
- Anabala, herself a mitflit, ended up running a mitflit council in Gauntlight Keep
- the campaign turned into a fight against the hunters and a noble family who captured mitflit
  "savants" for their minds.

It later went off-world: to Nirvana, a planar cargo hold, a jungle obelisk, and a mitflit village with
something called Mother.

- **Sessions:** "Word of the Session" (`journals/word-of-the-session.md`) logs 31 sessions, one line each, from
  14 June 2022 to 12 April 2023. The chat log shows the world still in use in May 2024, and the last write
  to it was July 2024.
- **Table (Foundry users):** Garsondee (Ingram, GM); Pippa (Anabala); Thorn (Hyuck, and the familiar Dave);
  Craig (Shucks); Liam (Rupiah, per the session-planning journal. Liam's linked Foundry character was later
  deleted and replaced). A sixth name, `bigossim`, appears only on card stacks.

## The campaign, in order

**[campaign-timeline.md](campaign-timeline.md)** reconstructs the whole campaign. The 31 logged sessions (June 2022 to April 2023) are

## Where this meets Blank Inheritance

**Decided by Ingram, 2026-09-24 (`LEDGER.md` #29):**

> "The Inicrium battery is an event which caused the Baldwin Manor to become overrun with Shadow creatures.
> Eventually they deactivated the battery which stopped the shadows from encroaching, this wasn't First Impact
> which happened later in the ruin of the first spaceship, the generation ship."

So the "power generator" of `LEDGER.md` #6 is the Inicrium battery, and the exploiting lord's castle is Baldwin
Manor. It stays a separate incident from First Impact.

**Correspondences noticed, not decided.** These are open for Ingram to place or discard:

- Current canon says a human lord captured a mitflit clan and used their savant intelligence to design
  weapons of war (master timeline, ~200+ years ago). Here, the Baldwins run a magical-weapons manufactory,
  a mitflit clan has lived at Volkheed since 4147 AR, and William Charles Baldwin's note orders mitflit
  "savants" to be taken.
- The source's own "Kortos Cataclysm" (an Inicrium explosion a thousand years before play) is a different
  event from both the Baldwin incident and First Impact. What it is in this world is unasked.
- The **Kortos (Console)** map's "Shuttle Location", the **Cargo Deck** maps, and **Mother** could bear on the
  generation ship, but nothing in the data says so.
- Founder names: the sheet reads "Shucks **Mord**", but the token and the bible read **Mordant**. Rupiah is also
  "the Bold" and "Dark Rupiah" in different scenes.
