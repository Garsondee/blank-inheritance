> player characters became the Directorate's four founders. It is the ground this campaign was built on.

> their labels); typos are the originals'.

**Text written on the map:**

> 1. "The Hypotenuse's melody, when sung in harmony with the Song of the Sides, echoes in Rath's Mathematics. Deduceth the refrain of the hypotenuse, when the Song of the Sides are 5 and 12."
> 
> - Answer: The computer is asking for the solution to a Pythagorean theorem puzzle. The 'Song of the Sides' refers to the sides of a right triangle, and the 'Hypotenuse's melody' refers to the hypotenuse. The group will need to remember or figure out the theorem, a² + b² = c². For a triangle with sides 5 and 12, the hypotenuse will be 13. 
> 
> 2. "In the realm of Hephaestus, two equal masses set equal distances apart. On a third object of smaller mass they spew their influence. Tell me, the seeker of the Knower's Grasp, where will this smaller object find rest if not in aether’s infinite expanse?"
> 
> - Answer: This question refers to an astrophysics or physics principle. The 'realm of Hephaestus' correlates to space, the two equal masses are two equally sized planets/star and the smaller mass is a smaller planet/star or even spaceship. The answer is that the smaller object will sit in the middle, equidistant between two larger masses, where the gravitational forces of both larger masses cancel each other out.
> 
> 3. "In the script of life, there are four scribes—Adenine, Cytosine, Guanine, and Thymine. When Adenine and Thymine entwine inscribed lines, and Guanine and Cytosine form their own bindings, tell me the number of possible combinations within a set of three?”
> 
> - Answer: This question is related to genetics and how DNA base pairs combine. There are 4^3 or 64 possible combinations for a set of three.
> 
> 2. "Taking counsel with the four winds, elemental harmony yields an unchanging constant. The scribe of the zephyrs inscribes elements with eight, eighteen, and thirty-two. These combine to form the median of creation. Discern the elements, and thou shall discern the median."
> 
> - Answer: This is an advanced riddle on periodicity in the periodic table of the elements, and it is asking for you to figure out which elements have 8, 18, and 32 electrons in an outer shell. The elements are Oxygen, Argon, and Germanium, all of which are part of the Elemental Median, or 'Life's Building Blocks' as they are often known in esoteric circles.
> 
> 3. "Within Elysium’s cosmic loom is a Weaver who entwines stardust threads into a tapestry of celestial art. One creation, Aurea Spiralis, grows ever outward in a divine proportion. Judge me this ratio, and the path to enlightenment shall be thine."
> 
> - Answer: The Weaver is referencing nature, 'Aurea Spiralis' is the Golden Spiral or Fibonacci spiral, and 'divine proportion' is the Golden Ratio. This riddle is asking for the value of that Golden Ratio, which is approximately 1.61803. Most people would recognize it as Phi (Φ).
