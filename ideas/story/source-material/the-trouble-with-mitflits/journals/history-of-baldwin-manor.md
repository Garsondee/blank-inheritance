===================================================================
                       HISTORY OF THE BALDWIN ESTATE
===================================================================
                                CHAPTER I
                           The Founding of Volkheed
                              4071 AR, Year of Ascending Souls
In the annals of time, in the year of 4071 AR, a humble village by the name of Volkheed was founded amidst the rolling hills of Kortos Island. Its origin marked an era of hope and determination, as settlers braved the uncharted wilderness in search of a new beginning. The pioneers of Volkheed sought refuge and an opportunity to build their lives from the ground up, nurturing the foundations upon which a prosperous future would stand.
                                CHAPTER II
                      The Birth of Inicrium Mining
                              4171 AR, Year of Bountiful Harvests
Around the year 4171 AR, the discovery of a rich trove of dark, glimmering ore known as Inicrium brought about a revolutionary development. Bold and enterprising individuals embarked upon its excavation, delving deep into the earth beneath Volkheed. The mining operations which ensued infused the local economy with newfound prosperity, fueling trade and creating wealth within the region.
                                CHAPTER III
                The Arrival of the Mitflit Clan
                              4147 AR, Year of Liquid Shadows
In the year 4147 AR, a mysterious clan of Mitflits arrived in Volkheed. Their presence, at first met with caution, soon grew to become an integral part of the village's tapestry. Their enigmatic skills and innate magical talents lent themselves to the growing Inicrium trade, ultimately shaping the future of both the village and its newfound allies.
                                CHAPTER IV
                          The Creation of Croglang Castle
                              4221 AR, Year of Whispers On The Wind
From the bountiful Inicrium trade, the prosperity of Volkheed enabled the construction of a magnificent fortress. In the year 4221 AR, Croglang Castle came into being, a mighty symbol of the village's perseverance and triumph. The castle stood sentinel against the unknown, guarding the inhabitants of Volkheed and the bustling mines below from possible threats.
                                CHAPTER V
             The Renaming of Croglang Castle to Baldwin Manor
                              4201 AR, Year of Gilded Feathers
As the years passed and Volkheed continued to flourish, so too did the powerful Baldwin family. Their distinguished lineage and influence earned them the right to claim Croglang Castle as their own, rechristening it Baldwin Manor in the year 4201 AR. With newfound vigor, the Baldwin family oversaw the continued Inicrium mining and prosperity of Volkheed.
                                CHAPTER VI
 Baldwin Manor's Rise as a Magical Weapons Manufactory
                           on the Island of Kortos
                              4301 AR, Year of the Golden Horizon to Present
As the Inicrium trade flourished, so too did the market for powerful arms and armaments. Baldwin Manor, under the guidance of the Baldwin family, transformed into one of the largest magical weapons manufactories on the island of Kortos. The skill and craftsmanship displayed by the Baldwin family led to the export of their wares across the sea, with the powerful city of Absalom becoming one of their most prominent clients. To this day, Baldwin Manor remains a testament to the strength, determination, and artistry that has sustained the Baldwin family legacy over the centuries.
