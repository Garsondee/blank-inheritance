## New Page

Guided by the benevolent inhabitants of Luminara, the group makes their way to the cathedral that stands as the spiritual epicenter of the city. As they approach, the cathedral's grandeur becomes apparent—it is a masterpiece of architectural beauty and divine craftsmanship, its walls and spires bathed in the radiant light that defines the city.
Constructed from a material that seems to be both stone and light, the cathedral glows with a serene brilliance, casting a soothing ambiance over its surroundings. The architecture combines elegance with strength, its spires reaching towards the heavens as if in a silent prayer, while the wide, welcoming steps seem to invite all who seek guidance, solace, or insight.
The entrance is framed by massive doors carved with intricate designs that tell stories of enlightenment, redemption, and the journey of the soul. These doors stand open, symbolizing the cathedral's ever-present welcome to those who come in search of answers or sanctuary.
Inside, the cathedral's interior is bathed in a soft, diffuse light that seems to emanate from within the very walls, illuminating stained glass windows that depict scenes of cosmic harmony and the interconnectivity of all beings. The air is filled with a gentle, melodious hum, a sound that resonates with the core of one's being, inspiring a deep sense of peace and reverence.
