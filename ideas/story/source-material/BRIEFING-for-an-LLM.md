# Briefing: the original campaign behind *Blank Inheritance*

*For an AI assistant being given background. Written 2026-09-24 for Ingram (she/her), the GM. It summarises
everything recovered from her old Foundry VTT server, so this document is self-contained. If the full files are also
attached, section 9 says what each one holds.*

---

## 1. Read this first: how to treat this material

**Everything below comes from an old, unfinished campaign.** It is Ingram's Pathfinder 2e game *The Trouble with
Mitflits* (2022–24). It is **source material and useful lore, not a straitjacket.**

- **Use it to clarify and to ground.** When you need to know what the founders were like, what happened to them, or
  where an idea came from, look here first rather than inventing something.
- **It can be rewritten.** Events can be changed, re-ordered or dropped if the new campaign needs it.
- **It can be enhanced and clarified.** Gaps can be filled, and thin moments expanded. Where the record is vague, you
  may propose an interpretation, as long as you say it's a proposal.
- **Blank Inheritance's own decisions win.** Where this material disagrees with something Ingram has decided for the
  new campaign (section 3), the new decision stands. Point out the clash rather than silently picking one.
- **Some of it is inferred, not recorded.** The GM's session log stops in April 2023, and the year after it was
  reconstructed from timestamps. Section 5 marks which is which. Don't present an inference as a fact.
- **Paizo's published content is not ours.** The old campaign ran on Paizo's *Abomination Vaults* adventure, but that
  text isn't reproduced here. Treat Paizo's named characters and places as borrowed scenery.
- **All of this is GM-side.** Never put it in front of players as-is. The new campaign's rule for anything read aloud
  at the table: no references to real-world works, places or decades, and don't mention a thing only to deny it.
- **Pronouns:** Ingram is she/her. For anyone else whose pronouns aren't stated, use they/them.

## 2. The one-paragraph version

Four adventurers were the player characters of *The Trouble with Mitflits*:
- **Hyuck** Bonebox, a gnoll barbarian
- **Shucks** Mord(ant), a skeleton rogue
- **Anabala** Macthata, a mitflit
- **Rupiah**, the Star Singer, an elf oracle

They made friends with the mitflits (small blue gremlin-folk everyone else treated as vermin), gave them a home and a
government in Gauntlight Keep, and fought the hunters and the noble Baldwin family who were capturing mitflit "savants"
for their minds. Along the way they:
- ended a shadow-plane incursion at Baldwin Manor caused by an Inicrium "battery"
- found a crashed starship with mitflit crew quarters inside a jungle obelisk
- met an incorporeal intelligence called Mother
- were flung through a portal to the plane of Nirvana

**The campaign stopped there, unfinished.** Ingram's words: *"We never finished that adventure and Blank Inheritance is
an attempt to tie EVERYTHING up all at once, give closure to the original adventure whilst also creating a new one."*

## 3. How it connects to Blank Inheritance (Ingram's decisions)

> **Updated after this briefing was written (LEDGER #31, 2026-09-24), and these win over anything below:**
> - **Two ships.** The generation ship crashed tens of thousands of years ago in a hidden jungle valley on Kortos (the
>   obelisk "temple"; the mitflits descend from its crew). A **search ship** came a thousand years later to find it,
>   crashed too, and lies under **Kurose**. Both were buried by eruptions and earthquakes.
> - **The Watcher is the generation ship's navigation and sensor system**, torn into an unbound nanotech swarm at
>   First Impact. It is the campaign's adversary, **played by the AI**, with the objective to observe, multiply and
>   expand. The themes are **mass surveillance and runaway agentic systems**.
> - **Sap is nanotech feedstock; the exotic matter is fuel.** A breached fuel container causes a subspace rupture:
>   First Impact, and **Second Impact** (the Kurose rupture, unavoidable, about three-quarters through). The Watcher
>   and Second Impact **both need a mitflit home-world solution**.
> - **Baldwin Manor and First Impact are separate events**, months apart in play. First Impact happened while the four
>   were fetching exotic fuel for the mitflits living beside the ship. Baldwin's family members died by accident, not
>   at the four's hands, and **William Charles Baldwin** blamed them anyway. **His present-day line is a beat** the
>   players will come across.
> - **Anabala** is a very early mitflit, long-lived because she carries the knowledge demon **Abraxas** (GM-only).
> - **Players:** Liam (Rupiah), Pippa (Anabala), Craig (Shucks) and **Steve (Hyuck)**, plus Jamie and Anne. The four
>   originals play their founders in flashbacks.
> - **No berserk**, and **no spaceship combat**.
>
> Full record: `ideas/story/bible/LEDGER.md` #31, and the rewritten `dossier-observer.md` and `dossier-first-impact.md`.


Blank Inheritance is a **Starfinder 2e** campaign. It is set about two hundred years after the founders' era, in the city of
**Kurose**, where teenage pilots fly giant robots called **Frames** for the **Directorate**, the organisation the four
founded. What Ingram has already decided, in her own words where quoted:

- **The four founded the Directorate.** After the rift, *"Rupiah asked the gods if there would ever be another such rift
  created on Golarion and the answer was yes, in the city of Kurose many hundreds of years in the future. Rupiah, Shucks,
  Hyuck and Anabala agreed to be there when this event would unfold to prevent it happening a second time. That is the
  Directorate."*
- **First Impact.** In the new canon, the four accidentally caused a catastrophe. They were retrieving an item from a crashed
  spaceship at Kortos. The throw worked and the catch failed, the container of exotic matter split, and a violet-black
  rift opened. It carved a path across the land into the city of Absalom and was stopped at a temple of Pharasma, with the four standing in front of
  it. The crashed ship is *"the ruin of the first spaceship, the generation ship."*
- **The Inicrium battery came first, and it is a separate incident.** *"The Inicrium battery is an event which caused the Baldwin Manor to
  become overrun with Shadow creatures. Eventually they deactivated the battery which stopped the shadows from
  encroaching, this wasn't First Impact which happened later."*
- **Where the old campaign ends and the new backstory picks up** (the "presumably" is Ingram's own): *"the adventure stopped with the players on a different plane of existence Nirvana.
  Presumably when the players go back to Golarion the First Impact Rupture was much larger and they swung in to try to
  stop it expanding further."*
- **The founders are all alive today**, about two hundred years later:
  - they work on the Directorate's base, *"except for Anabala and Shuck's Right Hand which are much more free agents"*
  - *"Rupiah works in the administration of the organisation, Shucks is head of medical and often involved in the science and
    Hyuck is the mad test pilot of some of the Directorates worst Frame concepts that never saw the light of day."*
  - Most of this is revealed to the players slowly. Shucks is revealed at the start.
- **The public version.** The founders are famous by name. But every official picture shows four tall, handsome humans, and the
  schoolbook never says how the rift began. A statue of a gnoll with "HYUCK" on the plaque stands in the harbour square,
  and people assume it's a mascot.
- **Mitflits** were *"star faring robots originally but their biology and magic and cybernetics all became muddled on
  Golarion over the thousands of years. They became a joke, the least of races, but Rupiah used diplomacy with them
  instead of violence and started them down a path towards getting home."*
- **Not decided yet**, so treat these as open: how the founders have lived two hundred years (only Shucks is undead);
  most of the player Steve's pitch for Hyuck (a god called Yabadabba, a time-magic mishap, his brother's skull sharing his body).
  Steve created Hyuck.

## 5. What happened, in order

**R** = recorded at the time. **I** = inferred from Foundry timestamps.

1. **Gauntlight (Jun–Sep 2022, R).**
   - The party starts in the town of Otari and explores the ruined lighthouse keep of Gauntlight (the published adventure).
   - Early on, diplomacy derails the script ("I will have to rewrite the whole damn scenario").
   - A peace pact is made, most likely with the mitflits.
   - Shucks's right hand becomes a character of its own ("Shuck's Gets Given a Hand", Aug 2022).
   - Anabala strikes a pact: *"I can find things and I can make things disappear - You owe me one favour."*
   - Hyuck's catchphrase is born: "Clean through the middle!"
2. **The Inquisitors and the hunters (Oct–Dec 2022, R).**
   - Inquisitors of Abadar (god of law and money) arrive, led by **Sofie Reitz**.
   - There's a tavern brawl with a band of **hunters**.
   - Hyuck covets a hunter's horse; he later pays a thief to steal it and has it made into armour, *The Barding of Vengeance*.
   - Nuns are kidnapped and memory-wiped by a mage, **Regina Key**, whose ciphered note is signed "WCB". It reads:
     *"Mitflits are there in force now, including many 'savants' ripe for the taking. I am gathering forces for a
     procurement operation."*
   - One nun, **Esther Williams**, escapes.
   - The party takes **Gauntlight Keep**, which becomes a bustling mitflit marketplace ("Miriq the Magnificent").
3. **The mitflit government (Jan–Apr 2023, R).**
   - A triumvirate forms. *"Yes, let's put Anabala in charge, what could go wrong?"*
   - The mitflit council discovers voting (it turns into bedlam), and the party brokers peace between Otari and the mitflits.
   - With the keep overcrowded, the party goes looking for a new mitflit home in three ruined forts: Fort Ranhold (insect tunnels,
     an ooze), Fort Croglang and Stonehill Keep.
   - A dungeon caves in, "Dave is a traitor", and "No-one trusts the Abadar Priest". **The log ends here.**
4. **Volkheed and Baldwin Manor (May–Aug 2023, I).**
   - The village of **Volkheed** and its inn, the Laughing Hound. The in-world time recorded there is "19th Sarenith, 18:00".
   - **Croglang Castle is Baldwin Manor**, seat of the Baldwin family's magical-weapons manufactory.
   - The GM's plan for this arc, verbatim: *"A castle which the group approaches - they are happy to hand over the
     Mitflit artefacts but require a little time and invite the group in. A siege begins. The group discovers that the
     people inside aren't innocent. Decision time! … The dark secret has to do with ritual sacrifice of prisoners. Both
     sides are fantatics."*
   - Documents found there: the history of the manor, its accounts, a materials manifest, a treatise on **Inicrium**,
     and a report on **Shadow Plane intrusions**.
   - In a single session the party crossed the fort, the **Shadow Plane** and **Abadar's Plane of Order**.
   - Then came the mines, and the fight inside the manor. A Baldwin's accusation is written on the map: *"That's my fucking horse's leather on that Gnoll."*
5. **The jungle and the starship (Sep–Nov 2023, I).**
   - A jungle, a cliff climb, and a black obelisk "like a foreboding finger of a long dead god".
   - At the entrance, a **computer** asks science riddles: Pythagoras, gravity, DNA, the golden ratio.
   - Inside, the maps are labelled **Reactor A, Reactor B, MITFLIT Quarters 1 and 2, The Bridge, Captain's Quarters,
     First Officer's Quarters, AI Reasoning Core** ("a space for briefings and education").
   - A **Mitflit Village** follows, and **Mother**: level 15, an incorporeal construct, and highly intelligent.
   - Shucks acquires a "Phaser (on Stun)".
6. **The Cargo Deck and the portal (Nov 2023 – Jan 2024, I).**
   - The ship's cargo deck, a creature vulnerable to "curved space", and a "Spacial Sealant Kit".
   - A mitflit council leader, Comhphas, sends a plea: *"The Mitflits are crafting a counterpart portal within Gauntlight. Once you're through,
     search for it … Time behaves differently there; you'll emerge just in time to make a difference. We're outnumbered,
     facing hunters with dark magic."*
   - A planar breach throws the party around the planes.
7. **Nirvana (Jan–May 2024, I and R).**
   - The party arrives "through an act not born of intent but of consequence".
   - **Sarenrae** (goddess of the sun and redemption) receives them in the city of **Luminara**, naming *"Rupiah, Shucks, Anabala, Hyuck, and Djarvik"*.
   - A zodiac puzzle about a hunter called Auros and a celestial key.
   - Demon-guarded portal locks, and a Balor.
   - **Last session: 30 May 2024.**

**Never played:**
- a prepared map with *"the ancient alien god"* waiting beyond a portal
- the **return to Gauntlight**: a map already set with 49 mitflits and their royal guard against **William Charles
  Baldwin's** army of hunters, infantry, archers and mobs

Per Ingram, they came back to find the First Impact rupture grown, and went to stop it.

## 6. The founders as they were

- **Hyuck Bonebox**, a gnoll barbarian (spirit instinct). The only sheet with a written biography, summarised:
  - He's superstitious and mutters to ancestral spirits, especially his grandmother.
  - He consults his elder brother's skull for big decisions, and keeps a trunk of ancestral bones.
  - He's hunting his father by scent. He wears five tiny skulls and swings a flail on a thigh bone.
  - He's the life of the party ("punch a hole clean through a pig").
  - Catchphrases: "Clean through the middle!" and "Wait! Did you hear that?"
  - He bears Abadar's Minor Curse, which marks his crimes on his skin. He picked up redemption-themed reactions
    shortly after the party met Abadar; the timing is from the data, and the reason isn't recorded.
  - His signature move is a homebrew all-or-nothing attack, *Straight Through the Middle!!!*
  - His talking axe complains about another talking sword.
- **Shucks Mord** (called Mordant on his token and in the new canon): a skeleton rogue with the Tomb Born background.
  - He's a ruffian, and later takes a Ghost dedication (ghostly flight and grasp).
  - He has battle medicine ("Dr Wint's Patented Battle Medicine") and Undead and Subterranean lore.
  - His detached **Right Hand** is a separate character, a scout and agent of chaos.
- **Anabala Macthata**: a mitflit (Ifrit).
  - Her sheet is labelled "Inscriber" but built on air-and-fire kineticist powers, with Loremaster and Investigator training.
  - She has Legal and Mitflit lore. She makes pacts and brokers deals ("Pact Broker").
  - She shrinks everything into bottles ("Anvil in a Bottle", "Cart in a bottle").
  - She ran the mitflit government.
  - Her player also sketched a homebrew pact-binder class with a four-stage curse.
- **Rupiah, the Star Singer** (also "the Bold" and "Dark Rupiah"): an elf oracle of the cosmos with bard training, and a
  Tall Tale background.
  - He has Theater Lore and Nirvana lore.
  - His player pitched a camp, chaotic nemesis from his performing past, leading a search party of fake travelling performers.
    Rupiah was travelling under a false identity.
  - New canon adds that he is elf nobility.
- **Others:**
  - Djarvik, a gnoll sorcerer (hag bloodline)
  - Sniffy Snout, a bloodhound shoony swashbuckler
  - Dave, Hyuck's familiar
  - Ally NPCs on PC sheets: Sofie Reitz, Mr Snuggly Wuffles (a ratfolk gunslinger Inquisitor)

  - **Castria Anne** (the Master Enchanter, ice and water; she signs the materials manifest)
  - **Agatha Laine** and **Reginald Cavil**
  - **William Charles Baldwin, "WCB"** (level 10, chaotic evil, brass knuckles): leader of the mitflit hunters, and the antagonist the ending was built around
- **The hunters:** Aubert "Golden", Kirkham, Thompkins, McGuire, Connor, Campbell, Hunniford and Shackley.
- **Inquisitors of Abadar:** Sofie Reitz, Kanika, Halina Benchae, Bronwen Rodford, Laurette Piraudrt, Zibene and Anja Blairr.
- **Mitflits:** Anabala; the council leaders Comhphas and Scrawng; Miriq; named stubs Losa, Comveelk, Jizlirt, Grot,
  Friz and Riegs.
- **Others:** Esther Williams (the nun who escaped), Regina Key (the mage, imprisoned), Arun (a tiefling haruspex who
  gave Hyuck a stone that answers one more question), Ulthak (a gnoll whose portrait file is named `dad.webp`, possibly Hyuck's father;
  unconfirmed), Mother, Sarenrae, Abadar.
- **Inicrium**, from the GM's in-world treatise:
  - It is "solid magic potential", a dark, glimmering "magical battery" that draws energy from the winds of magic.
  - Above about 50 g it collapses into an unstable grey metal ball, and around 100 g it explodes.
  - It caused the "Kortos Cataclysm" a thousand years before play. (That is a different event from both the Baldwin incident and First Impact, and unplaced in the new canon.)
- **Volkheed and Baldwin Manor**, from the GM's in-world history:
  - Volkheed village was founded 4071 AR on the Isle of Kortos, and Inicrium mining began around 4171.
  - A mitflit clan arrived in 4147 and lent its "enigmatic skills and innate magical talents" to the trade.
  - Croglang Castle was built from the proceeds, then claimed and renamed by the Baldwins. (The document's own dates are inconsistent.)
  - It became one of Kortos's largest magical-weapons manufactories, exporting to Absalom. Its 4721 net profit target was 200,000 gp.
- **The Shadow Plane report:** concentrated magical energy can make the Shadow Plane intersect the material world. The
  intersection dissipates naturally as the winds of magic disperse it.
- **The Kortos map** marks Absalom, Tallow, Hurdohr, Metz, Fort Ranhold, Kreasworth School of Magic, Eastpight, Stonehill
  Keep, Madrigal, "The Trouble with Mitflits", the Jewel of Abadar, "Volkheed & Baldwin House", Bearmouth, Piper's Cove
  and Otari. A second copy marks a **"Shuttle Location"**.
- **Nirvana and Luminara:** a golden, tranquil city of light with a cathedral at its heart. The GM's read-aloud texts survive.

## 8. Open threads and suggested questions (not decisions)

- Is the jungle starship, with its mitflit quarters and AI core, the **generation ship** of the new canon? And did First Impact happen
  there, between the starship arc and the portal to Nirvana? The old record doesn't say.
- Is **Mother** the ship's AI? She's incorporeal, a construct and highly intelligent, and she's placed on the cargo deck and in the Mitflit Village.
- Did time slip in Nirvana ("time behaves differently there"), and is that why the rupture grew?
- What became of: Esther Williams's lost memories, Regina Key, Dave's treachery, the mitflits besieged in Gauntlight,
  William Charles Baldwin, Rupiah's nemesis, Hyuck's father?
- Where does the old "Kortos Cataclysm" fit, if anywhere?

Raise these with Ingram as questions or proposals. Don't settle them on your own.
