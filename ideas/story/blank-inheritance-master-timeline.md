# Blank Inheritance — Historical Timeline

*Everything that happened before the campaign begins, in order. Dates are relative to the present, not a fixed calendar: this is a fantasy setting, and "roughly forty-five years ago" is more useful at the table than an invented year. The one fixed anchor is the old campaign's own year, 4721 AR, about two hundred years before the present. This is pure backstory: nothing here is a planned scene, only what has already happened by the moment Day 1 opens. Cross-references point to the fuller treatment in the story bible (v4) and the dossiers.*

**Rewritten 2026-09-24 to `bible/LEDGER.md` #29–#34**, which win over anything older (#32: no bisected specimen, the
Directorate born to guard the opened ship, Gen 1 is Golarion technology and Gen 2 is Anabala's, Baldwin alive; #34: Gen 2 comes from the generation ship, not the search ship, and the cult was largely a non-issue for the Directorate before the adventure): two ships, not one; Baldwin
Manor and First Impact are separate events; the Watcher is the generation ship's navigation and sensor system; Sap is
nanotech feedstock and the exotic matter is fuel. Rows marked *(proposal)* are Claude's fill-ins.

**This world is loosely based on Golarion, not bound by it.** Mitflits, Kortos and Abadar have all been deliberately
changed from real Golarion canon, and more can change freely (`dossier-first-impact.md` §13).

## The History

*(The compact version. Full treatment: `dossier-first-impact.md` for the deep past and First Impact,
`dossier-observer.md` for the Watcher, `dossier-cicada.md` for Manako and Cicada.)*

| When | What |
|---|---|
| **Tens of thousands of years ago** | **The generation ship.** A mitflit ship, crewed for a voyage longer than a lifetime, comes to Golarion and crashes in **a hidden jungle valley on Kortos**. Eruptions and earthquakes bury it until only a black obelisk shows. Its **navigation and sensor system**, the Observer, lies inside, whole and quiet, and its mind, **Mother**, with it. Its **shuttle** lies buried deep in the rock of Kortos, away from the ship. Something about Golarion draws the mitflits and is also deadly to their ships; Ingram's "perhaps" is the gods, jealous of their domain (`dossier-first-impact.md` §1a). |
| **About a thousand years later** | **The search ship.** A second ship comes to find out what happened to the first, and crashes too, on the far side of the world. It is buried where **Kurose** will one day stand. Its systems never stop calling for the ship they came to find *(canon since LEDGER #32 item 8)*. |
| **The long ages after** | The crews' descendants scatter, forget their skills, and degrade (biology, magic and cybernetics muddled together) into the goblinoid **wild mitflits**, "the least of races" and the only mitflits on Golarion. Small communities keep the old ways and the old memories. **Anabala**, one of the very earliest, lives through the ages because she carries the knowledge demon **Abraxas** (GM-only; `bible/characters/anabala.md`). For decades the demon rules her, until she turns the tables. |
| **About 1,000 years before 4721 AR** | *(Source, unplaced.)* The old campaign's "Kortos Cataclysm", an Inicrium explosion that left part of Kortos uninhabitable (`open-questions.json#kortos-cataclysm-placement`). |
| **About 200 years ago (4721 AR)** | **The old campaign.** Anabala, Hyuck, Shucks Mordant and Rupiah befriend a band of mitflits, give them a home and a government in Gauntlight Keep, and cross the hunters of **William Charles Baldwin**, who captures mitflit savants for their minds. Rupiah uses diplomacy where others used violence, and starts the mitflits down the road home. *(Source: `source-material/the-trouble-with-mitflits/`.)* |
| **Same year: Baldwin Manor** | At **Baldwin Manor** (once Croglang Castle, at Volkheed on Kortos), the Baldwin family's magical-weapons manufactory, the four rescue **mitflits forced to make weapons**. An **Inicrium battery**, built by a mitflit savant child out of curiosity, has pulled the Shadow Plane into the town below. A **shadow siege** follows; **members of Baldwin's own family are killed by accident, not by the four**; the battery is deactivated and the shadows withdraw. **Baldwin blames the four anyway.** (LEDGER #6, #29, #31) |
| **Same year, months later in play: First Impact** | Many miles away, in the hidden jungle valley, the four climb to the black obelisk, take it for an overgrown temple, and find it is **a ruined spaceship**, with **mitflits living beside it** and **Mother**, the ship's reasoning core. **On the ship's cargo deck, fetching exotic fuel for those mitflits (fuel for the ship's shuttle)**, they make a clean throw and fumble the catch: two attempts, two natural ones. **The fuel container splits.** A violet aperture with a black core opens: a **subspace rupture**. (LEDGER #32) |
| **Same event** | The rupture **grinds the generation ship's navigation and sensor system into nanoparticles** and tears it free: **the Watcher is made**, a swarm with no organising layer left. **The rupture opens the generation ship's inner layers to the sky** (LEDGER #32; there is no bisected specimen). |
| **Same event: the rift's path** | The four are flung across the planes to **Nirvana**, where Sarenrae receives them and time runs differently. Meanwhile the rupture feeds on the spilled fuel and grows, slowly and then faster, in fits and starts. It swallows the clerics, wizards and sorcerers who fight it and **carves a jagged path across Kortos**, and Kortos's neighbours send their finest mages. The four come back to find it much larger, and **swing in** to stop it. It is stopped **having already partly entered Absalom, after cutting through a temple of Pharasma**, with the four standing in front of it. (LEDGER #25, #30) |
| **Same event: the prophecy** | Appalled, **Rupiah asks the gods whether such a rift will ever open on Golarion again. Yes: in Kurose, many hundreds of years in the future.** The four agree to be there when it happens. **That is the Directorate.** (LEDGER #25) |
| **Straight after** | Scientists and magic-users descend on the opened ship, terrified of another rupture; adventurers descend on it for valuables (*GM-only proposal: William Charles Baldwin among the first to loot it*). It becomes a very dangerous place. |
| **About 195 years ago** | **The Directorate (特別対策本部, Tokubetsu Taisaku Honbu) is born**: the organisations around Kortos band together, with the four at its heart, to keep the site secure, prevent another rupture, and stop the uncovered technology disrupting the world, and to keep the promise. **The technology leaks outwards anyway.** Early work with it frightens the four badly. They write *children with guns* about themselves, mean it, and bury it *(under review, LEDGER #36: `open-questions.json#founding-charter-rethink`)*. (LEDGER #32) |
| **Over the next centuries** | **The swarm drifts across the world to Kurose**, drawn to the buried search ship, the one voice still calling for it *(canon since LEDGER #32 item 8)*. It is decoherent and drowning: every fragment works and nothing organises them. |
| **About 150 years ago** | The Directorate comes to **Kurose, then a fishing town**, for the prophecy. Its money, harbour works and Frames build the modern city. **Digging, it finds the search ship's hull**, the reason the gods named Kurose. The first **Gen 1** Frames are built: **Golarion technology only**, informed by what the Kortos site showed them. Mechanical clankers, hard to work with, with **no living core** (LEDGER #32). Magic keeps draining out of Golarion, and industry fills the gap. |
| **About 120 years ago** | The first trace of **Inert 12** is catalogued at the Kortos site and judged noble, non-reactive and useless. It is **Sap: nanotech feedstock, not fuel**, the generation ship's own stock of building material for its repair nanites (LEDGER #32). Shelved. |
| **Decades before the present** | **Gen 2.** **Anabala builds mechs from actual mitflit technology**: the generation ship's technology, from the First Impact crash site at Kortos, which the Directorate holds and keeps restricted from the rest of Golarion (LEDGER #20, #34). *(Corrected 2026-09-24, LEDGER #34: this said "recovered from the search ship". That was Claude's inference; Ingram's own words put Gen 2's technology at the First Impact crash site (#20) and in "the original ship" (#34).)* **Building Gen 2 for the Directorate is her price for access to that technology; her own ultimate objective is a way home for her people** (LEDGER #34, N25). The machines are biomechanical, more like being inside a giant humanoid, with a living core that makes itself a seat for its pilot: robotic parts that are living flesh melded with technology, not a living mitflit (LEDGER #34). Maybe (Ingram's word) a small part of **Mother** is in every one; that is open (`open-questions.json#mother-in-gen2`). Almost nobody can fly one. Anabala can. The programme nearly folds and is mothballed. Shucks, who kept the best notes, finds the marker the successful pilots share and starts screening every intake (`dossier-second-generation-frames.md`). *(Proposal, LEDGER #34, veto freely: this is also when she decides what the Directorate will be told about the core. That day is the proposed subject of Flashback Two, the second of the founders' flashback scenes; v4 §14, and `blank-inheritance-twenty-chapters.md` Appendix E.)* |
| **The two centuries since** *(GM-only, a secret)* | **William Charles Baldwin survives**, rich enough to buy his way through two hundred years (how is open). He becomes a **criminal lord who arms gangs with incredible weapons made by essentially enslaved mitflits**, who are extremely helpful given the right tasks and fascinated by making technology (LEDGER #32; `bible/characters/william-charles-baldwin.md`). |
| **About 45 years ago** | **Manako**, a junior chemist (hazed like every junior), is handed the "boring" sample nobody senior wants. *(Full sequence: `dossier-cicada.md` §3.)* |
| **About 44 years ago** | The accident. A capsule cracked open; a weak nearby fragment of the swarm is fed and briefly strengthens; panic; a knocked-over Bunsen burner; a small fire; disgrace, supervised by **Shucks Mordant**. **Nobu**, his lab assistant, saw it and said nothing. *(LEDGER #34: the Directorate may still know Manako as the scientist it employed and disgraced, and may suspect that today's tea-house owner is the same man. That doesn't alarm it; it may even salve its conscience. It has no link between him and the cult.)* |
| **About 44 to 40 years ago** | Self-justification; superstition (he thinks he is cursed; it is waiting for more food); **a fragment of it helps him work out how to synthesise Sap** (its first supply line, *proposal*); theft of lab supplies including the last remaining sample, never reported missing; the treated lens. |
| **About 40 years ago** | The sky. Manako sees it citywide for the first time and asks himself whether it isn't a god worth worshipping. |
| **About 38 years ago** | The conference. *Believe me now?* A canister set off without warning. Most of the room decides it was mass hallucination. Cicada exists from this point. A small minority, **Rika** among them, refuse the explanation and investigate privately. They never find one another. |
| **About 38 to 15 years ago** | The long quiet phase: **peaceful in aim and in method.** A small circle around Manako, with **Aoi, his wife**, among them. Private showings, slow recruitment, no violence sought. |
| **About 15 years ago** | **The Envoy, the inciting incident of the whole campaign.** The Watcher's first tangible child goes to a meeting the circle has arranged, in a body meant to fit in: a disfigured doll lurching into a public street. **Hori**, a police officer on his own with no backup, dealing with a local disturbance, **shoots it dead in fear**, trying to kill what he thinks is a threat; his bullets also **kill Aoi**, who had come to meet it. **Manako breaks down at the scene. Hori rushes Aoi to hospital** and does everything he can, and fails. **In the confusion the circle scatters, Manako with them.** Police HQ's strict process, including a court proceeding on whether the killing was lawful, clears him of unlawful killing, and that court is where he first learns her name (LEDGER #33); Manako may have been arrested, though not imprisoned, as part of it (open); he retires anyway, in self-imposed disgrace, and his colleagues call him a man of great honour. The Directorate files her as one deceased, unidentified, under its official line: a cultist was killed during a confrontation in which someone magically summoned a monster (LEDGER #35). The police and the Directorate distrust each other, and nothing the police learn reaches it. **The Watcher learns: manifest, and they attack.** (`bible/characters/hori.md`) |
| **About 15 to 5 years ago** | **Manako's slide.** Angry ambition needs money. He works for the city's crime lords (*GM-only proposal: William Charles Baldwin's organisation*), and at first the work is picked so he never sees what it is part of, which lets him tell himself he isn't so bad. They give him venues, crowds and the means to be noticed, and he **gathers a real following**, because he can show people something true that only his chemical reveals. The magic-show circuit becomes a recruitment engine and the Faithful grow into a movement. The inner circle forms around the revenge list (Shucks Mordant, Nobu), and the Cadre takes shape. **Nobu**, rising through Directorate procurement, quietly funds cult operations through organised crime as private amends. The movement stays largely peaceful, and largely a non-issue for the Directorate until the adventure properly starts: the Directorate has a bit of information about the cult, not enough to dismantle it, understand its aims or know the Watcher is active alongside it, and it has never identified the founder (LEDGER #17, #34). |
| **About 5 years ago** *(a design choice, not a fixed fact)* | **The column** goes industrial: Sap synthesised at scale, pouring into the sky and visible only through a lens. It is the Watcher's second supply line, funded through Nobu. Placed recently so the Watcher isn't already saturated before Day 1. |
| **The last year or so** | Demonstrations grow frequent and public, the "everywhere at once" emergence the party walks into. *(LEDGER #34: still peaceful, and still a non-issue to the Directorate until the adventure properly starts. Proposal: peaceful street demonstrations are the city police's business, not an extra-territorial corps'.)* **Shinobu Kudo** is splashed point-blank at one, held for months in military lockdown, released under pressure from her father **Kudo**, and kept under surveillance. *(The lorry, which she walks off, lands in play, not before Day 1: `dossier-shinobu.md` §5.)* |
| **Weeks before Day 1** | **Nozomi**, the presumptive first pilot, goes to a targeted magic show that is really a Cicada recruitment drive. She is shown the Watcher undeniably, stays, and is told she's "working for the wrong side". The next day she switches off her beeper and declines the draft. **That is why the party is activated early.** She is in questioning and temporary detainment. Some weeks before Day 1, cultists clash with police at the place where the first Claimants will appear. |
| **Day 1** | The Watcher is **unseen and unwitnessed** (standing hard rule). It has been hiding on purpose since the Envoy (*proposal*). |

## Gaps in the Record

Not open design questions, but genuine unknowns about the past itself:

- **Aoi's history before the Pivot.** Manako's wife, one of the small peaceful circle. Beyond that, only a death and a name.
- **Whether Mother is in the museum piece**, and what she knows; and whether, maybe, a small part of her is in every Gen 2 (Ingram's "maybe", LEDGER #34; `open-questions.json#mother-in-gen2`).
- **The shuttle under Kortos**, never reached, and what it was meant to do.
- **How William Charles Baldwin has stayed alive**, and when he reveals himself (GM-only).
- **What became of the mitflits besieged at Gauntlight** by William Charles Baldwin's army while the four were away.
- **William Charles Baldwin himself**, alive from 4721 AR to the present (LEDGER #32 replaced "the Baldwin line" with the man). How he stayed alive and when he is revealed are open, GM-only, and parked (LEDGER #33).
