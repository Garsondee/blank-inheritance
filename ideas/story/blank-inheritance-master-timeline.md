# Blank Inheritance — Historical Timeline

*Everything that happened before the campaign begins, in order. Dates are relative-to-present, not a fixed calendar — this is a fantasy setting, and "roughly forty-five years ago" is more useful at the table than an invented year. This is pure backstory: nothing here is a planned scene, a session, or a future event — only what has already happened by the moment Day 1 opens. Cross-references point to the fuller treatment in the story bible (v4) and the two dossiers.*

---

## The History

| When | What |
|---|---|
| **~200+ years ago** | A local human lord has captured a clan of mitflits and puts their savant intelligence to work designing weapons of war, growing rich on it. Among the side effects: one mitflit invents a power generator that draws unwanted attention from the shadow realm — a thread nobody ever followed up on. **Anabala**, moved by what's being done to her own people, recruits **Hyuck**, **Shucks Mordant**, and **Rupiah** to intervene. |
| **~200 years ago** | In the course of that intervention, the party attempts to retrieve an item from a crashed spaceship at Kortos. The throw goes perfectly — a clean Athletics success. The catch does not: two attempts, two natural ones, and the container of exotic matter splits open. A violet-hued aperture with a black core begins to open and grow, on the island where Absalom stands. As it grows large enough to notice, adventurers and mages converge from across the island and then the region to contain it. What happened next was never firmly established even by those who were there — the account handed down says only that the aperture was eventually forced back and crushed into a small space, and either destroyed or converted into something else. *(Later understood: this is the moment that pulverised part of the buried ship's sensor system into what would become the Watcher.)* A crater is left, and a specimen — a robot sliced clean through by the aperture's edge — is found buried far deeper than should have been possible. |
| **~195 years ago** | The four found the Directorate, so the response is faster next time. Early hands-on work with the recovered technology frightens them badly. They write *children with guns* about themselves, mean it, and bury it. |
| **~150 years ago** | Following the trail from the crater over generations, the Directorate locates the true mother lode — the rest of the hull — under a city forming in the Minkai-equivalent east, and relocates there. Reverse-engineering of the bisected specimen produces the first Frame shells, built as cargo-cult replicas of the one cross-section anyone has ever seen. Magic's slow recession from Golarion and industry's rise fill the resulting technological vacuum across this whole span. |
| **~120 years ago** | The first trace of what will later be called Inert 12 is catalogued near the original crater dig. Judged noble, non-reactive, useless. Shelved. |
| **~45 years ago** | Manako, a junior chemist, is handed the "boring" sample nobody senior wants. *(Full sequence: dossier-cicada.md §3.)* |
| **~44 years ago** | The accident. A capsule cracked open; a weak nearby fragment of the cloud is fed and briefly strengthens; panic; a knocked-over bunsen burner; a small fire; disgrace. |
| **~44–40 years ago** | Self-justification, superstition (he believes himself cursed; it is in fact waiting for more food), the first small cooperation, theft of lab supplies including the last remaining sample (never reported missing), the treated lens. |
| **~40 years ago** | The sky. Manako sees it citywide for the first time and asks himself whether it isn't a god worth worshipping. |
| **~38 years ago** | The conference. *Believe me now?* A canister, detonated without warning. Most of the room decides it was mass hallucination. Cicada exists from this point. A small minority — Rika among them — refuse the explanation and begin investigating privately. They never find one another. |
| **~38–15 years ago** | The long evangelical phase. Small demonstrations, slow recruitment through the magic-show circuit, no violence sought. The Faithful, in the terms later used to describe the movement, form and grow during this stretch. |
| **~15 years ago** | **The Envoy.** The Watcher's own first attempt at contact beyond its worshippers. It builds a body meant to fit society and fails; the result is a disfigured doll lurching into a public street. **Hori**, a police officer, shoots it in fear. The round passes through and kills **Aoi**, Manako's lover. **The Pivot:** the cult turns from witness to revenge. *(Full detail: dossier-observer.md §6, dossier-cicada.md §5.)* |
| **~15–5 years ago** | The inner circle forms around the revenge list (Shucks Mordant, Nobu). The Cadre — the armed operational wing — takes shape alongside it, distinct from the Faithful who continue demonstrating peacefully. **Nobu**, rising through Directorate procurement, begins quietly funding cult operations as private amends for his silence at the original accident. |
| **~5 years ago** *(a design choice, not a fixed fact — see dossier-observer.md §11)* | The column goes fully industrial, funded by Nobu's now-substantial signing authority. Placed recent rather than decades back so the Watcher isn't already saturated with mass before Day 1. |
| **The last year or so** | Demonstrations grow frequent and increasingly public — the "everywhere at once" emergence the party will walk into. **Shinobu** is splashed point-blank at one of them, detained under military lockdown on the assumption she might become something, held for months, released under pressure from her father, and kept under surveillance. |
| **Weeks before Day 1** | **Nozomi**, slated as the presumptive first pilot, attends a targeted magic show and cannot get back in the machine afterward. |

## Gaps in the Record

Not open design questions — genuine unknowns about the past itself, as established so far:

- **Aoi's history before the Pivot.** Exists in every account so far only as a death and a name.
- **The power generator, and the shadow realm's attention.** A mitflit under the exploiting lord built something that drew notice from the shadow realm, in the same period as the crater incident. What became of it, or whether it connects to anything since, has never been established.
