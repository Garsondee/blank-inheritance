## 1. Summary

Tens of thousands of years ago a mitflit **generation ship** came to Golarion and crashed on Kortos. About a thousand
years later a **search ship** came looking for it, and crashed too, on the far side of the world. Both were buried by
eruptions and earthquakes. **The mitflits are descended from the generation ship's crew.**

About two hundred years ago, four adventurers (Anabala, Hyuck, Shucks Mordant and Rupiah) found the generation ship
in a hidden jungle valley, took it at first for an overgrown temple, and, **on its cargo deck**, while fetching exotic
fuel for the mitflits who lived there, **dropped a container of it**. The fuel opened a **subspace rupture**: **First
Impact**. It tore the ship's **navigation and sensor system** into the swarm now called **the Watcher**, **opened the
ship's inner layers to the sky**, carved its way across Kortos into Absalom, and was stopped at Pharasma's temple with
the four standing in front of it.

Rupiah asked the gods whether it would ever happen again. **Yes: in Kurose, many hundreds of years from now.** The
Directorate was born first to **guard the ruined ship** at Kortos, and later moved to Kurose to keep the promise.
Kurose is where the search ship lies, fuel and all.

## 1a. The two ships

- **The generation ship.** Sent by the mitflits' ancestors tens of thousands of years ago, a crew meant to live and
  die aboard on the way. It crashed in **a hidden, jungle-choked valley on Kortos**, and was buried and half-swallowed
  by volcanic eruptions and earthquakes. What still shows is **a black obelisk on a rocky promontory**, "like a
  foreboding finger of a long dead god". The founders took it for a temple.
  - **Inside** (the old campaign's maps): Reactor A and Reactor B, **Mitflit Quarters** 1 and 2, an elevator, the
    Bridge, the Captain's and First Officer's quarters, an **AI Reasoning Core** ("a space for briefings and
    education"), and a cargo deck. A computer at the entrance asks the kind of questions a star-faring people would
    ask a visitor: geometry, gravity, the letters of life, the shells of the elements, the golden ratio.
  - **Its navigation and sensor system** is what became the Watcher (§5).
  - **Its shuttle** is buried deep under the rock of Kortos, **away from the ship itself**. The four never reached it
    (LEDGER #32; `open-questions.json#kortos-shuttle`).
  - **Mother**, the level-15 incorporeal construct the founders met in the mitflit village beside the ship and on its
    cargo deck, was the ship's **reasoning core**: the mind the navigation and sensor system reported to. **She may
    still be alive in the museum piece** (Ingram's "perhaps", LEDGER #32): weakened, unable to control the Watcher
    directly, but a valuable witness and source of information (`dossier-observer.md` §3, §17). **Maybe** (Ingram's
    word, LEDGER #34) a small part of her is in every Gen 2 Frame, the "cerebral glue" that makes the technology
    work; that is open (`open-questions.json#mother-in-gen2`). *(Proposal, LEDGER #34, veto freely: if so, Anabala
    took the pieces from this ship's reasoning core under the access she bargained for; see the next point.)*
  - **Gen 2 Frames are built from its technology** (LEDGER #20, #34): the original ship, the First Impact crash site
    at Kortos, which the Directorate holds and keeps restricted from the rest of Golarion. Building Gen 2 for the
    Directorate was Anabala's price for access to it (§8).
- **The search ship.** About a thousand years after the generation ship fell, **a second ship came to find out what
  had happened to it**, and crashed too. It is not a piece of the first ship. **Its hull lies under what is now
  Kurose**, and it is **why the gods named Kurose** (§10a): its fuel is where the next rupture will come from.
  - ~~**Gen 2 Frames** are built from its technology (§8; `dossier-second-generation-frames.md`).~~ *(Corrected
    2026-09-24, LEDGER #34: that was Claude's inference; Ingram's own words put Gen 2's technology at the First
    Impact crash site (#20) and in "the original ship" (#34). Gen 2 comes from the generation ship, above.)*
  - **Its buried systems are still calling for the ship they came to find**, and the Watcher (that ship's navigation
    and sensor system) answered the call. That is why the swarm is over Kurose rather than Kortos, and why the
    Directorate's digging strengthens the pull (LEDGER #32 made this canon; `dossier-observer.md` §5).
- **Why ships fall on Golarion.** Ingram's working hypothesis, marked "perhaps" (LEDGER #31): something about the world
  is both **attractive to the mitflits** and **critically dangerous to exploring ships**. Perhaps it is the
  **gods**, jealous of their domain, who foresaw that mitflits landing and spreading freely would change the world in
  ways they didn't want. The mitflits were not conquerors. They saw a place they could live, without understanding
  gods, or even the idea of a territorial domain (`open-questions.json#why-ships-fall`).
- **What became of the crews.** The generation ship's crew and their descendants scattered, forgot, and degraded into
  the goblinoid **wild mitflits**, the only mitflits on Golarion. The **High mitflits** of the home world (elegant,
  unblemished, aloof, blue-skinned high elves) are what they looked like before (`bible/species/mitflits.md`). They
  kept one trait that has cost them dearly: **they are extremely helpful when given the right tasks, and fascinated
  by the production of technology** (LEDGER #32). **Anabala** is one of the very earliest, kept alive through the ages
  by the knowledge demon Abraxas (`bible/characters/anabala.md`, GM-only).

## 2. Before: Gauntlight, the mitflits, and Baldwin Manor

The four's adventure (the old campaign, *The Trouble with Mitflits*) was never about a portal. It was about
mitflits: small blue folk everybody else treated as vermin, and savants that people wanted to own.

- **Gauntlight.** The four made friends with a band of mitflits, gave them a home in Gauntlight Keep, and helped them
  build a government (Anabala in charge, "what could go wrong?"). Rupiah used diplomacy where others used violence,
  and it started the mitflits down the road towards home.
- **The hunters and William Charles Baldwin.** A band of hunters, working for **William Charles Baldwin** ("WCB"), was
  capturing mitflit savants for their minds. The four crossed them repeatedly.
- **Baldwin Manor** (formerly Croglang Castle, at Volkheed on Kortos) was the Baldwin family's seat and one of Kortos's
  largest **magical-weapons manufactories**, exporting to Absalom, with **mitflits forced to make weapons** there. An
  **Inicrium battery** (Inicrium is "solid magic potential", a magical battery material; a mitflit savant child built
  the device out of curiosity, LEDGER #6/#29) **pulled the Shadow Plane into the world** and brought chaos to the town
  below. The four rescued the mitflits. **A shadow siege** followed, and **members of Baldwin's own family were
  killed by accident, not by the four.** They deactivated the battery and the shadows withdrew. **William Charles
  Baldwin blamed the four anyway.**

**Baldwin Manor is not First Impact.** They are two events many miles apart, and months apart in play (Ingram, LEDGER
#31). The old campaign's clock puts the whole run from Volkheed to Nirvana at only about a week of in-world time
(Sarenith, 4721 AR). That is a source detail and can bend.

**GM-only: William Charles Baldwin is still alive** (LEDGER #32), rich enough to have bought his way through two
centuries. He is now a criminal lord who arms gangs with incredible weapons made by essentially enslaved mitflits,
and the four founders' natural enemy. **A secret kept until the right moment** (`bible/characters/william-charles-baldwin.md`).

## 3. The Incident: First Impact

Many miles from Baldwin Manor, in a hidden jungle valley on Kortos, the four climbed to the black obelisk, answered
the computer's questions, and found that the "temple" was **a ruined spaceship**, with **mitflits living beside it**
in a village of their own.

**They caused First Impact while trying to get the exotic fuel for those mitflits. The fuel was for the ship's
shuttle** (LEDGER #32), buried deep under the rock of Kortos away from the ship. The four never got to it.

**It happened on the ship's cargo deck** (canon, LEDGER #32). The throw worked: a clean, successful Athletics check got
the container clear. The catch did not: two attempts, two natural ones. **The container split open.** An "exhaling
portal" opened, space curved, and a planar breach flung the four across the planes (§3b). Ingram: *"It was an accident
but I made it ripple outwards to become the adventure."*

A violet aperture with a black core had opened in the heart of the ship.

## 3a. The rift's path

Ingram, 2026-09-24 (LEDGER #25), authoritative:

- **What fed it.** The spilled exotic matter is fuel for technology at a godlike level. The aperture (violet-black, a
  subspace rupture) **fed on the matter near it and grew.**
- **How it grew.** Slowly at first. Those first on the scene worked out that **the rate of expansion was itself slowly
  increasing.** It grew in fits and starts, which made it dangerous to study.
- **What it cost.** It **absorbed many powerful clerics, wizards and sorcerers** who fought it.
- **Its path.** It **carved a jagged path through the landscape**, changing direction seemingly at random and consuming
  matter in its wake. Kortos's neighbouring states **sent many of their finest magic-users.**
- **Where it stopped.** It was stopped **having already partly entered Absalom, after it cut through a temple to
  Pharasma**, with the four standing in front of it at the last.

## 3b. Nirvana, and the return

The old campaign ended with the four **on another plane, Nirvana**, received by **Sarenrae** in the city of Luminara
(LEDGER #30). A mitflit council leader's message from the cargo deck had warned them: *"Time behaves differently
there; you'll emerge just in time to make a difference."*

**Ingram's working account ("presumably", LEDGER #30):** when the four came back to Golarion the First Impact rupture
was much larger, and they swung in to stop it expanding further. **That is why they arrive late to their own
disaster**: they were on another plane while it grew.

**Left open by the old campaign and waiting for closure** (Blank Inheritance exists partly to give it, LEDGER #30):
- the mitflits besieged at Gauntlight by William Charles Baldwin's army (`open-questions.json#gauntlight-siege-outcome`)
- the shuttle under Kortos, and what it was for (`open-questions.json#kortos-shuttle`)
- "the ancient alien god" prepared beyond Nirvana and never reached
- Esther Williams's lost memories, Regina Key, Dave's treachery, Rupiah's nemesis, and Hyuck's father.

## 4. The uncertain ending

What the people who were there knew: the aperture was eventually forced back and crushed into a small space, and
either destroyed or converted into something else. Nobody present ever confirmed which. **This ambiguity is deliberate
and original**, not a gap to be settled by fiat.

## 5. What's actually true: the Watcher is made

**The Watcher did not come through the rupture. The rupture made it.**

As the aperture expanded, it **ground the generation ship's navigation and sensor system into nanoparticles** and
tore it away from the ship and from Mother. The result is a swarm in which every fragment keeps a shred of function
and none keeps the organising layer, meaning the mind it reported to.

**It is not an invader. It is the ship's own eyes, smashed, and still working.** For its whole story since (from
drowning instrument, to Manako's echo, to the campaign's adversary), see `dossier-observer.md`.

## 6. The opened ship, and the birth of the Directorate

*(Rewritten 2026-09-24, LEDGER #32. This replaces the older "bisected specimen": Ingram, "It's not a bisected mech that
get's revealed, it's the inner layers of the spaceship.")*

**The rupture opened up the inner layers of the generation ship**, and laid a godlike technology open to the sky for
the first time in tens of thousands of years.

- **Scientists and magic-users** descended on the site, terrified that another rupture could happen.
- **Adventurers** descended on it looking for valuables. *(GM-only proposal, LEDGER #32: William Charles Baldwin, rich,
  on the island, with hunters and a weapons works, was among the first to loot it, and that is part of how he is
  still alive.)*
- **It became a very dangerous place.** So **the organisations around Kortos banded together**, and **the Directorate
  was born**: to keep the site secure, to prevent a future rupture, and to stop the technology uncovered there
  disrupting the world.
- **The technology leaked outwards anyway.** That leak is why a world of sailing ships and trams also has phones and
  giant machines: the era's impossible devices are ship technology that escaped the fence. *(The older idea entry called
  this "Gap-derived". The word is retired: the Gap is an event after this adventure, not the dig and not Second
  Impact (LEDGER #33; `open-questions.json#the-gap-name`).)*

More exotic matter was found in the ship, and so was **Inert 12** (§9).

## 7. Two ships, two places

The generation ship's ruin (the Directorate's first site) is at **Kortos, near Absalom.** The **search ship** is
under **Kurose**, on the other side of the world. They are **two different ships**, a thousand years apart.

The Directorate went to Kurose about a hundred and fifty years ago **for the prophecy**, when it was a fishing town,
and found the search ship's hull by digging. What guarding and studying the Kortos site had taught them told them
what they were standing on. **Kortos and Kurose are connected by the prophecy and by the same makers, not by the same
wreck.**

## 8. What the ship became: the Frames

*(Rewritten 2026-09-24, LEDGER #32. Ingram: "Gen 1 mechs are Golarion technology only… Gen 2 mechs are Anabala trying
to use actual Mitflit tech to build mechs, which is something we shouldn't confuse between the two.")*

- **Gen 1 Frames are Golarion technology only**, informed by looking at the godlike ship technology uncovered at
  Kortos. They are **not biomechanical: they are mechanical clankers, hard to work with.** Riveted plate, boiler
  technology, catwalks, and a radar dish standing in for a sensory organ nobody could comprehend. **There is no living
  core in a Gen 1 Frame and no seat that shapes itself**, and nothing in one ever takes control from its pilot.
- **Gen 2 Frames are Anabala's**: mechs built from **actual mitflit technology**. **Gen 2 is built from the
  generation ship's technology: the original ship, the First Impact crash site at Kortos, which the Directorate
  holds and keeps restricted from the rest of Golarion** (LEDGER #20, #34). *(Corrected 2026-09-24, LEDGER #34:
  this said the search ship under Kurose. That was Claude's inference; Ingram's own words put Gen 2's technology at
  the First Impact crash site (#20) and in "the original ship" (#34).)* They are built on the real thing, so being
  in one is **more like being inside a giant humanoid**. **Everything written about a living core making a seat
  shaped to its pilot's true form belongs to Gen 2 only** (LEDGER #5, narrowed by #32). **The Gen 2 core is
  biomechanical mitflit technology: robotic parts that are living flesh melded with technology. It is not a living
  mitflit** (LEDGER #34, answering the Casebook's D13, which this line used to call still open;
  `bible/species/mitflits.md#arc-mitflit-frame-core-link`). **Maybe** (Ingram's word) a small part of Mother is in
  every Gen 2, the "cerebral glue" that makes the technology work; that is open (`open-questions.json#mother-in-gen2`,
  §1a). The end result is that **these mechs aren't usable by just anyone.** Frame Sickness belongs to Gen 2 too.
- **Anabala's bargain** (LEDGER #34, N25). Anabala works with the Directorate, and has her own ultimate objective:
  using mitflit technology to get a way home for her people. Building Gen 2 for the Directorate was her price for
  access to the original ship's technology, which the Directorate keeps highly restricted from the rest of Golarion
  so it can't destabilise the world. This is how her machines became the Directorate's programme. *(Proposal,
  LEDGER #34, veto freely: her way home is perhaps the search ship under Kurose, which lifts at Chapter 15 in the
  twenty-chapter campaign draft, or perhaps the shuttle under Kortos (`open-questions.json#kortos-shuttle`). Not decided.)*
- **Mitflits and Gen 2** (LEDGER #34). Wild mitflits are uncomfortable around Gen 2 Frames, and mitflits don't
  instantly understand where Gen 2 comes from. *(Proposal, same entry, veto freely: they feel something of their
  ancestors' ship in a machine made of living flesh, and can't say what.)*

**Never confuse the two.** Full treatment: `dossier-second-generation-frames.md`.

## 9. Two substances that must not be confused

- **The exotic matter is fuel**: the ships' own fuel, "fuel for technology at a godlike level". A breached container
  opens a **subspace rupture**. That is First Impact at Kortos, and it will be **Second Impact** at Kurose.
- **Sap is not fuel.** It is **nanotech feedstock**, concentrated raw building material for nanobots, which gives the
  Watcher's children a body. **It was the generation ship's own stock for its repair nanites** (canon, LEDGER #32).
  The Directorate catalogued it about 120 years ago at the Kortos site as **Inert 12** (noble, non-reactive, useless)
  and shelved it, until a junior chemist named Manako was handed it about 45 years ago. Full treatment:
  `dossier-observer.md` §11 and `dossier-cicada.md` §3.

## 10. The founding consequence

The Directorate (特別対策本部, Tokubetsu Taisaku Honbu) was born about 195 years ago **from a coalition of Kortos's
organisations guarding the ruined ship** (§6), with the four at its heart. It had two jobs: keep the promise in
§10a, and make sure the response would be faster next time.

**UNDER REVIEW (LEDGER #36, 2026-09-25).** Ingram on the answer below: it "belongs to much older generated ideas and
notes that don't really feel sane any more". Don't build on it until `open-questions.json#founding-charter-rethink` is
settled.

Confronted with what they had nearly unleashed, and frightened by early hands-on work with the recovered technology,
the four wrote a private diagnosis of themselves (***children with guns***) and buried it. It is the blacked-out
paragraph of the charter that hangs in the lobby. **Two centuries later the institution puts real children into real
machines.** The founders diagnosed themselves accurately on day one, and spent two hundred years proving it.

Ingram, 2026-09-24 (LEDGER #25), verbatim in substance:

## 13. This world's relationship to Golarion

*(Added 2026-09-10, asked directly.)* This setting **started as Golarion** and remains loosely based on it, but is
**not a slave to it**: mitflits, Kortos and Abadar have all been deliberately changed from their Golarion baseline,
and more can change as the campaign needs. Treat real Golarion canon (and unedited stock SF2E flavour text, such as
the ancestry descriptions extracted into `data/campaign/ancestry-catalogue.json`) as a loose, atmospheric backdrop
this world grew out of, never as binding fact over something this campaign has decided.
