# Blank Inheritance — Dossier: FIRST IMPACT

*The exotic matter, the portal, the spaceship, and everything downstream of them.*

*GM reference. Everything discussed, consolidated into a single coherent account. Where the story has
more than one layer — what people at the time believed versus what's actually true — both are given,
clearly separated.*

---

## 1. Summary

Two hundred years ago, a botched rescue attempt cracked open a container and tore a hole in the world.
What everyone present understood at the time was a portal, contained and eventually crushed. What
actually happened, understood only much later, is that the portal's violence pulverised part of an
already-buried starship into the thing that is now called the Watcher. The crater it left behind, and
the machine it sliced clean in half on the way down, are why this entire campaign's mechs,
institutions, and monsters exist at all.

---

## 2. Before: The Lord and the Mitflits

The old party's adventure was not originally about a portal. It was about a local human lord who had
captured a large clan of mitflits and was exploiting their savant-level intelligence to design weapons
of war — growing very rich on it in the process.

Two separate incidents came out of that exploitation — same four people, same overall intervention,
but two different encounters with two different endings:

- **The generator siege, resolved (`bible/LEDGER.md` #6, added 2026-09-10; corrects earlier drafts of
  this file that called it an unresolved thread).** A mitflit savant *child* — curiosity, not
  direction from the lord — built a power generator. Switching it on let shadow-plane creatures
  overrun the town below the castle; the castle then came under siege as Anabala's four approached.
  They fought up through the exploiting lord's own manor house as a "fog wall of shadow" closed in,
  worked out that destroying the generator was the fix, did it, and the incursion retreated. **Wholly
  separate from the exotic-matter incident below** — its own encounter, its own ending, nothing left
  dangling.
- **The catastrophic release of exotic matter** — the incident below, and the one that actually
  matters for everything downstream of this document.

**Anabala**, moved by what was being done to her own people, recruited **Hyuck**, **Shucks Mordant**,
and **Rupiah** to intervene.

---

## 3. The Incident

In the course of that intervention, the party attempted to retrieve an item from a crashed spaceship.

The throw worked. A clean, successful Athletics check got the item clear.

The catch did not. Two attempts to catch the container of exotic matter, two natural ones. The
container split open.

A violet-hued aperture with a black core began to open and grow, **on the island where Absalom
stands.**

As it grew large enough to be noticed, adventurers and magic-users converged from across the island,
and then the region, to contain it.

---

## 4. The Uncertain Ending

What the people who were there actually knew: the aperture was eventually forced back and crushed into
a small space, and either destroyed or converted into something else. Nobody present ever fully
confirmed which.

**This ambiguity is deliberate and original, not a gap to be resolved by fiat** — it was framed from
the start as something the characters themselves never got a clean answer to.

---

## 5. What's Actually True

**The Watcher did not come through the portal. The portal made it.**

The aperture's expansion, in passing, pulverised part of the buried ship's sensor system into a
semi-sentient cloud of nanomachines. That cloud is what survives today as the Watcher, or — in the
mitflits' own, more accurate word — the Observer.

**It is not an invader from elsewhere. It is wreckage**, created on the spot, by an accident two
teenagers and a barbarian caused trying to save a captive people.

*(Full treatment of what it became: dossier-observer.md.)*

---

## 6. The Crater's Aftermath

A crater was left. And in it, a specimen was found buried far deeper than should have been possible —
deeper than dwarven caverns.

The aperture had bisected it on the way down, slicing clean through and exposing its internal
workings — an accidental, perfect cross-section, the most legible possible gift. This is what let the
era's magic-users begin reverse-engineering the technology inside it.

More of the same exotic matter was subsequently found and studied nearby. Sensible, given how
dangerous the first release had been — if it ever happened again, the response needed to be faster and
more decisive.

---

## 7. The Long Trail — Two Locations, Not One

The crater and the specimen sit at **Kortos, near Absalom.**

The rest of the ship — the actual hull — is somewhere else entirely. Following clues from the crater
dig over generations (roughly the century and a half after the incident), the Directorate located the
true mother lode under a city forming on the opposite side of the world, in the Minkai-equivalent
east, and relocated there. **That city is where the campaign takes place.** The crater and the
campaign's city are connected only by that long, multi-generational trail of discovery — not by
proximity.

---

## 8. What the Robot Became — the Frames

Reverse-engineering the one bisected cross-section, over roughly a century and a half, produced the
Frames.

**They are not salvaged pieces of the original machine. They are cargo-cult replicas**, built in the
only technological vocabulary available to the people doing the copying: riveted plate, boiler
technology, a radar dish standing in for a sensory organ nobody could actually comprehend. The original
specimen could have looked like anything — blue, organic, wrong — nothing like the children built in
its image.

**Each Frame houses a biomechanical core, mitflit-scale and alive, inside a human-built terrestrial
steel exoskeleton.** The Directorate has tried many shell designs around these cores. Some work. Some
fail catastrophically. Every single one, eventually, **develops the same hole in the same position —
officially logged as metal fatigue. It is not fatigue. The core is making a seat.**

**Confirmed 2026-09-10 (`bible/LEDGER.md` #5): this IS the mechanism behind "the cavity fits a
disguised alien pilot's true shape."** One fact, not two — the living core is shaping itself for
whoever is actually piloting, alien or not. Every Frame is quietly adapting to its pilot from the
inside, the entire time, whether anyone involved knows that's what's happening.

*(Full mechanical treatment: story bible v4, §3.)*

---

## 9. What the Exotic Matter Became — Inert 12, or Sap

The substance recovered from the crater was catalogued roughly 120 years ago, judged noble,
non-reactive, and useless, and shelved for a century before being handed — as the least interesting
assignment available — to a junior chemist named Manako, roughly 45 years ago.

Its true property, worked out much later than anyone in this history knew it: **it is food.** It feeds
the shredded nanomachine cloud, causing it to multiply. Visibility and brief solidity are not a
separate effect — they are what density looks like. **Every use of it permanently grows the Watcher.**

*(Full treatment: dossier-cicada.md §3, and story bible v4 §8.)*

---

## 10. The Founding Consequence

Roughly five years after the incident, the four who intervened founded the Directorate — explicitly
**so that the response would be faster and more decisive next time.**

In doing so, confronted with what they had nearly unleashed, they wrote a private self-diagnosis about
themselves — ***children with guns*** — and buried it out of fear of what they had become.

**Two centuries later, the Directorate's actual institutional practice is putting real children into
real guns. The founders diagnosed themselves accurately, on day one, and then spent two hundred years
proving themselves right.**

## 12. Loose Threads

Not gaps to be filled unilaterally — genuine open questions, as established:

- ~~The power generator and the shadow realm.~~ **Resolved 2026-09-10** — see §2 and
  `bible/species/mitflits.md`. A separate, closed incident; not connected to anything downstream of
  the Kortos accident.
- **What actually happened to the portal itself**, beyond "crushed into a small space and either
  destroyed or converted into something else." This was left uncertain by design and should probably
  stay that way rather than be resolved for its own sake.
- **Whether anything else survived the crushing** besides the fragment that became the Watcher — the
  museum piece (a coherent, unpulverised remnant, story bible v4 §13) suggests at least one other piece
  is out there, but its full extent has never been established.
- **Why the four founders are all still active roughly 200 years later.** Asked directly 2026-09-10
  (`bible/LEDGER.md` #8) alongside Rupiah's ancestry — **only the ancestry half was answered.** Rupiah
  is confirmed an elf, elf nobility specifically. Shucks Mordant has an obvious mechanism (literal
  undeath, `bible/characters/shucks-mordant.md`). Whether the Kortos incident itself is *why* all four
  have survived this long, versus it just being an unexamined fact of who they naturally are, is
  **still genuinely open** — don't invent an answer either way.

## 13. This World's Relationship to Golarion

*(Added 2026-09-10, asked directly rather than assumed.)* This setting **started as Golarion** and
remains loosely based on it, but is **not a slave to it** — mitflits, Kortos, and Abadar have all
already been deliberately changed from their real Golarion baseline, and more can change freely as the
campaign needs. Treat real Pact Worlds/Golarion canon (and unedited stock SF2E flavor text, such as
ancestry descriptions extracted verbatim into `data/campaign/ancestry-catalogue.json`) as a loose,
atmospheric backdrop this world grew out of — never as binding fact that overrides something this
campaign has actually decided.
