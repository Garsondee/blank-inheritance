# The idea ledger

*Moved here on 2026-10-01 from `stage.md` §0.1, when all of Stagecraft's documents were gathered into this folder.
Every idea Ingram has had about Stagecraft, every idea the plates showed, and every idea Claude added is listed once,
with the document and section that specifies it and the plates that draw it. **Nothing is dropped; this table is how
it stays that way.** When an idea is added, changed or cut, this table changes in the same edit, and
`test/stagecraft-docs.test.mjs` fails if a section or plate named here does not exist.*

*How to read a row. **Where** names the document and section (`stage.md` is what the stage does, `diorama.md` how it
looks, `renderer.md` how it is drawn, `feel.md` what must never be lost, `module.md` how it is packaged). **Plates** are
the lab's mockups: 01 to 12 the stage on a flat picture, 13 to 30 the diorama, 31 to 37 the older systems redrawn in the
diorama, 38 the three.js spike. **Diorama** is the subsection of `diorama.md` §11 that says how the idea works on the
3D floor.*

## M. Her ninth message: chalk, painted (2026-10-03)

*"Rethink how we do chalk. Ideally we don't use vector lines. Ideally I want it to really be a smooth art experience, with
