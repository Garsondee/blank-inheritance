# The plot timeline: the whole story in one store, on one editable timeline

*The plan Ingram approved on 2026-10-07, kept as the design (her words and what they settle: `canon/decisions/D070.md`).
It is built in phases (§8). **What is built** is listed just below and updated as each phase lands; the rest of the
document is the plan as approved. Story text in the examples is there to show a format: it is not canon, and canon
wins wherever the two differ.*

- **Phase 4, 2026-10-07:** the page, to edit, and review mode.
  - **Saving** (`tools/timeline/ops.mjs`, `POST /api/ops`): a save is a list of operations (field, text, ink, pencil,
    cut, park, note), each carrying what the page showed. It needs the page's own origin and the token the server put
    in the page, and takes the lock in `harvest/timeline/`. Each operation is checked against its file as
    `core/scene-edits.mjs#mergeRecord` checks a record (merged, already, clash, refused); one clash or refusal and
    nothing is written. What is written goes through `tools/story/edit.mjs`, which now reaches every kind of item:
    a file's head for chapters, threads and questions, a Book's section, and `cut()`. A save that would add an error to
    `story check` is rolled back whole; errors already there never block her. A copy in `data/` that only needs
    `story build` (a told fact's words) is rebuilt instead. About half a second a save, on all of canon.
  - **Her day record** (`tools/story/daybook.mjs`): one `kind: timeline` decision a day, its number claimed by creating
    the file exclusively; every edit quoted as she set it, a drag included; every item she touches cites it. `story
    check` fails on a line it can't read back and on any older wording that comes back over hers (the reversion guard,
    the scene-edits rule applied to canon), and so does `npm run deploy` (`tools/story/guard.mjs`, beside the
    scene-edits guard in `tools/module-sync/deploy.mjs`); `story restore` puts hers back.
  - **Notes for Claude**: quoted in her record, numbered N1, N2...; their state and Claude's reply in
    `harvest/timeline/notes.json`, written only by `story note`. A reply reaches an open page live.
  - **The page** (`editing.js`, `review.js`, loaded only by the live page with the server's token, never by the
    claude.ai copy; the rules in `core/timeline-edits.mjs`): Ink it, Edit (her words and the item's own fields, with
    suggestions), Cut (with wording to retire), Park, Note for Claude, Back to pencil; ink locked until unlocked; pencil
    dragged along time (the drop's words from `dragWhen`); Undo of anything in this sitting (Ctrl+Z, from what each save
    says it left behind); Claude's edits glowing as they arrive, and her unsaved typing kept when they do (saving then
    shows both versions if they clash: Keep mine, or keep what it says now). **Going through the pencil**: one item at
    a time, grouped by thread, who or what, chapter, whose words or where it came from, with its source, her words,
    what uses it and the ink beside it; Ink it, Edit then ink, Park, Cut, Skip (I, E, P, C, S); her own earlier words
    inked a group at a time.
  - **Claude's side**: `tools/story/pre-edit.mjs`, a PreToolUse hook beside the scene-edits one, stops an edit of a
    canon file she changed on the timeline since the conversation was told (once: re-read, then edit on top of hers),
    refuses hand edits of her day records and the notes file, any write to `quarantine/` and any new file under
    `quarantine/ideas/story/`. `tools/story/session-start.mjs`, a SessionStart hook, says what she did since last time, her open
    notes, any reversions, the health up to the horizon and whether the claude.ai copy is behind. (The node registry's
    summary still runs beside it until its retirement, migration 2 in Phase 6.)
  - **The sandbox** (`npm run timeline -- --sandbox`, or `plot-timeline-sandbox` in `.claude/launch.json`): the same
    page on a scratch copy of canon, to try it, or to check it live, without a single word reaching the real one or a
    day record crediting her with clicks that weren't hers. A copy whose server has gone is cleared at the next start.
    `npm run timeline -- copy` (`plot-timeline-copy`) serves the exported read-only copy exactly as claude.ai does, so
    it can be checked without a sign-in before or after publishing.
  - Tests: `test/timeline/ops.test.mjs`, `test/tier1-timeline-edits.test.mjs`, `test/story/pre-edit.test.mjs`, and
    the server and copy tests. Checked live in the sandbox with real clicks, typing and a drag: a field, undo, a drag,

- **The Great Merging, begun 2026-10-07 (D080).** `tools/story/merge.mjs` (check, apply, list, undo; the pure reading of a
  record is `merges.mjs`) makes one item of pencil pieces that are one thing, so the pencil comes down to an amount she
  can go through.
  - **The rules it holds.** Pencil into pencil only (never ink, an open decision, a provisional or a cut item); the pieces
    agree on who wrote them, who in the world knows them, when the table is told and on payoff, and their `when` is the
    same or only one has one; her own words (`by: ingram`) are only ever joined exactly as she said them; the merged
    words keep every number, name and quoted phrase and nearly all the other words (the checker measures it, and warns
    when words are added or a piece shares almost nothing with the others); nothing she edited, parked or left a note on
    is merged away; no scene, check or objective names a piece; a piece's own date or payoff may not name another piece
    of the same merge. A proposal may say `crossFile`: its pieces then come from several files and the merged item
    lives in the home entity's.
  - **Nothing lost, nothing hidden.** A `kind: merge` record (Claude's, never authority for ink) keeps every original
    exactly (its section number, its file, the blank lines after it) and every line the merge rewrote (a clock step, a
    road, a date anchor, a `reveals:`), so `undo` puts each piece back where it was (checked byte for byte on a copy of
    the real canon: 202 mechanical merges applied and undone). The merged item keeps every source and `from:` key (the
    coverage proof still reads 1,132 of 1,132; a `from:` key that looks like an id is never rewritten), and an older
    record that names a merged piece still resolves through an alias: `story context`, `story where`, the check and the
    tests follow it, so D071's list of what it brought in still reads true. The pre-edit hook refuses hand edits of a
    merge record.
  - **Checked as it is made.** An apply is run through `story check` afterwards: a merge that would add an error is set
    aside and reported, the rest are made again, and if the errors can't be traced nothing changes.
  - **On the page.** A merged item's inspector shows the pieces as they were, with **Looks right** and **Undo this merge**
    beside the ordinary Edit (undo is refused once the merged item has been edited, and the pieces are listed so she can
    bring them back by hand); a **Merges** list (in the header, with the count still to check) groups them by file with
    an **All N look right** button per file; a merge record's page lists what was **left apart on purpose**, the
    **possible overlaps across files** and the **disagreements** the agents found, for her to confirm (the Casebook's
    "Still hers" repeats the disagreements). Her choices are quoted in her day record (`merge D081#M3 looks right`,
    `un-merged D081#M3`).
  - **The first two waves** (D081 and D082): 155 merges took 345 pieces down to 155 items. Pencil facts went from 874 to
    688 and all facts from 1,128 to 942; ideas from 111 to 107. Agents read the facts by theme and the ideas, each
    proposing merges that had to pass the checker; independent reviewers then read every merge's pieces against its
    words, and a fix or a drop was needed on roughly a third of them (the usual defect: a colon or a connective that
    makes one piece the reason for another, or a reordering that moves what "it" or "so" points to). D081 is the 143 that
    passed that review (140 facts, 3 ideas); D082 is 12 more, pieces from one old section, tidied and named.
  - **Why it stopped at about a fifth of the pencil.** Most of the remaining pairs differ in `known`, `told` or `by`, and
    often on purpose: a table-facing summary told early beside its GM deepening told later is a layered reveal, not a
    duplicate. Going further is a call for her: let a merge take the narrower `known` and the later `told` and flag it,
    or give a fact layers.

## 1. Principles

- **One store, many views.** `canon/` (plain text in git, the existing fact format) is the only place a story fact is
  written. The timeline page, the Casebook, Foundry's lore and Claude's context are all generated from it. The Casebook
  stays (D047 #9) as the document view, and the timeline becomes its visual and editing companion.
- **Ink and pencil, by her rule.**
  - **Ink** is Episode One and the induction as they stand. Their open questions stay open.
    - Episode One means: Scene 0 to Flood Tunnel Clearance, the night's rests and the Magic Show; their checks, fight,
      hazard and objectives; and every fact they tell or rely on, GM-only secrets included.
    - The induction means: character creation, the backgrounds and classes, and FORM 1-B.
  - **Everything else is pencil until she has gone through it.** That includes facts from her own earlier decisions,
    which carry `by: ingram` so they read as her words awaiting a second look. Claude's own ideas carry `by: claude`.
  - Pencil can be read, discussed and drawn, but **never cited by play material**.
  - Pencil reaches ink only through an explicit **Ink it** in the page, or a decision quoting her: `decide` for her
    words, `accept` for Claude's.
  - **Typing never changes a status** (scene-edits: an edit is not a review).
- **One writer per status.** Following `design/data-ownership.md`: only `tools/story/edit.mjs` changes a status, and
  only by citing a decision record in her words. Import records (`kind: import`) never count as authority, which closes
  the loophole where a hand-edited `sketch → decided` would pass today's check. The Approvals Desk stays the only
  approver: the page never writes a plan row's `State` or an `approve` line.
- **She goes through pencil at her own pace**, in the page's review mode. Nothing forces it. The health checks raise
  only what the next chapter needs; everything past the **event horizon** (the next Book) is quiet.
- **The interface is the gold standard for story** (Ingram, D079: "treat this interface as the new gold standard for story
  related stuff, make other things work with it rather than clinging to any older concepts"). When a tool, check,
  document or habit disagrees with how canon and the timeline hold the story, the other thing changes.
- **The present year is 4921 AR** (her words, D070). Dates are stored relative to Day 1, and the page can show them in
  AR.
- **Built for two places.** The pure parts live in the module's `core/`, where repo tools already import from (as
  `tools/story/lib.mjs` imports `core/day-ring.mjs`), so a Foundry window can reuse them later. Those parts are the
  model, the axis, the checks and the edit rules. The window's edits would come home as harvest records, like
  scene edits.
- **A decision record first** (D070 is the next free number today; other sessions also add records, so the numbers
  here are placeholders). Before anything moves, it quotes her request, today's answers and her notes on the draft. It
  amends D047 #9, §6.3, and the README rule ("decided or plainly marked as waiting": pencil is the marking).

## 2. The filing rules

One home for every kind of story material. These open `canon/README.md`, and `CLAUDE.md` points there instead of
retelling anything.

| What it is | Its one home | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Her words: answers, rulings, lore she states, her edits and notes on the page | `canon/decisions/`, verbatim, the same turn | the authority |
| A whole document she gives | `canon/sources/` | the authority |
| A fact about the world: people, places, groups, things, events, history | `canon/world/<entity>.md` | ink or pencil |
| What happens when: Books, chapters, episodes, beats, the order of scenes | `canon/plan/` | ink or pencil |
| Play material: scenes, checks, fights, hazards, objectives | `canon/scenes/`, `challenges/`, `encounters/`, `subsystems/`, `objectives/` | ink once approved (Episode One is ink now) |
| A thread or front | `canon/threads/` | ink or pencil |
| A question the table pursues, with its clues | `canon/questions/` | ink or pencil |
| A decision she still has to make | an `open` fact in the entity or chapter it belongs to (never a separate list) | open |
| An idea, a brainstorm, a "what if", alternatives | `canon/ideas/<topic>.md`, each idea live, parked, adopted or rejected | always pencil; adopting one turns it into facts or beats |
| A lore discussion | no transcript: what she said becomes a decision, and what Claude proposed becomes pencil facts or ideas, listed in that decision | follows its parts |
| Craft, research, the rules mirror | `design/`, `design/research/`, `reference/` | never holds a story fact; may cite canon ids |
| The Casebook, the timeline copy, told facts, NPC and location story text, Foundry lore | generated, never edited by hand | a copy |
| The old stores | `quarantine/`, read-only | searchable for ideas, never cited |

**The rules** (how each is enforced is in brackets):
1. **One home.** A story statement lives in exactly one place, and everywhere else cites its id. [citations resolve;
   the import's dedupe; NPC and location story text become generated]
2. **Nowhere else.** No story fact goes in `CLAUDE.md`, memory, design documents, `ideas/entries/` (features only from
   now on), code comments, or chat alone. A memory may point at canon, never state a story fact. [the retired-wording
   and verbatim-fact scan covers `CLAUDE.md` and memory; the PreToolUse hook refuses new files under `quarantine/ideas/story/` and
   any write to `quarantine/`]
3. **The same turn.** When she states lore, the decision record and everything it creates are written before the turn
   ends, and then `story check` runs. [the existing rule; the `/lore` routine]
4. **Every item carries** an id, a status and a source, and pencil carries its author (`by:`). [`story check`]
5. **Changes go through the tools** (`story edit` or the page). A status rises only through a decision in her words.
   [the write-path test]
6. **Rejected stays rejected.** A cut fact or a rejected idea keeps its wording under `retire:`, and nothing may bring
   it back: not canon, play data, `CLAUDE.md` or memory. [`story check`]
7. **Brainstorms and discussions end filed.** A brainstorm ends with its ideas in `canon/ideas/`, each with a state,
   and one line saying where everything went. A lore discussion ends with its decision record. [the `/lore` command
   runs this routine]

## 3. The store: formats

Every format is checked against `tools/story/lib.mjs`, so the parsers change little.

**`when`** (`tools/story/when.mjs`: `parseWhen`, `formatWhen`, `resolveWhen`). Plain words, editable by hand, and
today's only value parses unchanged. Day 1 of intake counts as 0.
```
when: about 200 years ago                              approximate point
when: 44 to 40 years ago                               span
when: about 1000 years after generation-ship.crashed   anchored: moves with its anchor
when: day 1, late morning                              Episode One (the plan's TIMES words)
when: chapter 6, late        when: book iv             the future, by play order
when: about 15 years ago | about 1 year ago            an open fact: one ghost per candidate
when: 4721 AR                                          a calendar year (the present is 4921 AR)
```

**Sketches** sit below a `## Pencil` line in each world file, so ink always reads first. `parseWorld` treats the line
as notes.
```
## Pencil
## manako.lens-years
In the four years after the spill, a fragment of it helps him work out how to make Sap himself.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D071 · from: v4-10 · known: nobody · told: later · when: 44 to 40 years ago
```
- `by:` is required on a sketch.
- `from:` is a short key that the import record resolves to a path at a commit, so canon never names an old path.
- A planted fact carries `payoff: <fact id>`. Its own `told` is when it's planted, and the payoff fact's `told` is
  when it pays off. "Hinted" is worked out from those, not stored.

**Threads** (`canon/threads/<id>.md`). A fact joins a lane through its entity, or through `threads:` on the fact. A
scene's threads come from the facts it cites, so scene JSON is untouched and its approval hash doesn't change.
```
# Manako and Cicada
kind: front
lane: people · order: 2 · entities: manako, cicada, envoy, hori, aoi
impulse: To be believed: make the city look up and see what is in its sky.
doom: The city sees it, and the thing in it learns what being seen is worth.
intensity: 1 1 2 2 | 3 4 3 4 | 5 4 5 5 | 4 5 5 | . . . . .
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D071

## Clock · Cicada goes public
- cicada.violet-marks
- beat--police-clash
- beat--kiris-show
```
- `intensity` is the rise and fall: one slot per chapter, grouped by Book, 0–5, with `.` meaning not sketched.
- A clock segment fills when its item is told or played.

**Chapters 2–20** (`canon/plan/chapter-NN.md`). `parsePlan` reads only table rows and `##` headings, so `###` beats
don't affect it. Beat ids carry no chapter, so moving a beat is a cut and paste.
```
# Chapter 6 · The Show
book: II · rank: Duty Section, Clearance 2 · status: sketch · by: claude · source: D071
Kiri's show: a whole hall looks up through treated lenses.

## Beats
### beat--the-sky-full-of-eyes
Through the lenses the audience sees the sky over Kurose black with eyes on stalks. Cicada calls it a god.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D071 · kind: reveal · pace: down · threads: watcher
```
- `kind` is one of: event, reveal, choice, flashback, end. `pace` is up, down or lateral, the sawtooth.
- `told: in:beat--…` and `told: in:chapter-06` already pass today's pattern.
- Play order is the file, then the line, so the early-reveal check works across chapters.
- `canon/plan/books.md` holds the five Books.

**Questions** (`canon/questions/<id>.md`): what the table wonders. This is one home for:
- the episode files' "dangling threads";
- the Book questions;
- the Casebook's roads and plants;
- the Mystery Ledger.
```
# Who wrote Cicada?
answer: manako.cicada
misdirect: cicada.leaderless
raised: in:episode-c1e1--the-errand
answered: in:chapter-12
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D071

## Roads
- core · physical · manako.part-number-hand
- testimony · manako.disgrace-records
- pattern · manako.revenge-list
```
- The roads use the six clue kinds of `design/mystery-and-investigation-design.md` §3. The `core` road is the one that
  must reach the table and is never behind a roll.
- `misdirect` is a belief in the existing format, whose `truth` is the answer.
- Errors: an unknown kind, or a missing fact.
- Warnings, only for chapters inside the horizon: fewer than three roads; one kind only; a pattern clue as the only
  road; no core road; a road told after the answer.

**Ideas** (`canon/ideas/<topic>.md`): brainstorms and "what ifs", kept apart from the world so alternatives never pass
as facts. Their ids carry no dot, like beats, so they never collide with fact ids.
```
# Ideas: the mayor
## idea--the-donors-raid
A raid threatens one of the mayor's donors, and the mayor quietly asks the party to look the other way.
status: sketch · by: claude · source: D068 · state: live · threads: city · when: book ii
```
- `state` is one of:
  - `live`;
  - `parked`;
  - `adopted`, with `became:` naming the facts or beats it turned into;
  - `rejected`, with `retire:` naming the wording that must not come back.
- On the page, ideas show as faint chips on their lane, behind a switch.

**Her day record** (written by the page as she saves; `kind: timeline`):
```

kind: timeline

> `watcher.g1-new-kind` · inked

## Changes

```
- Every item she edits gains the record in its `source:`, so the existing "decisions agree with facts" check holds.
- A drag is quoted as the value she set, so a drag-only day still has her words.
- A note's open or done state and Claude's reply live in `harvest/timeline/notes.json`, written only by `story note`.
- **Screen layout is not story.** Lane order sits in the thread headers, eras in `tools/timeline/eras.mjs`, and folded

## 5. Saving

`POST /ops` in `tools/timeline/server.mjs` copies the playtest hub's pattern rather than importing it. Each save:
1. **Checks the request:** the token, the Host and the Origin (the hub lacks the last two).
2. **Takes the canon lock** (`tools/scene-edits/store.mjs#acquireLock`).
3. **Checks `before` on each operation.** This ports the merge rule of `module/blank-inheritance/scripts/core/scene-edits.mjs#mergeRecord`
   (merged, already done, or clash) from JSON paths to Markdown sections. A clash shows both versions, and hers wins
   on a click.
4. **Writes through `tools/story/edit.mjs`,** which changes only the touched lines and keeps each file's line endings,
   using `writeFileAtomic`.
5. **Runs `checkModel`** and compares the errors with those that existed before the save. Only a new error rolls back,
   so Claude's half-finished work never blocks her.
6. **Appends to her day record.** The next D number is claimed by creating its file exclusively, so a chat session
   can't take the same number.

**What else protects her work:**
- A **reversion guard**, as scene-edits has: `findReversions`, applied to the day records, so no older wording can
  silently replace hers. It fails `story check` and `npm run deploy`.
- The PreToolUse hook (`tools/scene-edits/pre-edit.mjs`) learns `canon/`, so Claude rereads any file she has just
  changed before editing it.
- **`sceneHash` also covers the words of every fact a scene cites,** so changing a fact reopens that scene's approval.
  No scene is approved yet, so this costs nothing now.
- Auto-committing her edits to git is **off** unless she asks for it. The day records are the journal, and
  `story restore` replays them.

## 11. Risks

1. **Something becomes ink without her word.** Covered by import records that carry no authority, the explicit
   verbs, the write-path test, and the scene hash that covers cited facts.
2. **Pencil swamps ink, or Claude rereads rejected ideas as truth.** Covered by the Pencil line, context packs that
   put ink first, coarse sketches (one per beat, not per sentence), conflict reports, and `retire` lists. The volume
   is roughly 800 Casebook items plus the dossiers, against 181 facts today.
3. **Her edits get lost.** Covered by the lock, the `before` checks, exclusive D numbers, replayable day records and
   the reversion guard.
4. **The effort sinks, or the page goes unused.**
   - The text tools come first and help with no page at all.
   - Reading comes before editing, and lenses arrive only when she asks.
   - Canon stays readable in any editor.
   - Her note on the Gazetteer is honoured by keeping every calculation in shared `core/` modules, so the timeline can
     move into Foundry, or live in both places, without a rewrite.
5. **Rules erode.** New sessions and other Claude conversations may file story material in old places. Mitigated by
   the hook refusals of rule 2, the scan of `CLAUDE.md` and memory, the `/lore` routine, and a session-start line
   counting anything filed outside its home.
