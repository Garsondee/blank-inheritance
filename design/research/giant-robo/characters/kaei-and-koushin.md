# Kaei and Koushin (花栄・黄信)

*Experts of Justice · the inseparable pair who refused promotion to stay in the field. Part of the [Giant Robo research shelf](../README.md). **Research, not lore — these characters do not exist in our setting, and nothing here decides anything.** Tags: **[S]** source, **[R]** reading, **[U]** suggested use.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Japanese** | 小李広の花栄 (Kaei) and 鎮三山の黄信 (Koushin) — "Kaei the Little Li Guang" and "Koushin, Guardian of Three Mountains" |
| **Role** | Joint commanders of Ryozanpaku, the IPO's fortress-headquarters. Frontline officers, not staff |
| **Borrowed from** | Hua Rong 花榮 and Huang Xin 黄信 of the *Water Margin*, epithets intact |
| **Powers** | Kaei: an archer of impossible speed, whose soul-chasing life-taking sword technique turns one arrow into a rain of them. Koushin: a swordsman who splits structures and tears through formations. Both outrun motorcycles |
| **Fate** | Survive |

## 1. What the series gives us **[S]**

- They wear Chinese-style armour and helmets in a world of business suits, and are never seen apart.
- **They were both students of Shinmei, a former member of the Big Nine, and when he died they were nominated to replace him — and refused.** They wanted to keep going to the dangerous places themselves. Taisou took the seat instead. That single piece of backstory explains everything about how they behave.
- They command from the front, unlike the Big Nine, who direct.
- Koushin is abrasive and domineering. He threatens to kill the twelve-year-old protagonist when the boy refuses an order — and then, once the boy finds his resolve, is the first to back him. Kaei is warmer, and never shows the boy contempt at all.
- **[S]** Koushin looks strikingly like an unmasked version of the enemy's silent enforcer, down to the armour. **The series never mentions it.**

## 2. How they enter and leave a scene **[S/R]**

**[S]** Together, at speed, from the direction of the fighting.

**[R]** A pair is more efficient than two individuals. The audience learns both by contrast in a single scene — the warm one and the hard one — and never has to be told which is which again.

## 3. Why they work **[R]**

- **The refused promotion is the best single line of NPC backstory in the series.** It is one sentence; it establishes their competence, their values, their relationship with a dead mentor, and their opinion of the organisation's leadership, and it explains why two people this strong are not in the room where decisions are made.
- **Good cop and bad cop, with the bad one being right.** Koushin's threat is monstrous and his read of the situation is correct; the boy does have to act. Then he supports him. **[R] That sequence — pressure, resistance, resolve, respect — is a complete relationship arc in about ninety seconds, and it is repeatable at a table.**
- **The unexplained resemblance.** Koushin looks like the enemy's enforcer and nobody says anything. It costs one design decision and generates fan speculation for thirty years.

## 4. The archetype **[U]**

**Name:** *The Inseparable Pair.* Two officers who are always together, contrast in temperament, and share a history that explains their rank.

**The one rule:** **They must disagree with each other in front of the party.** The pair exists so that the party can hear an argument between two people who both have authority. If they only ever agree, they are one NPC with two faces.

**Voices:** Koushin is clipped, commanding and rude; Kaei is dry and encouraging, and finishes his partner's sentences more politely than his partner started them.

**Audition lines** (written for this shelf, not quotations):

- *Koushin, to a child:* "You will do it, or I will put you somewhere you cannot refuse from. Choose which of those happened."
- *Kaei, immediately after:* "He means that he would rather you chose. He has never said it that way in his life."
- *Koushin, later:* "Good. That is what it looks like. Remember what it looked like."
- *On the promotion:* "They offered. We are not administrators. Somebody has to be at the front, and the two of us are better at it than we would be at meetings."

## 5. For Blank Inheritance **[U]**

**Worth stealing:** the refused promotion as one-line characterisation; the pair who argue in front of the party; the pressure-then-respect sequence with a young PC; the unexplained visual resemblance to an enemy, left uncommented.

**Worth avoiding:** using the threat beat on a real player without knowing the table. Koushin threatens to kill a child to force an action. At a table that is coercion of a player, and `design/agency-and-player-choice.md` applies. The *pressure* is usable; the death threat needs the group's tolerance established first, and a safety-tools conversation is cheaper than an apology.

**Where it might attach:** the Directorate's field operations imply officers running them from the front. A pair like this is a good shape for that slot — and, per `feedback_npc_stubs_by_default`, a two-line stub each is the right first version.
