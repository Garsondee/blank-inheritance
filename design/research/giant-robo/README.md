# Giant Robo research shelf — index and router

*Written 2026-09-13. A research sweep of **Giant Robo: The Day the Earth Stood Still** (ジャイアントロボ THE ANIMATION -地球が静止する日, Yasuhiro Imagawa, seven OVA episodes, 1992–1998), gathered to feed NPC creation for this campaign. It exists because the campaign's tone is already locked to this series (see the `feedback_tone_giant_robo_not_eva` memory and `design/campaign-arc.md`), and because we need a stocked shelf of **archetypes** — not borrowed characters — before casting a full adventure's worth of NPCs.*

***This is not a decision document, and none of it is lore.*** *Nothing here overrides `ideas/story/blank-inheritance-v4.md`, `ideas/story/bible/`, `design/campaign-arc.md`, `design/scale-and-chassis.md`, or any entry in `data/campaign/open-questions.json`. No character described here exists in our world. No name, faction or power written here is claimed for our setting until the user places it deliberately. A research document may name a gap; it may never fill one.*

---

## 1. Conventions

Every claim carries a tag, for the same reason the craft library tags its own:

- **[S] Source** — what the series, its production record, or the reference works actually contain. Traceable to the sources in §5.
- **[R] Reading** — an interpretation of why something works. Argued, not verified.
- **[U] Use** — a suggested application to this campaign. **Always a suggestion.** If it touches a live open question it names the question and stops.

Japanese names are given in romaji with kanji on first use. Where English releases disagree on spelling (Vogler/Fogler, Fitzgerald/Fitzkerald/Fitzcarraldo, Chujo/Chūjō), the most common English form is used and the variants noted once.

## 3. The thematic documents

- **[the-buried-atrocity.md](the-buried-atrocity.md)** — the Tragedy of Bashtarle as a reusable story engine: prosperity built on a hidden death toll, a will misread, and a truth revealed in four passes.

## 5. Sources

Gathered 2026-09-13.

- **Japanese Wikipedia, 「ジャイアントロボ THE ANIMATION -地球が静止する日の登場人物」** — the deepest single source: epithets in kanji, ages, techniques, relationships, and production notes sourced to the *30th Anniversary Book* (Hobby Japan, 2022). Most per-character detail here comes from it.
- **English Wikipedia, *Giant Robo: The Day the Earth Stood Still*** — production history, staff, themes, the wuxia and *jiang hu* framing, episode list, reception.
- **TV Tropes**: `Anime/GiantRobo`, `Characters/GiantRobo`, `Trivia/GiantRobo`, `YMMV/GiantRobo`, `Awesome/GiantRobo` — trope-level description, fan reception, the unmade arc titles.
- **Manga Wiki (Fandom), *Giant Robo (OVA)*** — a mirror of an older, heavily cited Wikipedia revision; carries the Bandai Visual liner-note citations behind the design and themes material.
- **Villains Wiki (Fandom), *The Magnificent Ten*** — per-member powers, and the organisational rules (who may keep a private army).
- **Mitsuteru Yokoyama World Wiki (Fandom), *Kenji Murasame*** — the character across all his appearances.
- **English Wikipedia, *108 Stars of Destiny*** — the real Water Margin epithets, star names and weapons behind the Experts.
- **Anime News Network, Mike Crandol, *Old School: Giant Robo* (2003)** — critical reading.

## 6. Scope, and what comes next

This sweep covers the 1992–1998 OVA only. It does not cover the 1967 manga, the 1967 tokusatsu (*Johnny Sokko and His Flying Robot*), the 2007 *GR: Giant Robo*, or the *Day the Earth Burned* manga, except where they explain something in the OVA. The user has said an **Evangelion sweep** will follow as a separate shelf; keep the two apart. They are being mined for opposite things — Giant Robo for warmth, theatricality and honourable enemies, Evangelion for whatever the user names when that sweep starts.
