# Evangelion research shelf — index and router

*Written 2026-09-13, as the second shelf under `design/research/` and the follow-up to [Giant Robo](../giant-robo/README.md). A sweep of **Neon Genesis Evangelion** (新世紀エヴァンゲリオン, Hideaki Anno, 26 TV episodes 1995–96, plus *The End of Evangelion*, 1997), gathered to fill the gaps the Giant Robo sweep could not — school life, an institution whose treatment of its children is the subject, enemies built as puzzles, relationships that degrade, and operational texture.*

***This is not a decision document, and none of it is lore.*** *Nothing here overrides `ideas/story/blank-inheritance-v4.md`, `ideas/story/bible/`, `design/campaign-arc.md`, or any entry in `data/campaign/open-questions.json`. No character described here exists in our world.*

> ## ⚠ The guardrail — read this before anything else
>
> **This campaign's tone is locked to Giant Robo and explicitly not to this series.** The standing rule (`feedback_tone_giant_robo_not_eva`) is: fun anime, warm, funny, uplifting; comedy is load-bearing; dark is allowed, **depressing is a fault**.
>
> **This shelf is therefore mined for structure and texture, never for register.** Take the school corridor, the fortress city, the sixteen puzzle-enemies, the budget meetings, the friendship that fails. Do not take the despair, the ending, or the voice. Every file here repeats that line, and [tone-guardrail.md](tone-guardrail.md) sets out exactly which parts are quarantined and why.

---

## 1. Conventions

Same tags as the Giant Robo shelf:

- **[S] Source** — what the series, its production record, or the reference works actually contain.
- **[R] Reading** — an interpretation. Argued, not verified.
- **[U] Use** — a suggested application here. **Always a suggestion**, and where it touches a live open question it names the question and stops.

Japanese names are given surname-last in the common English form (Shinji Ikari, not Ikari Shinji), because that is how every English source and the project's existing material refers to them. Where the series and the films differ, the TV series is the default and the difference is noted.

## 6. Scope

This sweep covers the **1995–96 TV series and *The End of Evangelion***. The *Rebuild* tetralogy (2007–2021) is referenced only where it changes something structurally relevant, and the manga only where it differs usefully. Where the two TV endings and the film disagree, all are reported rather than reconciled.
