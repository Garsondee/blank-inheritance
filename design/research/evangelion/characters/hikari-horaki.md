# Hikari Horaki (洞木ヒカリ)

*Class 2-A representative · ordinary authority, and the one who notices. Part of the [Evangelion research shelf](../README.md). **Research, not lore — this character does not exist in our setting, and nothing here decides anything. Mined for structure, not for register** ([tone-guardrail.md](../tone-guardrail.md)). Tags: **[S]** source, **[R]** reading, **[U]** suggested use.*

| | |
|---|---|
| **Role** | Class representative of 2-A. No powers, no clearance, no part in the war |
| **Family** | Older sister Kodama, younger sister Nozomi. **The three sisters are named after Tōkaidō Shinkansen services** — Kodama, Hikari and Nozomi, slowest to fastest |
| **Naming** | Surname from Ryu Murakami's *Ai to gensō no fascism* |
| **Fate** | Survives. In the *Rebuild* films she marries Toji and has a child |

## 1. What the series gives us **[S]**

- A rigid, rule-abiding, slightly grumpy exterior over a sweet, patient, caring one. She runs the classroom and is obeyed.
- She is attentive to her sisters and her friends, and she constantly taunts the boy she is obviously in love with.
- **She is the only classmate who approaches Asuka** — the loudest, most abrasive, most difficult person in the room — and she keeps doing it after being rebuffed.
- **When Asuka collapses completely, Hikari takes her into her home**, with no agenda and no conditions.

## 2. Why she works **[R]**

- **Ordinary authority is more useful at a table than any amount of power.** She can tell a pilot to stop leaning on the desk, and the pilot stops. An NPC who can direct a PC over something trivial, and be obeyed, is worth more than three who admire them.
- **She is the repair mechanism in a series full of decay.** Of the eight relationships catalogued in [relationships-that-degrade.md](../relationships-that-degrade.md), three improve, and she is personally responsible for the most important one. **[R] The thing she does is not complicated: she keeps approaching somebody who is rude to her, and does not stop.**
- **She has no idea what is actually happening.** She is not cleared, not briefed, and not important to the plot — and she provides the only functioning care in the story. That is a deliberate and rather pointed arrangement.
- **She is funny about the right things.** Her crush is the series' longest-running gentle joke, and it is one of very few things in it that simply ends happily.

## 3. The archetype **[U]**

**Name:** *The One Who Notices.* An unremarkable peer with real social authority and no access, who sees a PC or NPC coming apart before anybody with clearance does.

**The one rule:** **She keeps approaching after being rebuffed, and the rebuff costs her something.** If she is merely nice, she is wallpaper. The archetype is about persistence against friction, and the players must see the friction.

**Voice:** brisk, a little bossy, formal with strangers and warm with friends. Scolds and organises. Changes the subject when teased. Says the direct thing when it matters and then immediately gets embarrassed about having said it.

**Audition lines** (written for this shelf, not quotations):

- *Authority:* "You're on cleaning duty. I don't care what you did last night, and I don't want to know. The bucket's by the door."
- *Persistence:* "You said that last time, too. I've brought two anyway, so one of them's going to waste unless you take it."
- *The moment:* "You can stay at ours. No — I've already told them. It's done. You can be horrible to me about it tomorrow."
- *Deflecting:* "I do *not*. Honestly. He's an idiot and I'm only being polite. — Stop looking at me like that."

## 4. For Blank Inheritance **[U]**

**Worth taking:** the peer with ordinary authority; the person outside the secret who provides the only real care; persistence against rebuff as a visible, costly act; a light running joke that is allowed to end well.

**Worth avoiding:** nothing much. **This is the single most tone-compatible character on the shelf** — she would sit comfortably in the Giant Robo register without a word changed, which is worth noting given how little else here would.

**Where it might attach:** a class representative is a role our school setting implies and does not yet name (`quarantine/ideas/story/bible/locations/main-school-complex.md`, `ideas/entries/school-schedule-system.json`).
