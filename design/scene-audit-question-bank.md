# Scene Audit — Question Bank

*Compiled 2026-09-18. The full raw extraction behind [scene-audit.md](scene-audit.md)'s curated
checklist — six parallel research passes, one per craft-library shelf-section plus the two research
shelves, each instructed to read the full text of its assigned documents and pull out every
load-bearing, concretely checkable diagnostic it could justify, not just the ones that made the
final cut.*

**This is a reservoir, not the working checklist.** `scene-audit.md` distills roughly a third of
what's below into something actually usable at signoff time — comprehensive would defeat the point
of a checklist anyone runs every time. Keep this file around for when a category needs deepening
later: a real mystery-heavy session wants the full mystery-and-investigation-design extraction below,
not just the four questions that made the curated cut; a session built around a returning villain
wants the full recurring-antagonist-engine list, not just the highlights.

Organized by source document, in the extracting agent's own words (QUESTION/WHY/SCOPE/CATEGORY),
lightly reformatted for consistency. Provenance tags ([E]/[T]/[C]/[Q] for the craft library,
[S]/[R]/[U] for the research shelves) are carried over from each source document as the extracting
agent found them.

## Narrative and interactive-story theory

### narrative-theory-foundations.md

1. **Q:** Does this scene route through an existing established tie/relationship rather than introducing a fresh, unconnected thread? **WHY:** the causal network model [E] — "importance is a property of wiring, not prose quality." **SCOPE:** BOTH — **CATEGORY:** Causality & Wiring
2. **Q:** If a scene isn't landing, which of the five discourse knobs (order, duration, frequency, mood, voice) is actually wrong? **WHY:** Genette's five knobs [T] — the only levers discourse controls over a fixed fabula. **SCOPE:** BOTH — **CATEGORY:** Discourse Diagnostics
3. **Q:** Does the scene change location, introduce an NPC, cause an event, or have a character act — without a stated transition, relationship, cause, or want, respectively? **WHY:** the five-dimension event-indexing model [E] — "confusion at the table is almost always one of these four." **SCOPE:** SCENE — **CATEGORY:** Comprehension & Indexing
4. **Q:** Is any thread relying on surprise as its primary engine for longer than a single payoff? **WHY:** suspense/curiosity/surprise as the only three engines [T/E] — surprise "costs a setup and pays once." **SCOPE:** SESSION/ARC — **CATEGORY:** Narrative Engines
5. **Q:** Is a looming threat/revelation shown narrowing over time rather than sprung with no lead-in? **WHY:** information-gap theory [E] — "dread is fine; ambush is not." **SCOPE:** BOTH — **CATEGORY:** Suspense & Disclosure
6. **Q:** For every new or newly-prominent NPC, can you state what they want, from/through whom, and who helps or opposes them? **WHY:** Greimas's actantial model [T]. **SCOPE:** BOTH — **CATEGORY:** Character Placement
7. **Q:** Does the scene hand players a connected conclusion, or separate facts they connect themselves? **WHY:** retrospective coherence [T] — "give a fact, not a conclusion." **SCOPE:** BOTH — **CATEGORY:** Inference & Delivery

### agency-and-player-choice.md

1. **Q:** Does any outcome get quietly ensured regardless of player choice while implying the choice mattered? **WHY:** the four families of answer to the narrative paradox [T] — silent mediation ("quantum ogre") is ruled out entirely. **SCOPE:** BOTH — **CATEGORY:** Agency Honesty
2. **Q:** If a system adapts to player behavior, is that adaptation diegetic and disclosed rather than a hidden GM thumb on the scale? **WHY:** the Mirror's correct construction — "the antagonist learning," not the GM softening. **SCOPE:** BOTH — **CATEGORY:** Agency Honesty
3. **Q:** Is it clear which internal-ontological register is live (personnel scale/Frame scale), and does it stay coherent within that register? **WHY:** Ryan's interactivity typology [T]. **SCOPE:** SCENE — **CATEGORY:** Register & Scale
4. **Q:** For each affordance/role offered, does it evoke a desire the scene's structure actually supports? **WHY:** agency-as-matching (Wardrip-Fruin et al.) [T]. **SCOPE:** SCENE — **CATEGORY:** Agency & Affordance
5. **Q:** Will the player notice each consequence, and is something later scheduled to make it matter again? **WHY:** meaningful play's discernible + integrated test [T]. **SCOPE:** BOTH — **CATEGORY:** Consequence & Follow-Through
6. **Q:** Does a new objective/side plot carry two-plus learnables rather than one? **WHY:** "An objective with one learnable is not an arc, it is an errand." **SCOPE:** SESSION/ARC — **CATEGORY:** Consequence & Follow-Through
7. **Q:** Do most choices land as cheap acknowledgement, with real branching reserved for a disclosed Bifurcation Point? **WHY:** the illusion-of-agency finding [E, duration caveat Q]. **SCOPE:** SESSION/ARC — **CATEGORY:** Branching Economics
8. **Q:** Does every new side plot touch at least one of the six threads rather than existing as a self-contained detour? **WHY:** the storylet soundness constraint. **SCOPE:** SESSION/ARC — **CATEGORY:** Thread Integration

### environmental-storytelling-and-place.md

1. **Q:** Has a narrative-architecture mode (evocative/enacted/embedded/emergent) been named for this location before writing it? **WHY:** Jenkins's four modes [T]. **SCOPE:** SCENE — **CATEGORY:** Mode & Register
2. **Q:** Does any detail exist only to explain the location, rather than being an artefact requiring inference? **WHY:** "a space that TELLS has a narrator hiding in it" [C]. **SCOPE:** SCENE — **CATEGORY:** Evidence vs Exposition
3. **Q:** If a fact must survive the campaign, is it attached to a revisitable place described the same way every time? **WHY:** the memory-palace effect [E]. **SCOPE:** BOTH — **CATEGORY:** Memory & Continuity
4. **Q:** For an institution with something to hide, has at least one absence, repair, or worn surface been written before any of its secrets? **WHY:** absence/wear/repair as embedded narrative [C]. **SCOPE:** SCENE — **CATEGORY:** Evidence vs Exposition
5. **Q:** Across the session's location list, is at least one location authored on purpose as a breather with nothing at stake? **WHY:** spatial rhythm/compression-release [C]. **SCOPE:** SESSION/ARC — **CATEGORY:** Spatial Pacing
6. **Q:** For any scene-depth-or-above location, can you answer "what do players actually do here" with concrete activities? **WHY:** reality effect vs. texture-that-displaced-consequence [T/C]. **SCOPE:** SCENE — **CATEGORY:** Affordance & Utility
7. **Q:** Does the description lead with a non-visual sense, orient with one landmark, and (on return) describe only the delta? **WHY:** the theatre-of-the-mind rule set [C]. **SCOPE:** SCENE — **CATEGORY:** On-Screen Delivery
8. **Q:** Does the described scale of a location match its actual measured footprint? **WHY:** the footprint gate. **SCOPE:** SCENE — **CATEGORY:** Grounding & Consistency

### mechanics-as-meaning.md

1. **Q:** For any rule this scene leans on, can you state in one sentence the claim about the world it enacts? **WHY:** procedural rhetoric [T]. **SCOPE:** BOTH — **CATEGORY:** Procedural Rhetoric
2. **Q:** What would a pure optimizer do repeatedly because of the driving mechanic, and is that person a recognizable protagonist of this story? **WHY:** the ludonarrative harmony/dissonance test [C/Q]. **SCOPE:** BOTH — **CATEGORY:** Reward & Theme Alignment
3. **Q:** Where a conflict can resolve multiple ways, does the reward structure actually pay differently? **WHY:** "if two outcomes pay the same, the system has said they are the same." **SCOPE:** BOTH — **CATEGORY:** Reward & Theme Alignment
4. **Q:** Is there a stated theme with no mechanical reward attached, at risk of losing by default? **WHY:** incentive design [C] — "warmth and slowness lose by default." **SCOPE:** SESSION/ARC — **CATEGORY:** Reward & Theme Alignment
5. **Q:** Does any new penalty permanently shrink a character's future menu of options, rather than raising the stakes of a recoverable state? **WHY:** "penalties cost risk, never choice." **SCOPE:** BOTH — **CATEGORY:** Penalty Design
6. **Q:** For any new tracked number, has it been decided whether its failure mode is neglect (number it) or gaming (leave it unnumbered)? **WHY:** [C]. **SCOPE:** SESSION/ARC — **CATEGORY:** Mechanic Design
7. **Q:** If the scene leans on an adaptive system, is its output actually computed from real play signals and changeable by players? **WHY:** the Mirror done correctly — "falsifiable in play." **SCOPE:** BOTH — **CATEGORY:** Procedural Rhetoric

### dialogue-and-character-voice.md

1. **Q:** Does every speaking NPC have a legible want driving the conversation, with every line serving it? **WHY:** "a line with no want underneath it is exposition in a costume" [C]. **SCOPE:** SCENE — **CATEGORY:** Want & Negotiation
2. **Q:** Where an NPC withholds something, is it a fact being evaded (subtext) rather than a feeling? **WHY:** subtext as legible Gricean-maxim violation [T]. **SCOPE:** SCENE — **CATEGORY:** Subtext & Register
3. **Q:** Are NPCs differentiated by rhythm, refusal, or retreat-under-pressure rather than a swappable tic alone? **WHY:** voice differentiation levers ranked, tics weakest [C]. **SCOPE:** BOTH — **CATEGORY:** Voice Distinctness
4. **Q:** Where the scene must deliver known information, could it be a disagreement between two informed characters instead of one explaining? **WHY:** exposition techniques ranked — disagreement is "the strongest available." **SCOPE:** SCENE — **CATEGORY:** Exposition Technique
5. **Q:** For any conversation run as a scene, can you name what will be different at the end, and what the bad-outcome version looks like? **WHY:** "a conversation is a scene" (entry/turn/exit). **SCOPE:** SCENE — **CATEGORY:** Scene Structure
6. **Q:** Is this NPC's prepared material built as a generator (want, register, samples) rather than a sequential script? **WHY:** "a script is close to useless at the table" [C]. **SCOPE:** SCENE — **CATEGORY:** Prep Format
7. **Q:** Is every GM-facing note plain and actionable, with obliqueness reserved only for the character's actual speech? **WHY:** the plain-instruction/oblique-speech field split. **SCOPE:** SCENE — **CATEGORY:** Prep Format

## TTRPG design and table craft

### ttrpg-campaign-and-session-craft.md

6. **Q:** Does every clock advance only because of a real fictional event, never simply because sessions have passed? **WHY:** [C]. **SCOPE:** BOTH — **CATEGORY:** Pressure Mechanics
